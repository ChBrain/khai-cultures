## plot_05, and why it is not a renumber

Greece's line ended at the Asia Minor Catastrophe. A century sat unstaged, and the
event that actually shaped every living Greek - the debt crisis - was nowhere. That is
not `plot_99` material: `order_plot_zero.md` says the present is "the decade still
resolving", and 2010 to 2018 is history now.

**The order had already solved this and it cost nothing.** The present is held at `99`
so that "the present stays last however many plots are added before it." So the crisis
is `plot_05`, appended after `plot_04` and before `plot_99`, **with no renumbering and
no cross-reference churn** - the first time in this walk the mid-line hole has been
filled by using the numbering as designed rather than by folding into a neighbour, as
Belgium's Conscience and Romania's Brâncuși were.

## plot_00: Πριν από την πόλη

`plot_01` is Classical Athens - democracy, philosophy, the Parthenon - which is the
measure every later Greece is judged by. **Homer is three hundred years older and was
in no plot at all**, which is how a culture ends up with its own origin sitting dead
in its Company.

In the eighth century BC a singer sits before people who have gathered and begins the
story they already know. He is not reading it; **there is nothing written to read.**
And the people listening here know the same story as people six days' sail away whom
they have never met and whose city is not theirs.

These are Greeks before there is any Greece: no state, no capital, no single land -
hundreds of separate cities on rocky peninsulas and islands, at war with each other as
often as with anyone. **What they share is not power. It is a language, some shrines,
and a poem.**

Then, in the same century, they take the Phoenicians' consonant script and do what
nobody had done: **hand the signs they do not need to the vowels.** That is the first
alphabet writing every sound, and every alphabet in Europe descends from it. And among
the oldest surviving Greek writing there are no laws and no accounts, but **lines of
hexameter scratched on pots.**

**So the writing arrives to hold the poem, not the other way round. A people with no
state wrote down what it had instead of one.**

The Tension: a people that became one without a state, on a language and a poem, learns
that what holds it is not power but what everyone knows by heart - which is exactly how
it will survive centuries with no state at all. **And it is the weight. A people that
first chose to be one inside a poem already finished will forever be judged by
something said before it; every later Greece is measured against a standard it did not
set, and the ancient glory is both pride and load.**

## plot_05: Η κρίση

In 2013 `Νίκος` graduates in a year when nearly one young person in two cannot find
work. Nothing has happened to him personally - no bankruptcy, no dismissal, no lost
house. **He simply enters a world with no place in it, and the first thing he opens is
not an advertisement in Greek.**

From 2010 to 2018 the country loses about a quarter of its economy, the deepest
peacetime contraction of any developed economy. Unemployment passes a quarter and among
the young approaches a half. Three lending programmes, cuts to wages and pensions, and
in the summer of 2015 the banks close and controls go on the movement of money. Around
half a million people leave, mostly young and educated.

**What did not collapse was the household.** One pension feeds three generations.
Children come back to the family home at thirty and are taken in without discussion.
`Το καφενείο` stays open on small orders and long sitting, **because it never sold
coffee - it sold room.** And in hands with nothing to do, `το κομπολόι` runs bead by
bead: the object of waiting, through ten years of waiting.

What did that work has a name, and it is `το φιλότιμο` - behaving rightly toward
family, stranger and neighbour, generously and **without calculation.**

The Tension, and it is the reason this plot had to exist: filotimo carried the decade,
and carried it exactly as its own definition requires - without calculation, and
therefore **without a record. What issues no receipt is not measured, not funded and
not repaired.** A virtue that covers a failure keeps people alive and at the same time
makes the failure bearable, and what is bearable takes a very long time to get fixed.
The country left the programmes in 2018 and did not leave the loss: **the quarter of
the economy came back over the years, the half million people did not.**

## plot_99: Ο κόσμος έρχεται

In August, Eleni lays a table for fourteen. Three are her own, home for ten days from
abroad; four are strangers who rented the house opposite and were called over because
that is what one does; two work in the taverna and are not from here. **The table is
full. In November the village will have fewer inhabitants than ever.**

Ten million people receive **more than thirty million visitors a year**, three times
the population. The other leg is at sea: the Greek fleet is still the largest merchant
fleet in the world by tonnage, and `Σταύρος` is away for months while `το καράβι` earns
outside the borders - **in this country wealth comes from the water and not from the
ground.** The Aegean brings both: the sea that carries the visitors has since 2015 been
one of the roads by which people reach Europe. And the summers bring fire; the Evros
fire of 2023 was the largest recorded in any country of the Union. **Each time, before
anything else can be organised, the neighbours are in the road.**

The Tension: the people that became one inside a poem about a man who spent ten years
getting home now lives a decade in which everyone arrives and its own do not come back.
**The table did not empty - it filled with other people.** And filotimo, which does not
ask who is sitting there, takes them all in with the same ease, because that is what it
knows how to do. The decade does not decide whether the country keeps its young. It
shows only that **an identity built on hospitality is superb at receiving and much
worse at holding on, and that a house which is always open is also the house one leaves
most easily.**
