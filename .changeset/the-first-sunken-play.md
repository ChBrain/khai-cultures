**The sunken is the umbrella's third collection, and `cimbri` is its first play.**

`sunken/` is declared beside `groups/` and ships with the umbrella. The count is
taken over `cultures/` alone, so the minor stays at 319 and a sunken play cannot
move it by existing. The registry gains a `sunken` array with no change to the kit.

The Cimbri end in 101 BC and have no successor community; Denmark holds their
remains and says something about them, which is the bar `order_the_sunken.md` sets.
The play collects those remains by specifier and adds its own line, from an origin
outside the chronology to a present that is custody and not descent.
