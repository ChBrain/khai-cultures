**Four lines of the Aargau prose pass kept, the rest reverted.**

The pass rewrote all 22 files one-for-one. Four lines beat what was there and
are kept: the Rüeblitorte named as the screen a canton of asphalt, turbines and
reactors holds up; the same cake as the reason nobody asks about the power
stations; the Ausgleich as survival instinct rather than virtue, in a canton
Napoleon welded out of parts that had never belonged to each other; Martina's
silver ring and the shoulders that rise at noise from the valley, which is a
Tell where the line it replaced described how she speaks.

The rest is reverted, and one file is the reason it had to be:

`plan_tiefenlager.md` is a **plan**, and a plan is a commitment with targets
somebody intends to tick. The pass turned its Taxonomy from `Ein Auftrag` into
`Ein Verdikt` and rewrote all four targets as accusations - "Der Widerstand der
Anwohner ist bürokratisch betäubt", "Das Stigma ist unauslöschlich in die
Landschaft gebrannt". Nobody works toward those. It also deleted the third
target, that the region be party to the decision and not only subject to it,
which is a real commitment the Sachplan process actually carries, and replaced
Nagra - a cooperative seated in Wettingen, in this canton - with "die Experten
aus Bern".

Elsewhere the pass traded facts for images: Brown and Boveri founding a machine
works in Baden in 1891 became "der ländliche Stolz wird gegen Kupfer und Stahl
eingetauscht", and 1415 acquired a vocabulary of razing, amputation and
castration for an occupation after which Schloss Habsburg is still standing. It
introduced 14 en-dashes, which the validator rejects.
