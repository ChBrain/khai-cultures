Asturias gets a plot line and leaves the umbrella. Third of the eight.

**Two plots.**

- `plot_00_nada_se_guarda_solo.md` — in front of the house stands a second, smaller
  house, raised off the ground on four stone mushrooms so that neither water nor
  mouse can climb. Nobody built that for pleasure: where it rains most of the year,
  what is left on the ground does not reach March. This is a narrow strip standing
  on end — the Atlantic hits the range and unloads on it, and between the sea and
  the summits there is almost no flat ground, only dozens of short fast rivers each
  cutting its own valley, so the country is not a plain with villages but a series
  of valleys that barely speak to one another. From that comes the small scale of
  everything, and the obligation to turn out and mend the road, because the road is
  the only thing joining you to the rest: _lo comunal no es una idea generosa sino
  una infraestructura._ And from that comes the rest, which is all one thing. The
  apple will not keep, so it becomes cider. The grain will not keep on the floor, so
  it goes up into the little house on four stones. The milk will not keep, so it is
  curdled and put into the only cold damp larder to hand, which the mountain itself
  provides — limestone full of holes at a steady temperature all year. _La geología
  de la cordillera no es un paisaje, es un electrodoméstico._ And the same wall kept
  one more thing: an army that had crossed the peninsula in a few years stopped
  here, and this corner was left unfinished. The mountain preserved a gap the way it
  preserves a cheese, and for the same reasons.
- `plot_99_lo_que_vuelve.md` — the coal did not fade, it had a date. European
  deadlines set the end of 2018 for subsidised mining, and the Nalón and Caudal
  basins went from tens of thousands of miners to none. What was local was _how_ it
  closed: negotiated, signed between government, companies and unions, the habit of
  sitting down in assembly that long predates 1934 good at least for that. But the
  arithmetic underneath is harder than the closure — more people have died here than
  were born for decades, and this is among the oldest populations in Spain and in
  Europe. Meanwhile the hillside climbs. Where nothing is mown or grazed the scrub
  returns and the trees behind it, and with them the Cantabrian brown bear, under a
  hundred animals in the nineties and several hundred now, its two populations
  rejoined — one of the clearest recoveries in Europe, and it happened in good part
  **because the people left**.

The two ends meet on the same sentence, tested from both sides. What was
deliberately kept — the trade, the assembly, the language, still not official
after another failed attempt — has not kept. What came back is the thing nobody
maintained, and it came back precisely because it stopped being worked.
