**The queue enumerated cultures and nothing else**, so twenty-one groups answered to
`group-ratchet` and to nothing that ranked them. `next` now reads **340 units: 319 cultures,
21 groups, and the sunken, of which there are none yet.**

A group is read off its own wall. `company_coverage` and `culture_conformance` refuse a group
outright — correctly, and #725 is what made that refusal loud — so `group_coverage` answers
instead, and the fields line up almost exactly. `dead` **is** uncast in both: a Company element
no plot fields. `noOrigin`/`noPresent` are the same two questions any plot line is asked.

What has no culture equivalent is the Triggers chain — an entry linking no plot, a plot no entry
chains — so it is carried as its own term rather than bent into one. Rated at `uncast`, because
the two are nearly the same fault said twice, and counted **per item**, because eight orphaned
plots is not the same problem as one. A group is level 1 deliberately, not by the fallback: it
ships no `geo.json` because it is not in the ISO tree, it collects the things that are.

**The medians are now per kind, and that is the load-bearing part.** Most groups have no dated
plot at all, so one house-wide median would have dragged the span down and re-wrung cultures
nobody had touched — the widening would have re-ranked the whole queue as a side effect of
looking somewhere new. The culture queue is verified bit-identical to before this change.
