Castile and León conforms and leaves the umbrella, and the diacritics ratchet
takes its first tick.

**The first repair the wall was built for.** `position_language_es_es_cl.md`
arrived flat: correct Spanish with every accent removed, in its body and in its
declared name both — _"el espanol castellano de Castilla y Leon"_ — while the
culture holding it had been writing the accented form in its own prose all
along. Fifteen words restored and the declared name with them, nothing else
touched, and the link text in the two personas and the play brought into line.
House count **50 → 49**.

Then the tongue moves byte for byte into
`packages/khai-cultures-tongues/es/` as the fifteenth regional Spanish variety.
Only castile_and_leon linked it, so step 3 and step 4 rode together under the
limit set at #608. It is the Castilian the rest of the language is measured
against from the opposite side to Madrid's: not the norm because power sat
there, but because the s/z distinction and the living vosotros survive here in
ordinary speech.

**The culture is authored** — _Es una manera de ser español, no un segundo
pasaporte._

**Two plots, because one sentence costs two.** Five dead entries, and an arc
that turned out to close on itself.

- `plot_00_la_tierra_que_hubo_que_regalar.md` — the Duero was the frontier for
  two centuries, and a no man's land cannot be settled by order. It was settled
  by contract: cartas pueblas, fueros, communal land, exemption from tribute,
  and the right to be judged by neighbours. Whoever agreed to live where they
  might be killed was paid in liberties, because liberties were the only
  currency left. Castile is a line of fortifications that became a country;
  León is a Roman legion's camp that kept its name. The fuero here is not
  decorative antiquity — it is the price paid for there being anybody at all.
  Which is also why the Comuneros of plot_02 were not demanding something new.
- `plot_99_nueve_por_kilometro_cuadrado.md` — the largest autonomous community
  in Spain, emptier than tundra in places, Soria at about nine per square
  kilometre. It did not empty from catastrophe: the work stopped needing people.
  The same modernisation `plan_duero_water_conservation` carried as progress
  made a holding that fed five families feed one better. The answer came
  politically — the 2019 Revuelta de la España Vaciada, and Soria ¡Ya! taking
  three seats in the Cortes in February 2022 — and the hardest fight is the wolf
  north of the Duero, protected in 2021 and loosened again mid-decade, which is
  really an argument about whether a countryside is decided by those who work it
  or those who visit it.

The two ends meet: _qué hay que dar para que alguien se quede._ A land that
filled itself by offering terms has nothing left to offer the people already in
it, and is asking the question of the carta puebla eight centuries later and
backwards.
