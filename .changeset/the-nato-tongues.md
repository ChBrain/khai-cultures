a step about NATO at all.

The EU walk's step 3 moved the members' own tongues. NATO's does not have that shape.
Measured against the six members still under the umbrella:

| member          | blocked by                               |
| --------------- | ---------------------------------------- |
| montenegro      | its own `cnr`                            |

**Four of the six tongues that had to move are held by cultures outside NATO

the first time in this session it has bound a whole pass rather than a single file.
Turkey is the sharpest case: **it is content-whole, holds no tongue, and was blocked
only by Persian**, because Fatih and Yunus Emre read it.

## Scope, and one deliberate omission

Six moved: `sq`, `cnr`, `mk`, `fa`, `bn`, `hi`.

**`yue_hk` was deliberately not moved.** Canada is the only NATO member that needs it,
and canada also nests in hong_kong and reaches its culture-position, so it stays
blocked until hong_kong migrates whatever happens to the tongue. Moving `yue` in this
pass would have freed nothing, and a move that frees nothing is a move that charges a
culture nobody opened for no return.

## Two anchors under a country's name

None of the six had a directory in this package, and the package holds a directory as
a language only if it holds its anchor - so a `sq/` or `fa/` containing only a national
variety would have been a directory that is not a language. Both were read before being
renamed, and neither was a variety:

- **`sq_al`** carries the admirative mood, the definite article suffixed to the noun,
  and a full case system. **None of that is Albania's as against Kosovo's or Gheg's**,
  and its own declared name is already `gjuha shqipe`. Renamed to the anchor on the
  `ar`, `ro` and `pt` precedent.
- **`fa_ir`**, titled "Iranian Persian", carries the ezafe, compound verbs on `kardan`
  and `dadan`, and the `aruz` metre. **Dari and Tajik have all three, and not one
  chapter names anything Iran's as against Afghanistan's or Tajikistan's.** This is the
  **fifth instance of the `sv_fi` shape** - the anchor's material under a country's
  name - and the widest-reaching file moved in this session.

**No `sq_al` and no `fa_ir` are written.** Inventing a variety to fill the hole a
rename leaves is the opposite of the correction, on the `pt` precedent.

## Five titles left wrong on purpose

Every file moved byte for byte. Correcting a title in the same change would make the
move an authoring and charge the culture that owned it - including **iran, which
nobody opened**, which is exactly what the care point forbids.

| tongue | title as it stands         | discharged when          |
| ------ | -------------------------- | ------------------------ |
| `sq`   | "the Albanian Language"    | albania migrates         |
| `cnr`  | "the Montenegrin Language" | montenegro migrates      |
| `mk`   | "the Macedonian Language"  | north_macedonia migrates |
| `fa`   | **"Iranian Persian"**      | iran migrates            |
| `bn`   | **"the Mother Tongue"**    | bangladesh migrates      |

Two of those are wrong in substance and not only in style. `fa` names a national
variety for a file that is the language. And `bn` is not a name at all - **though the
phrase is not arbitrary: this is the tongue whose speakers were shot for it in 1952,
and whose day the world now keeps as International Mother Language Day.** The
resonance belongs in the prose, where it already is; a title is a name. `hi` arrived
correctly titled and opens no deferral.

Three of the five discharge inside this walk. Eight deferrals now stand open, up from
three, and that is the honest cost of the pass.
