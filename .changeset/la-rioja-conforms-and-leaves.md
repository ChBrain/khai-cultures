La Rioja conforms and leaves the umbrella. Third tick of the diacritics ratchet,
and the most pointed one.

**The repair.** `position_language_es_es_ri.md` arrived flat, body and declared
name both — _"el espanol riojano"_. Seventeen words restored, the declared name
with them, nothing else touched. House count **48 → 47**.

The irony is worth recording rather than smoothing over: this is the tongue file
of the one place in the world where Castilian was first written down, in the
margins of a Latin codex at San Millán, and it had reached this house spelled
without a single accent.

It then moves into `packages/khai-cultures-tongues/es/` as the seventeenth
regional Spanish variety, steps 3 and 4 riding together under the limit set at
#608. It completes a set: this variety is **leísta**, while `es_es_cm` and
`es_es_md` are **laísta**, and the three sit within two hundred kilometres of
each other — a sharper picture of the centre of the language than any of them
gives alone.

**The culture is authored** — _Es una manera de ser español, no un segundo
pasaporte._

**Two plots.** Four dead entries, and a region that is a corridor.

- `plot_00_todo_pasa_por_aqui.md` — the Ebro opens a flat, temperate passage
  between two sierras, and that passage is the road between the Meseta and the
  valley running down to the Mediterranean. A corridor accumulates: Roman vines
  that stayed because the ground suited them, the pilgrim road, three kingdoms
  taking turns at the frontier, and monasteries set back from the road just far
  enough to work and not so far as to stop hearing it. Even the name is not a
  kingdom's — it comes from a small river, the Oja. And the Glosas follow from
  the geography rather than from genius: in a corridor you hear languages that
  are not yours daily, and a man writing in the margin what a Latin word means
  in the tongue actually spoken outside is solving a practical problem. _La
  escritura de una lengua nueva aparece justo donde la vieja deja de
  entenderse._
- `plot_99_como_llamarlo.md` — the region has spent the decade arguing about one
  word. In 2017 the consejo regulador let a wine say which village and which
  vineyard it came from; in 2018 Rioja Baja was renamed Rioja Oriental, because
  an adjective that sounds like _less_ is eventually paid for in the price; and
  part of the Rioja alavesa has been pushing for its own denomination, approved
  by the Basque government, challenged by the State, and in the courts since.
  Meanwhile the wine moves on its own — harvest weeks earlier, new planting
  climbing toward the sierra for the cold the valley no longer gives. _El
  viñedo se está yendo del río que le puso el nombre a la comarca._ And in the
  ribera the canning that this region also lives on travels anonymously,
  because it never fought for its name and the wine did.

The two ends meet: the first thing written here was a margin note explaining
what a word meant. A thousand years later the region is still writing in the
margin what its words mean, except the margin is now a regulation and what is
under discussion is money and belonging.
