**Gullah (`gul`), written before the culture that will link it.**

Every other tongue in this package arrived as a repair: a persona was found
carrying a language the house did not hold, and the file went in behind it. This
one goes in ahead. `order_a_culture_without_a_map.md` sets the build order as
tongues, then cultures, then regions, then groups, and `us_gullah_geechee` cannot
be written until Sea Island Creole English exists to be linked.

**The prose is in Gullah, and that was a decision.** This package allows English
only for a tongue nobody _can_ write - `bfi` has no orthography at all - and
Gullah does not qualify: it has a whole New Testament (Sea Island Translation
Team with the American Bible Society, 2005), Lorenzo Turner's corpus, and living
speakers. Taking English would have meant recording a debt the rule says not to
incur. The other risk is real and is the one accepted: this ships under the
language's own name, and `review: native` is what it is flagged for.

156 varieties, 72 languages, version derived at 0.72.0.
