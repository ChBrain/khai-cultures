Make the tongues drift queue worth reading before the walk leans on it for 313
more varieties. It had two faults. It matched on substring, so the German
"weniger" was reported as the country Niger, and it treated every mention alike,
so the three real entries sat in a list where nearly every entry was a variety
naming itself, which the doctrine allows. Now a mention needs a letter boundary
in front of it, and deliberately none behind it, because the mentions worth
reading arrive inflected: "baden-württembergische", the genitive "Melillas". And
the queue sorts by how much a mention wants reading. A culture that does not cast
the variety comes first and is nearly always wrong. A culture that does cast it
is listed second and is the harder read, since the allowed sentence and the
forbidden one name the same place; the Tarifit finding lived in that tier, so it
is listed rather than excused. Proven both ways against the Hamburg variety:
"weniger" no longer reads as Niger, and a planted claim about the schools of
Melilla is reported foreign and sorts to the top. Governance only; the house
ships nothing.
