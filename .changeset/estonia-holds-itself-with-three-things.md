## `plot_00` Maarahvas ja hiied, enne kirja

They do not call themselves Estonians. The word arrives in the nineteenth century;
before it they say **maarahvas**, the land people — as against the manor, the town
and the church, where German is spoken. The peasant is a serf and his language is
what the gentry call the peasants' dialect.

But it is not an Indo-European language. Not Baltic, not Slavic, not Germanic —
every neighbour is a distant cousin of every other, and this is something else,
whose only close relative is across the gulf. **However long the place is ruled,
that language will not dissolve into its neighbours' — it is too foreign to melt.**
Memory is held in the `regilaul` and the `kannel`, in verse built to be
learned without paper, and the holy places are the _hiis_ groves in the forest,
where the church did not reach and the baron's order did not carry. That is where
the forest stops being landscape: **it is where you go when you need something.**

## `plot_05` Põhjala poole, 1991-2001

A man who was put on a train to Siberia as a child stands in front of a foreign
hall and says his country is not a Baltic country. `Lennart Meri` argues
about geography, and wins: the occupation is what was _done_ to them, not what
they _are_. The bridge was already there — Finnish television was the only window
west in the north of the country, and thousands learned Finnish just by watching.

The plot does not hide the price: **the Baltic Way was one body, and ten years
later the northernmost of the three is looking across the gulf and no longer wants
to be the one it is counted with.**

## `plot_06` Tiigrihüpe ja pronksiöö, 1996-2007

A computer goes into a country classroom and a cable into the wall. The roof
leaks. Somebody decided the computer comes first.

The logic is poverty's logic: no money, and no old institutions to demolish
either, so build now what richer countries will only get to in ten years. ID card
2002, the world's first national internet voting in 2005, Skype written in
Tallinn. Then April 2007: the Soviet-era memorial is moved, the city burns for two
nights and one person dies, and Estonia learns it has two memories and one city.
A week later three weeks of network attacks arrive, and **the state that fits in a
pocket discovers a pocket can be emptied.** Both are consequences of the same
decision.

## `plot_99` Praam ja mets

Six on Monday morning there is a queue of men with tool bags at the Tallinn
harbour, and the same queue the other way on Friday evening. `Kristjan` has not
emigrated — he comes home at weekends, votes from his phone — but his week is in
Helsinki and his screen is in English. Two of the three things that held this
people before it had a state have become the state's own emblems: the language is
official, the song is world heritage. **Only the forest got nothing, and it is the
only one that has not changed.**
