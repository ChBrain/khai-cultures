**Visegrad, member one of three.** Hungary out, and the plot line given the two
ends the house asks every culture for.

Hungary arrived at this pull request **already at zero dead Company** — the only
one of the three that did — so nothing here is debt repayment. What it lacked was
an origin and a present.

## The origin was already written, under the wrong number

`plot_01_honfoglalás` is the Magyar tribes crossing into the Carpathian Basin
around 895 and settling among Slavs and Germans, and its Tension is not the
founding of anything: _a nation whose language nobody around it understands_. That
is a `plot_00` in every respect except its number — `order_plot_zero.md` asks for
where a culture **comes from**, and warns that a play opening on a treaty has
described a passport. This one opens on a migration and ends on solitude.

**So it is renumbered, not rewritten.** `plot_00` is outside the chronology and
prependable, so 02, 03 and 04 keep their numbers and nothing else moves.

## The present had to be written

Hungary's plot line stopped at **1956**, seventy years short.

`plot_99_az_ures_hely` — _The Empty Place_. Its Cue is a grandmother laying the
Sunday table with one plate more than there are people in the house, which is a
**non-state subject**, as `order_the_passport.md` requires: not the accession, not
the citizenship law, a woman setting a table.

What the decade is actually doing is two opposite things at once. Free movement
since 2004 has taken hundreds of thousands of young Hungarians west, and the 2011
citizenship law has given Hungarian passports to Hungarians beyond the borders. **The
nation grows whole in language and on paper while it empties in people**, and
those are the same ten years.

The Tension is the part that is new in a thousand years of this play: since
Trianon every Hungarian loss has been something done to the nation — _the nation
was taken, it did not leave_. This one is voluntary, and there is nobody to blame,
because the one who leaves is also going for their family. The open question of the
decade is not whether the Hungarian word survives, but **where it is said** — at the
table with one plate too many, or on the telephone on Sunday afternoon.

It stages what was already standing there waiting for it: **Erzsi**, who in her own
Action leaves an empty place at the festive table for the relatives beyond the
border and the ones who left, and who now cannot say which absence she is mourning;
**Bence**, the Transylvanian, whom the border no longer calls away but bread does;
and **megmaradás**, the plan that has always been aimed at the neighbours and now
turns to face its own gate. `plan_megmaradás` names _fogyatkozás és kivándorlás_
outright — the material was in the play before the plot was.
