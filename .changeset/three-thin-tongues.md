Write out the three tongues the Länder batch found thin. Berlin, Brandenburg and
Bremen each had four chapters of Standard German and nothing of themselves: the
opposite of drift, a variety that claims nothing about a culture because it says
nothing at all. Berlin now carries the unshifted ick, det, wat, Kopp and Pund that
a Low German city kept when it went High German, the j for initial g, and the one
feature that is grammar rather than sound, the collapse of dative and accusative
that the sentence denying it performs. Brandenburg says what it stands on, the East
Low German of the Mark, and what it keeps, the j that grows with nearness to the
city and the landscape words Fließ, Luch and Kietz; its Loses is that it loses its
own contour to Berlinisch, because its features are heard as Berlin outside the
Mark. Bremen carries the northern unshifted s before t and p and the Low German
words that enter the High German sentence undeclined, so the sentence holds two
languages and one grammar. The Schnauze is written as a register the tongue holds,
never as a temperament of the people who speak it. Empty rather than a patch: a
variety in `packages/` is not in the umbrella's `files`, so editing one ships
nothing until the tongues package publishes itself.
