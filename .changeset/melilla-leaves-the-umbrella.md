Melilla gets a plot line and leaves the umbrella. **The eighth of the eight, and
the last Spanish sub-national culture under it: `packages/khai-cultures/cultures`
now holds no `es_` directory at all.** All nineteen are packages.

**Two plots, written against Ceuta rather than after it.** Both are Spanish
cities on the African coast and the easy failure was to write the same pair
twice. The distinction that keeps them apart is real: **Ceuta controls a passage;
Melilla is the door of a hinterland.**

- `plot_00_el_unico_abrigo.md` — a skipper working down the Rif coast has gone
  days without anywhere to get into: cliff, beach open to the north, not a notch
  to anchor in with wind. Then he rounds a large cape and behind it is a rock
  with sheltered water at its foot, the only place for many miles where a ship
  can wait out weather. That is the first fact and it has never changed, which is
  why there was a Phoenician and then a Roman town on this rock long before the
  name it carries now. But an anchorage is worth nothing by itself: it is worth
  what lies behind it, and behind it is a populated, working mountain that is
  hard to govern from outside. _Esto no controla una travesía, es la puerta de
  una comarca. La ciudad no vive de lo que pasa por delante, vive de lo que baja
  de detrás._ From which follows the asymmetry to reckon with from the start: the
  district and the port are one economy and have always been two jurisdictions —
  and, in time, the city came to hold both halves of its own trade inside itself.
- `plot_99_la_puerta_cerrada.md` — the door closed in pieces and each piece took
  something. In August 2018 the other side closed the commercial customs post
  without notice — one this city had held since the nineteenth century, which is
  a real difference from the other Spanish city on this coast, **which never had
  one and therefore could not lose it.** What was left was the porteo, carrying
  on backs what could no longer go by lorry, until that ended too; then the whole
  border shut for more than two years, and when it reopened the old movement did
  not return. And on 24 June 2022 the perimeter saw the gravest thing this city
  has known in democracy: a mass attempt to enter that ended with dozens dead,
  counts disputed between the parties, investigations opened and accounts that do
  not agree. What stands is what was built while the door was open — an entire
  Modernista ensanche raised in thirty years on iron money, twelve kilometres
  from the Rif, one of the largest concentrations of that architecture in the
  country. And one account inside is still open: close to half the residents
  carry the district's root and speak at home a language the city does not
  officially recognise.

The 24 June 2022 entry is written the way this house wrote Cyprus and Ceuta: the
fact stated, the disputed counts and the open investigations named as disputed
and open, and no adjudication.
