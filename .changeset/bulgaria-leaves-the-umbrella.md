## plot_00: По-стари от името си

`plot_01` is the Golden Age and the making of Cyrillic, which is already the thing
this culture is proudest of. The origin has to be older than the letters, and it is
older than the name.

In the seventh century, in a village on the plain south of the Danube, farmers watch
horsemen arrive from the steppe speaking a language they have never heard. **Nobody
drives anybody out.** Within a few generations the horsemen's grandchildren speak the
farmers' language - and the whole people carries the horsemen's name.

The ground beneath both is older still. Thracian mounds stand in the fields and the
ploughman goes round them without knowing who lies inside; by the Black Sea a
necropolis holds **the oldest worked gold known anywhere, from about 4500 BC** -
older than the pyramids and older than writing. The Thracians left no letters of
their own: graves, gold, and, it is said, song.

So the people that emerges is **assembled**: the name from the horsemen, the speech
from the farmers, the ground from a people who never wrote. Even the letters come
later and from outside, carried by two brothers from Thessaloniki.

What holds through all the assembling is not a state but the pasture. The shepherd
is alone with the flock and plays the `гайда` - a skin bag, a chanter
and a drone pulling one long unbroken note underneath. The beat of that music does
not divide evenly, it runs in sevens, nines and elevens, and no neighbouring empire
ever bothered to write it down. The villages wrote it down, with mouths and hands and
no paper. Much later "Излел е Дельо хайдутин", played on a Rhodope kaba gajda, left
the solar system on Voyager's Golden Record: **the oldest thing this nation has is
also the furthest thing it has sent.**

The Tension: a people whose name, speech and letters come from three different places
learns early that a self is assembled - and that what was assembled can come apart
piece by piece. That is where the patience to endure five centuries of `игото` comes
from: this nation survives by holding, not by ruling. **And it is where the shadow
comes from too. Whoever knows his ground is older than his name starts measuring
himself by his antiquity rather than by his today, and pride in what he gave a
thousand years ago can quietly replace the question of what he gives now.**

## plot_99: Подкваската

That question, asked straight.

At dawn in May `Невена` is in the field in the Rose Valley
picking petals while the dew is still on them. **There are fewer hands along the row
than last year, and last year there were fewer than the year before.** The rose does
not wait: what is picked after sunrise is no longer worth what was picked before it.

Bulgaria is emptying faster than any country on earth. The 2021 census found about
6.5 million people - some 850 000 fewer than ten years earlier, and about two and a
half million fewer than at the end of the eighties. **No war caused it.**
`Георги` left for the west after accession and comes home only for
holidays; the villages around the `розобер` keep fewer hands each
year and their schools close.

And yet three things leave this ground for the world and do not stop. **Rose oil**: a
large share of the world's supply comes out of the valley around Kazanlak, and a
single kilo needs tonnes of petals picked by hand before the sun. **`Кисело
мляко`**: the bacterium in it carries the country's name, and
the yoghurt itself is made with a starter passed from pot to pot - **a living thing
that survives only if somebody carries it onward and feeds it.** And the people.

Once a year, though, the emptied squares fill. On `24 май` the
children come out with flowers and wreaths, sing "Върви, народе възродени" and
celebrate the letters. Георги comes home for that day without hesitating, and Невена
watches grandchildren who speak to each other in another language and know that
anthem by heart. **The square is not filled by a state, an army or a victory. It is
filled by the alphabet.**

The Tension: the nation that gave the Slavs their letters now exports three things -
the most delicate thing its ground makes, a live starter named after it, and itself.
All three travel well. **The difference is that the first two were made to be carried
elsewhere and kept alive, and the third is what stays behind to make them.** `Да не
изчезнем` is the name of a plan, not of something achieved. The decade does not
settle whether a people scattered across the world and alive in other people's houses
has been preserved or is going. But 24 May shows something the census does not
measure: what gets this nation on its feet is not a state and not a victory but the
letters it made itself. **Only, an alphabet can be kept by a few, and a valley can be
picked only by hands.**
