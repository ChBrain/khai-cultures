**Two tongues the house named and could not point at.** Both are targets from
`order_a_tongue_without_an_address.md`, and between them they are that order's two
detectable tiers made concrete.

**Nauruan (`na`) was tier 1 — found by a rule.** `nauru/persona_the_miner.md` says in
plain prose _"Nauruan is the miner's mother tongue"_ and then wires speaking, thinking
and hearing at mother-tongue depth with nothing to point at; `persona_the_frigatebird_catcher.md`
does the same. Nauru's only language position was `en_nr`, English as spoken there — so the
two personas whose tongue is _not_ English were the two with no address. The house-wide
scan for this fault returned exactly these two files, so tier 1 is now at zero.

**Punic (`xpu`) was tier 2 — found by reading.** `persona_septimius.md` wires Latin to `la`
and Arabic to its own culture, then carries Punic at `process_speaking_carried.md` with the
tongue named in Arabic prose alone. The file _passes_ the tier-1 rule, because it does wire
tongues — just not that one.

Each has a defining trait that is about text, which is what this house holds. Nauruan ships
**two live orthographies** — the missionary spelling and the 1938 committee revision — so the
tilde vowels are present or absent by the writer's schooling rather than by rule; it is a
language in no trouble spoken and in trouble typed. Punic is the opposite: **dead in the mouth
and open on the page**, thousands of inscriptions in a consonants-only abjad, with every vowel
reconstructed from Greek and Latin transcription and one passage of running speech preserved
by Roman copyists who could not read it.

Both are written in English on the `nv` precedent. The grammar is described accurately; four
chapters composed _in_ either would be invention, and a wrong sentence in a language of twelve
thousand speakers is not a small thing to publish. Nauruan carries `review: "native"`. **Punic
deliberately does not** — there is no native reader to flag it for and has not been since about
the fifth century. What that file wants is an epigrapher, and the provenance says so.

The tongues version moves 0.69.0 → 0.71.0 because that minor **is** the language count, derived
by `build.mjs` rather than declared; the 120 dependent range updates are its output, and
`packages/*/package.json` is `shared` in `branchScope`, so they bind no lane.
