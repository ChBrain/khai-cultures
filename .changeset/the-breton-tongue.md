**Breton joins the tongues.**

The house held no Breton and no culture could bring it in: Brittany has no play
yet, so there was nothing to migrate it out of. It is written here instead, the
way Corsican was written one release ago.

Every chapter is the office and not the map. What it gives: the mutations, which
change a word's first letter and not its last according to whatever stands
before it; the prepositions that inflect for person, so `ganin` and `ganit` are
one word rather than two; vigesimal counting the French around it gave up; and
two great varieties, KLT and Vannetais, over which the orthographic argument was
fought to three competing answers instead of being left alone. What its grammar
makes a speaker mark: the element in focus, because there is no neutral word
order to fall back on and a subject placed before the verb leaves that verb in
the third singular however many the subject is - `ar vugale a zebr`, not
`a zebront` - so every sentence declares what its speaker put forward. What it
cannot say: itself with one vocabulary, because administration, school and
science arrived in French and the Breton words built to replace them were built
by learners, leaving the neo-Breton of the schools and the inherited Breton of
the old speakers thin against each other; and anything at all without also
saying something about the language, since in Brittany speaking Breton is
already a statement. And how it sorts the world, by making the join between two
words audible so that no word can be lifted out of its sentence, and by settling
the ranking before the mouth opens rather than repairing it afterwards.

This is the second tongue whose Has and Loses both turn on a written norm, and
the two answer the question opposite ways. Corsican has no norm because nobody
wanted one. Breton has three because everybody did.

Flagged `review: native`. The prose is written in peurunvan and not by a Breton
speaker, and wants one before anyone treats it as settled.

and updated the declared range in the 50 packages that depend on it. Ships no
