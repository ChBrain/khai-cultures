**Visegrad, member two of three.** Poland out, at zero dead Company, with both ends
of its plot line written.

Poland arrived carrying **four dead Company elements** — the White Eagle, pierogi,
the pilgrimage and Wigilia — and a plot line that ran 1772 to 1989 with no origin
and no present. The two new plots clear all four, because each of the four was
waiting for one of them.

## plot_00: Lech i orzeł

`order_plot_zero.md` asks where a culture **comes from**, not where a state was
founded. Poland's baptism in 966 would have been the passport; the legend is not.

A wanderer looks for somewhere to settle on a great plain with neither mountains
nor sea to shelter it, sees a white eagle over its nest against a red sunset, and
goes no further. Lech founds **Gniezno** where he saw the **gniazdo**, and takes the
eagle for a sign. The country's name comes from neither a dynasty nor a ruler but
from **pole**, the open field: the _Polanie_ are the people of the plain.

The Tension is the whole play in one sentence. A plain gives fertile soil and a
road open in every direction, which means open to the neighbours in the same
degree. **A nation named after a field has no border that cannot be walked across,
so its only fixed point is a sign rather than a frontier** — the eagle, the speech,
the custom. That is its strength, because a sign cannot be divided between three
empires. It is also its weakness, because a nation that knows itself by a sign
rather than a border must defend the sign from its own people too.

It stages `piece_orzel_bialy`, whose own Load Bearing says the eagle stood on
insurgents' seals when there was no state — **a sign taken up before there was
anything for it to stand for.**

## plot_99: Kraj, do którego się przyjeżdża

The plot line stopped at 1989, and `plot_04`'s own Tension already named what came
next: _emigrację, która opróżnia kraj_. So the present had to move past emptying.

**It did, in the opposite direction to Hungary's**, and that is the honest reading
rather than a symmetry — after 2004 some two million Poles left, and then the
country began to fill: for work first, and after 2022 in flight. Poland, which
counted the ones who left for two hundred years, is counting arrivals for the first
time.

The Cue is Halina in a stairwell hearing, through the wall, a language she
understands every third word of — a **non-state subject**, as
`order_the_passport.md` requires. Not the accession, not a policy: a neighbour
upstairs speaking Ukrainian to a child and Polish to the lift.

It stages the other three dead, and each one earns it from its own text:

- **Wigilia** already sets an empty plate for the absent traveller. The custom is
  centuries old and was always symbolic. It is now being tested on a real one.
- **pierogi** are made in company at the table, and the hands being taught at that
  table are no longer only Polish.
- **the pilgrimage**'s own Direction says _obcy stają się jednym ludem_ — strangers
  become one people. It is the only custom this nation has for taking someone in.

The Tension: **martyrologia** taught Poles to know themselves by what was taken
from them; it did not teach what to do with people who were robbed somewhere else
and now live one floor up. The decade does not decide whether the newcomers stay —
some already have. It decides whether **polskość is a sign you carry or a descent
you prove.**
