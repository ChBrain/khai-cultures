**Brittany was a passport; restaged, and Gallo cast.**

Six plots, five of them a state acting on a people, became eight with three of
them so. The origin is now a migration and not a kingdom: the Britons crossing
between the fifth and seventh centuries, taking the west and leaving the east
to the people already there, which is where the two languages come from and why
the line between them is on no map. Then linen money turned into stone, three
centuries of men at sea, a million people leaving for Paris, and the bocage
cleared for a food industry. The symbole, Nantes and the tongue without a status
are kept; 851, 1532 and 1790 keep their place in the Arc.

Gallo is staged. When this culture shipped there was no Gallo position and the
culture-position named it as the thing that is never counted, which stopped
being true when `gallo/position_language_gallo.md` landed. The tongue is now in
the Company, the culture-position opens on two languages, and a persona speaks
Gallo and no Breton.

`khai-cultures-fr-brittany` is at 0.1.0 with no CHANGELOG and has never
published, so this declares no bump: there is no released content for it to
change.
