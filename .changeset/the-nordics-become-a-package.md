**All five members are packages, so the group can be one.**

`migrate_culture.mjs --group nordics` had been refusing since the group was
written, because a group package cannot depend on members that live inside the
umbrella. Denmark, Sweden, Norway, Finland and Iceland went out one per pull
request, each carrying the plots that cleared its dead Company, and with Iceland
merged the plan came back clean. `nordics -> @chbrain/khai-cultures-nordics`,
**0 own link files and 0 inbound** — the group already addressed every member
through its package name, so nothing had to be rewritten.

**One thing was fixed first.** The group's own persona, `the Interpreter`, was
declared in the play's Company and staged in no plot. `company-coverage` does not
charge groups — the wall reads cultures only — so nothing was failing; it was dead
all the same, and it is exactly the fault the five member pull requests have spent
this run clearing. She was plainly written for `plot_99`: the plot is four people
in their twenties switching to English, and her Action says she is the one who
goes along with it and feels the loss. **She is now in that room.** The Cue puts
her at the table, the Action says what she does there — follows all three of the
others because Swedish was made compulsory for her at nine, answers in Swedish
half a beat late, notices the switch — and the Stage carries her with the reason
it matters: _the ones who inherited the comprehension cannot feel it going,
because they never felt it arrive._

The package depends on all five members plus the tongues and the language engine,
and the registry resolves them. **319 cultures at 0.319.0**; production packages
**57**, 0 findings — group packages are outside that count, as `khai-cultures-dach`
already is.

The umbrella's `groups/` directory keeps the rest; the nordics is the fourth group
to leave it.
