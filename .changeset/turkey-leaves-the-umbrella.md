## What was actually blocking it

This is the part worth keeping. Turkey held **no tongue of its own** and owed
**nothing** in its Company. Every one of its own affairs was in order. The single
thing standing between a finished culture and its package was this:

```
turkey cannot migrate yet:
  - persona_fatih.md links ../iran/position_language_fa_ir.md
  - persona_yunus_emre.md links ../iran/position_language_fa_ir.md
```

**A file inside iran** - a culture in no group being run, that nobody had opened, and
that nothing else in this walk touched. Fatih and Yunus Emre read Persian, so turkey
could not be published while Persian sat in somebody's culture directory. #641 moved
it, and turkey became ready without a single byte of its own changing.

That is the working order's care point paying off in the one direction nobody plans
for: **the step-3 pass that looked like it was for albania was the one that freed
turkey.**

`plot_05` and `plot_06` are the interesting ones. **Most cultures in this house have
no mid-line plots at all** - that is the hole greece and romania each had to be given
one for. Turkey has two, and neither is a state date: one is an agricultural policy
that changed what a whole coast grows and drinks, and the other is the argument the
culture is actually having.

And `plot_00` is exactly what `order_plot_zero.md` asks for and rarely gets: **not a
founding, but the moment a people stopped being described and started writing.**
