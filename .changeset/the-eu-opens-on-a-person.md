**The group the design record named as the hardest case, and it was.**

`groups-and-references.md` §2.1.1 warns that a group is the easy place to get
`order_the_passport` wrong, "because the obvious material for an alliance is the
alliance", and it names the EU as the worst of them. It was right. All four of
this group's Cues opened on a state or a community of states: _six nations look
for a way_, _the common market of the six prospers_, _the community decides to
match the moment_, _the nations of the East ask to come home_. Four passports in
a row.

On top of that the group ratchet found what it found everywhere: a Triggers
chapter naming three themes and linking to none of the four plots sitting in the
same directory, no `plot_00`, no `plot_99`, and no own Company at all.

**Every Cue now opens on a person.** A boy walking from Swabia to Bologna. A
crate of Ruhr coal reaching a Lorraine steelworks with nobody's permission. A
woman in Cork counting what her brother sends home from Birmingham against a
Dublin wage that does not exist. A haulier with a folder of currencies and a
second folder of customs forms. A plumber in Katowice who knows somebody already
working west without papers, for whom accession is not a flag but the difference
between that and a contract. Three thousand students nobody expected anything of.

The treaties are all still here and still named. What changed is who the scene
opens on.

## `plot_00` The Licence to Teach Anywhere, from 1088

The founding documents read as though free movement were invented in 1957, and
they read that way because **the founders were rebuilding rather than inventing,
and rebuilding is less flattering.** For four hundred years this continent had a
qualification that travelled with the person holding it and had no state behind
it: the _licentia ubique docendi_, issued by universities with no army and no
territory, honoured by institutions that had never corresponded and by princes at
war with each other. Erasmus spent his working life in seven cities in five of
what are now member states. Then the vernaculars rose, the confessions split, the
states hardened, and the thing that had been normal became the thing that has to
be reconstructed by treaty.

## `plot_99` The Returners, since 1987

The exchange scheme is the Union's only genuinely popular institution, **and it is
popular because it is the only one that happened to people rather than to their
governments.** It has moved something like fifteen million; the programme's own
accounting puts the children born to couples who met on it in the high hundreds of
thousands.

And the argument runs both ways at once. A nurse leaving Riga is a nurse Riga has
lost. A town emptied of everyone under thirty does not experience free movement as
a freedom, and the resentment that produces is not xenophobia and does not answer
to being called it. **The licence is held most warmly by the people who used it and
most coldly by the people whose children used it and did not come back. Both are
true, and they are the same policy.**

Then 2022: several million admitted in weeks by activating a directive written in
2001 and never once used, after an argument that lasted days rather than the years
it took the previous time. The plot says plainly that nobody has satisfactorily
explained the difference to themselves.

## Its own Company

The group had none. Two files, both belonging to the space between the members
rather than to any of them:

- **`position_the_licence_to_move`** — a permission that belongs to the person and
  not the state that issued it, whose ancestor is the medieval licence. _It is the
  one thing in the Union most people have actually used_, and it loses ground not
  to unpopularity but to distance: a right nobody in a town has ever exercised is
  indistinguishable, to that town, from a right that does not exist.
- **`persona_the_returner`** — went at nineteen, came back at twenty-three, says
  _back home_ about two places in the same conversation without hearing herself.
  Her Shadow is the point: she mistook a permission for a fact of nature, and was
  surprised when the member that left took its universities out — not that it
  happened, but that it _could_.
