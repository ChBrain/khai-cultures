**The mother-tongue rule charged two personas for prose that was right, and the
fault was in the rule.**

`order_the_mother_tongue.md` landed an hour ago asking, where a Projection links
exactly one tongue, that the persona be written in it. One tongue link removes the
question `us_california/persona_chloe.md` poses - which of several tongues the
mother grip takes - and leaves a second question it did not ask: **whether that
single tongue belongs to the mother grip at all.**

`guinea_bissau/persona_okinka_pampa.md` says in her own prose that her language is
**Bijago**:

> A sua lingua i a Bijago: el ta papia i ta pensa na lingua di as ilhas

The only tongue she LINKS is the Portuguese she _"ta karega di longi"_ - carries
from far off - under a `borrowed` grip. The rule read that one link as her mother
tongue. Her sister finding, `persona_lavrador_di_caju.md`, was wrong the same way.

## The guard

The single tongue must sit **nearer a mother grip than to any other grip.** That is
a distance test, and a far weaker one than the test that failed at #702: it is not
choosing between tongues, only asking which grip the one tongue stands beside.

- Der Abt keeps his finding: `[Schweizerdeutsch](gsw)` sits next to the verb he
  _spricht_, and the Latin he writes in is named in prose without a link.
- Okinka Pampa loses hers: `borrowed` is closer to the Portuguese than either of
  her mother grips.

The four that remain are all the same shape: written in the standard, speaking the
dialect - `ch_st_gallen` twice, `switzerland/persona_tell.md`, and
`de_schleswig_holstein/persona_wullenwever.md`.

## What Guinea-Bissau's real fault is

**Bijago is named and not held.** Okinka Pampa is said to think in a tongue this
house has no file for - which is the fault `an`'s provenance note opened about
Aragonese, and the one that brought Yiddish to light. `persona_wiring` still cannot
see it: it asks whether a grip has a tongue, not whether the tongue a persona is
SAID to think in exists. That needs reading prose for a language name, which no
counter here does, and it is carried as a Target rather than guessed at.

## How this was found

By starting the worked example the campaign needs. The first culture opened was
Guinea-Bissau, and its two findings did not survive being read.
