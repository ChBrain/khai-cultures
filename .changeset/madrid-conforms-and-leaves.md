Madrid conforms and leaves the umbrella.

The sixth run of the sub-national recipe, and the one where the nesting
sentence is hardest to write, because Madrid is where the standard was made.

**The tongue.** `position_language_es_es_md.md` moves byte for byte into
`packages/khai-cultures-tongues/es/`, the fourteenth regional Spanish variety
there. Only madrid linked it, so step 3 and step 4 rode together under the
limit set at #608.

It is the odd one of the fourteen, and instructively so. The other thirteen
describe a Castilian coloured by something next door. This one is the Castilian
of the place the norm came from, so it has almost nothing next door to point
at, and what it ends up describing is Spanish itself — ser against estar, the
s/z distinction, vosotros, the subjunctive — with exactly one local claim in
it, the castizo laísmo. That is the honest shape of a variety that is the
reference: it can only describe itself as the thing everything else is measured
against, plus one habit the norm does not accept.

Madrid keeps a second language-ish position, `position_castilian_dialect.md`
(_el habla madrileña_), which is not a `position_language_` file and so stays
with the culture.

**The culture is authored.** The house's nesting sentence is appended to
`position_culture_madrilenidad.md` — _Es una manera de ser español, no un
segundo pasaporte_ — which for the capital is the least obvious of the eleven
and the most worth saying.

**Two plots, because one sentence costs two.** Five dead entries this time,
one more than navarre.

- `plot_00_el_agua_que_no_se_ve.md` — Madrid has no reason to exist. No river,
  no port, no sea, no stronghold, no ancient see. What it has is the Guadarrama
  above it and the meltwater running underneath it, reached by hand-dug
  galleries. That is why the Córdoba emirate put a frontier fortress here in
  the ninth century, and why Philip II chose it in 1561: Toledo had the
  primate, Valladolid had its pride, and Madrid had a wall, an aquifer and
  nobody important. It was picked for having nothing of its own to defend.
- `plot_99_el_aire_y_el_alquiler.md` — the low-emission zone worked: 2018,
  the 2019 suspension struck down in court, the perimeter widened to the whole
  municipality, NO2 down, with the EU referral of Spain to the Court of Justice
  underneath it all. And the centre cleared of cars filled with tourist flats.
  It closes onto plot_01, whose own Tension is the housing crisis the court
  caused on arrival in 1561: the same mechanism, except the decree is now the
  price.

The pattern the house keeps finding turns up here in a new key. The cocido and
the tapeo survive because they can be sold; the long sobremesa, which nobody
charges for, is the part going. What is taught — or priced — survives, and what
is assumed is lost.

**Then the mechanics.** `madrid` renames to `es_madrid`, migrates to
`@chbrain/khai-cultures-es-madrid`, and the four inbound links to the tongue
are rewritten to the specifier.

Counts, run rather than assumed: sub-national cultures not yet conforming
54 → 53; production packages 93 → 94; umbrella cultures 226 → 225; tongues
varieties 138 → 139 with the language count unchanged. `es_madrid: 0 dead,
0 waived, 14 in Company`.

Reported, not repaired: the tongue's body is written without accents
throughout — a third instance, after `es_es_ct` and `es_es_nc`, which is now a
pattern in the Spanish varieties rather than a one-off. The play declares
`language: es`, which here is simply correct, so Madrid does not add to the
#640 count.
