## And the chapter #614 flagged

`pt_br` carried **the anchor's material under a variety's name**: its `## Has` opened
_"O que o português permite dizer"_ and then listed the personal infinitive, the
future subjunctive and nasal vowels — all pan-Portuguese, and the first two in the
anchor in substance. That was the `sv_fi` fault confined to one chapter, and #614
left it because rewriting it would have charged `brazil` for five dead Company
elements.

`brazil` is a package now, and this file sits in the tongues package regardless, so
**rewriting the chapter authors no culture.** It now carries what is actually
Brazilian and appears in no other file here:

- **unstressed vowels pronounced in full**, so _telefone_ keeps its four syllables
  where the European reduces them to two — the difference an ear catches before any
  word
- **proclisis as the natural order**, _me diz_ and _te amo_ even opening a sentence,
  which the grammar on the other side does not allow
- **`a gente` as a whole first person plural taking a singular verb**, a grammatical
  person the language grew after it crossed, and that European Portuguese does not
  have

Checked against the anchor: **none of the three appears there.** The variety's other
three chapters were already its own and are untouched.
