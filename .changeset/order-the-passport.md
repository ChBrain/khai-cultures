**The Passport**, an order that narrows the defining question the way
`order_plot_zero.md` narrowed it at the floor.

Three cultures were staged in a week - Corsica, Brittany and Alsace - and
sixteen of their eighteen plots were a state acting on a people. All three cited
`order_the_defining_question.md` in their REFERENCES while breaking the failure
it names, and one defended the shape in writing.

The order records three things. That the defining question's tell is
self-referential: it measures the agreement between a play's self-description and
its scenes, so a play whose Arc, Name and Stakes are themselves constitutional
passes it perfectly. That the test which does work is naming the subject of each
plot's Cue. And that two candidate counters were measured against the whole house
and both fail - the piece-or-process proxy passes the worst offender, and the
dated-title proxy is inverted, since Hesse dates five of five titles and is sound
while the restaged Brittany dates one of eight.

It also records what the rewrites taught, which is not what the diagnosis
predicted: the political plots were not merely crowding others out, they were
answering a different question, and a plot line with the state as its only actor
has only one question available to it. Ships nothing.
