Extremadura conforms and leaves the umbrella. Every Spanish sub-national culture
now conforms, and the sub-national debt is the 48 American ones alone.

**A repair the wall could not see.** `position_language_es_es_ex.md` was never in
the count of fifty, because its body carried _extremeña_, _perdío_, _friín_ — a
handful of marks, enough to pass. Its declared name still read _"el espanol
extremeno"_ and sixteen further words were missing their accents. Repaired
anyway, declared name included, because the culture was being authored
regardless.

That is the evidence behind the open Target in
`management/orders/order_the_written_accent.md`: **a rule that asks for one mark
lets a file with two marks and thirty faults through.** The flat count is
unchanged at 46, correctly — this file was never in it. Nine near-zero files are
still waiting behind that threshold, and this one was not even among them.

The tongue then moves into `packages/khai-cultures-tongues/es/` as the
nineteenth regional Spanish variety. It is the southern set — seseo, aspirated
final s, intervocalic d gone — sitting on a Leonese floor the rest of Spanish
does not have, which is what the `-ino` diminutive is doing there.

**The culture is authored** — _Es una manera de ser español, no un segundo
pasaporte._

**Two plots.** Four dead entries, and a culture whose subject is time.

- `plot_00_lo_que_tarda.md` — the name means the far edge, and the soil agrees:
  slate and granite, thin ground, burning summers. Intensive cereal ruins it in
  two generations, so what was made instead was the dehesa, which is neither
  forest nor field but something invented — oaks thinned by hand to a park, with
  pasture under them, stock on the pasture and acorn in autumn, producing four
  things at once because no one of them would feed anybody alone. And all of it
  is slow: cork every nine or ten years and not sooner on pain of killing the
  tree, decades before a holm oak really bears, a montanera of two or three
  months at the end of two years of pig. _La dehesa no admite prisa, y quien mete
  prisa deja de tener dehesa._ Which is also why so many left: whoever stayed
  had a lifetime of waiting for a tree, and whoever went could stake everything
  on one year.
- `plot_99_el_sol_se_va_por_el_cable.md` — the region with among the lowest
  incomes in Spain is one of the largest solar producers in Europe, and those
  are the same fact: flat, cheap, sunlit ground with few people to object is
  exactly what a photovoltaic plant asks for. The two largest in Europe when
  built are here, named Núñez de Balboa and Francisco Pizarro, after two men who
  left this land to make a living elsewhere. The land that exported people for
  five centuries now exports electricity, down a cable, to the same places those
  two ended up. Meanwhile the Plasencia–Badajoz line opened in 2022 after years
  of _tren digno_ protests and still does not properly reach Madrid. And _la
  seca_ takes the oaks that nothing replaces, because nothing young grows under
  grazing — a dehesa without regrowth is not a sick wood, it is a wood with a
  date.
