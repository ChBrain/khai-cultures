**A unit is anything the house packs. A culture is a unit the count is taken over. A group
was the first thing that is one without being the other.** `order_the_sunken.md` proposes the
second: a production for a people that has ended, assembled out of what the living are
holding.

## Found the only way this house finds anything

A hole is visible here only when something already points at it - Yiddish by four live
references, Magdeburg 1631 by the Reiter's own yearbook. The sunken are found the same way:
a piece, a place or a persona naming a people with no node. Swept against thirty-four
spellings, **seventeen already appear**: Taíno in 18 files, Sumer in 14, Nabataean in 14,
Phoenician in 9, Aksum in 9, Mississippian in 7, Carthage and Punic in 5, Únětice in 1.

Tens, not hundreds, and every one already carried by somebody.

## The bar, both halves

**No successor community** - Latin is not sunken, it became Romance; Punic is. And **already
carried by a living culture that says something about it**. Without the second half this
becomes a catalogue of every archaeological horizon with a type site.

## Custody is its present, not an exemption

The order refuses a `plot_99` waiver. A sunken production has a present; it is the remains'
and not the people's, and it is usually contested. Nebra writes itself: looted in 1999,
recovered in a police sting in 2002, held at Halle, on the UNESCO register since 2013, and
printed on the marketing of a Bundesland reassembled in 1990 as proof that it has roots.
Somebody else's roots - which `de_saxony_anhalt`'s own `plot_09` already calls a
_geliehener Ursprung_, while the only place the house names those people is one row of a
references table.

## Most of the bill is already paid

`order_a_group_is_not_a_culture.md` bought this. `cultureUnits` splits on membership of
`cultureIds()` and nothing else, so any non-culture unit is declined by all four content
walls and named by `notCultureNote`, instead of passing green having inspected nothing -
which is what happened the first time a group was handed to them. Nothing in that split has
to learn the word "sunken".

What is unpaid: the sunken has no walls of its own, and neither do groups. The order says
answer both ratchets together, never twice.

## And it closes something open

`order_the_mother_tongue.md` carries an unresolved Target: `libya/persona_septimius.md` grew
up in Punic, "which is extinct and unheld, and **no wiring clears that**". A tongue can be
extinct and a persona can name it, and today there is nowhere for either to point.

Eight Targets, none checked. No code, no collection, no play yet: the order comes first this
time, which is the reverse of how groups arrived.
