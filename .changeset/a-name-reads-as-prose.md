**A sixteenth wall, asked of the same links as the thirteenth.** `links` checks that
an address points at something. It never asks what the link is _called_, and
`[pitch_corsica.md](pitch_corsica.md)` resolves perfectly while stopping the sentence
dead.

Measured over 41,944 markdown links: 6,129 name a link after its file, and 5,494 of
those are registers — a row in the REFERENCES table, a `- **Anchor:**` line, a bare
back-reference on its own line — where the filename _is_ the information the reader
came for. Counting those would be the counter `order_the_passport.md` forbids. So the
question is asked of sentences only, and a line is a sentence when, with its links
taken out, three words of it are still standing.

That leaves 635, and the good news is where they are not: **every play, plot, persona,
place, piece, position and process in the house carries zero.** The voice was already
right where the voice is the product. All 635 sit in `README.md` (305) and
`REFERENCES.md` (330), and they are two sentences repeated across some six hundred
packages. The first hands over its own repair, the natural name being one word to the
left: `[The pitch](pitch_corsica.md) is written from…`.

So the rule lands in two pieces. `tests/house.test.mjs` holds the culture prose at zero
outright, since that is where it already is. `khai-link-names` is a ratchet over written
units, because six hundred packages cannot be repaired in one lane and a wall that turns
red on all of them is a wall that gets bypassed. It does not charge the undermined:
unlike an address, a name only goes wrong when the sentence holding it is rewritten.

`management/orders/order_a_name_reads_as_prose.md` records the rule and carries the
repair of the 635 as targets.
