**A mapless culture can have one parent.**

`<host>_<kin>_minority` was the Danish-German border's form, not the general
one. Both its minorities have a kin that acts. The Gullah Geechee were made on
the Sea Islands and the Catawba were on the river before Carolina existed:
neither has a kin, so the id is `<host>_<name>` with no suffix and the
culture-position links the host alone. Tier one binds what a culture links, not
what it narrates, so Catawba ships without `chc`.

The first Target was backwards. `subnational-conformance` does not over-require
where there is no geography; it reads `geo.json` for an ISO code and skips the
culture when there is none, so a mapless culture nesting in nothing would pass.
319 cultures, 319 with a `geo.json` - the blindness has never been exercised.
