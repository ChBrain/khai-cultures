**Finland's Arc is trust, and the plot line had no civil war in it.**

Four plots — 1835, 1917, 1939, and the school reform — with the year 1918 simply
absent between the second and the third. **Eight of nineteen Company elements

**`plot_00` Kaski ja sauna, ennen kirjoitusta.** On a new site you build the sauna
first, not the house — it is the one warm clean space you can raise quickly, so
children are born in it, the dead are washed in it, meat is smoked and malt
sprouted in it. **It is a building the settlement runs on, not a custom.** Swidden
farming means land is a circuit, not a plot, so fencing it would be pointless, and
`jokamiehenoikeus` arrives as practice that later statutes can only acknowledge.
The `puukko` is on the belt because in forest you survive by whittling, mending
and butchering yourself. And the language belongs to neither neighbour: fifteen
cases, no future tense, and everything a people who wrote nothing down still sang.

**`plot_03` Sisällissota ja Suomenlinna 1918.** Six weeks after independence the
country splits. The war is short; what follows is not. The winner takes tens of
thousands of prisoners without a plan to feed them, and **the sea fortress built
to keep an enemy out is filled with its own** — some eight thousand on the islands,
hundreds dead of hunger and disease, and nationwide more died in the camps than in
the fighting. Then the hardest part: nobody agrees what to call it. War of
liberation to the winners, class war to the losers, and for decades the country
uses two words for one event depending on which side a grandfather was on. Not
until the late 1990s does the state start counting the dead by name from both
sides. **Before that, reconciliation was silence** — not forgiveness, a decision
not to speak.

**`plot_05` Korvike ja kahvitauko, 1939-1954.** Coffee is rationed in autumn 1939
and then simply runs out. Roasted rye, barley, chicory, dandelion root — it does
not taste like coffee and nobody pretends otherwise, yet it is brewed in the same
pot and offered to the guest in the same way. **What is missing is the bean, not
the practice.** Rationing outlives the war by nine years; coffee is the last
rationed commodity in the country, freed in 1954. Then Finland goes on to drink
more of it per head than anywhere on earth, and negotiates the break into
collective agreements — a thing that elsewhere is a habit and here is in writing.

**`plot_99` Raja ja onnellisin maa.** Eight consecutive years measured the world's
happiest country, over the same years that 1,340 km of border stopped being
settled. NATO in April 2023 after eighty years of the opposite, decided without a
tearing row, in the familiar manner. What the ranking measures is trust — that a
wallet comes back, that the office does what it says — **not joy; the name is a
translation error**. `Aino` would call the ranking boasting. `Eero` reads it in
English, because he reads everything in English. Sauna culture goes on the UNESCO
list in 2020: the building once raised because you would not survive the winter
without it is now heritage. And on the coast `Solveig` still answers in Swedish,
in a bilingualism that works exactly as long as somebody still has both.
