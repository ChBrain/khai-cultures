**`order_what_to_do_next.md` refused a score, and the refusal was right about the risk and
wrong about the remedy.** It said weighting _"out of order"_ against _"no plot_00"_ means
inventing numbers, and that such numbers "drift towards whatever the last person wanted to
work on". Two facts overturned it.

## The ladder could not say "a country before a state"

Depth in the tree is not a yes-or-no fault, and a ladder of yes-or-no facts cannot hold one.
What that cost, in this week's own history: `de_thuringia` and then `de_saxony_anhalt` were
authored on consecutive working days while `sweden` waited behind them, because all three
were `wrong` and the tiebreak was hole width.

## And severity did not add

One blocking finding and a line that is backwards with three placeholder chapters and nine
findings answered to the same rung. Measured when the score landed: **`us_south_carolina`
owes seven things at once and sat fifth**, behind cultures owing one thing each.

```
Next: us_south_carolina  192 points  (wrong)
  40 backwards + 25 1 blocking + 30 no origin + 30 no present + 20 hollow
  + 18 3 uncast + 10 unmigrated + 12 level 2 + 6 hole 261y + 1 span 261y
```

## What the reshuffle actually is

|                          |                                |
| ------------------------ | ------------------------------ |
| cultures owing something | 264                            |
| move more than 50 places | **80**                         |
| median move              | 31 places                      |
| head, ladder → score     | `sweden` → `us_south_carolina` |

Mean points per rung come out at 132 `wrong`, 121 `unbracketed`, 60 `thin`, 37 `unmigrated`
— the rung order surviving as a tendency rather than a gate, which is what the weights were
set to inherit.

## What answers the drift, since a promise would not

- **The arithmetic prints with every row.** A weight is argued against a named culture, never
  in the abstract, which is what the original objection actually feared.
- **The weights are pinned by a test.** A number cannot move without that line moving in the
  same diff. Probed by changing `disordered` from 40 to 95: the test fails.
- **Level comes from `geo.json`, never from the id**, because ids lie — `el_salvador` and
  `dr_congo` read sub-national and are countries.

## The guarantee that was traded away

Under the ladder anything false ranked above anything merely missing. Under points, five
missing things outrank one false one; the bands overlap, `wrong` reaching down to 58 and
`unbracketed` up to 184. That is the intended trade, recorded in the order — and it is one
line to take back (`rung * 1000`) if it ever reads wrong.

Five tests added, 13 in the section, and the existing totality property is unchanged: the
comparator is points then id, so no two distinct cultures tie.
