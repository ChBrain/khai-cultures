**The East Asian tongues.** Step 3 for hong_kong, four tongues - and, like the NATO
pass, it is mostly a step about somebody else.

Hong Kong could not move for three reasons: it held `en_hk` and `yue_hk`, and it
reached `zh_cn` inside **china**, a culture in no group being run and one nobody has
opened. So step 3 here is not hong_kong's own tongues. **Two of the four belong to
china.**

## Scope: four, and why the fourth

`en_hk`, `yue_hk` and `zh_cn` are what hong_kong needs. **`lzh` is not**, and it was
moved anyway - the opposite call from `yue` at #641, on the opposite measurement.

`yue` was left behind there because moving it would have freed nothing. `lzh` frees a
great deal: **thirteen files across five cultures link it - china, japan, south_korea,
taiwan and vietnam - and four of those five are outside the culture that held it.** A
written language that four countries read and none of them spoke had been sitting
inside one country's directory. That is the care point in its clearest form in this
whole session, and leaving it there while moving its neighbour would have been a
choice to walk past it.

## Two anchors under a country's name, and a flag

- **`yue_hk` → `yue`.** The **sixth** instance of the `sv_fi` shape, and the plainest.
  The file arrived as `yue_hk` and was **already titled "Cantonese"**, with its declared
  name the bare 廣東話. Its Has is Cantonese entire: the entering tone and stop codas
  kept from Middle Chinese, **so that Tang and Song verse still scans when read aloud
  in it**; six to nine tones; the whole set of sentence-final particles by which one
  character at the end of a clause fixes the speaker's attitude. **Not one of those is
  Hong Kong's as against Guangzhou's.** Only the casual mixing-in of English leans to
  the city, and that is a register note, not a grammar. **No deferral is opened** - the
  title was already the bare name.
- **`zh_cn` → `zh`.** Its own title reads "the Chinese Language" and its declared name
  is the bare 汉语, and there was no `zh/` for it to be a variety inside.

**And one substantive flag, recorded rather than quietly fixed.** `zh`'s Has names four
tones - 阴平, 阳平, 上声, 去声 - which is **Standard Mandarin's system and not
Chinese-wide**, and the `yue` file moved in this same pass contradicts it in its own
Has with six to nine. Either this file is the anchor and that paragraph is a variety's
material sitting inside it, or the file is `cmn` under a country's name. **It is filed
as the anchor because that is what it calls itself twice over**, and the paragraph is
left for a reader who can judge it. Repairing it here would have edited a file china
owns, and china is exactly the culture the care point protects.

`en_hk` was the only straightforward one: `en` already had its anchor, `en_hk` is a
genuine variety of it, and it slots into `en/` with no rename and no question.

## One title left wrong on purpose

Only one, and it is china's: `zh` reads **"the Chinese Language"** where this package
writes the bare name, corrected when china migrates. `yue`, `lzh` and `en_hk` all
arrived correctly titled and open nothing. **Six deferrals stand.**

## What it unblocked

**`hong_kong` is ready**, which was the point. But the queue moved further than that:

| culture     | blockers before | blockers after          |
| ----------- | --------------- | ----------------------- |
| hong_kong   | 3               | **0, ready**            |
| canada      | 2               | **1: hong_kong itself** |
| china       | 3               | **1: japan's `ja`**     |
| japan       | 2               | **1: its own tongue**   |
| south_korea | 2               | **1: its own tongue**   |
| vietnam     | 2               | **1: its own tongue**   |
| taiwan      | 3               | 2                       |

**`lzh` was the only cross-culture reach japan, south_korea and vietnam had.** Each of
the three is now held by exactly one thing, its own tongue. And **china, which held
both of the files this pass took, now holds nothing of its own at all** - it is one
move from ready, and the move is japan's.

Thirty files were relinked across eight cultures - canada, china, hong_kong, japan,
singapore, south_korea, taiwan and vietnam - every one reaching an anchor by specifier
now.

**Tongues 60 languages → 63, 127 varieties → 131, 0.60.0 → 0.63.0**, ranges synced by
the build. Cultures unchanged at 319; **no culture authored.**
