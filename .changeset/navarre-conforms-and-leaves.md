Navarre conforms and leaves the umbrella.

The fifth run of the sub-national recipe, and the first where the tongue that
moved describes two territories at once.

**The tongue.** `position_language_es_es_nc.md` moves byte for byte into
`packages/khai-cultures-tongues/es/`, the thirteenth regional Spanish variety
there. Only navarre linked it, so step 3 and step 4 rode together under the
limit set at #608. It arrives correctly titled. Its own body says the thing the
culture is about: in the northern valleys it carries the sung intonation of
euskera underneath while the words stay Castilian, and in the Ribera it does
not. One variety file, two halves of a territory.

**The culture is authored.** The house's nesting sentence is appended to
`position_culture_cultura_navarra.md` — _Es una manera de ser español, no un
segundo pasaporte_ — which is what makes navarre a culture with a line of its
own rather than a folder under Spain.

**Two plots, because one sentence costs two.** Clearing the four dead entries
touched the plots and woke plot-zero, so both ends were written:

- `plot_00_la_puerta.md` — the western Pyrenees hold the only comfortable land
  door into Iberia. A kingdom exists to hold that door, and to hold it must
  hold two unlike halves, mountain and Ribera, which is why its law is pacto and
  not decreto. _Este territorio sabe pactar con quien viene de fuera desde hace
  mil años. Con quien vive dentro le cuesta bastante más._
- `plot_99_la_ciudad_de_todos.md` — San Fermín 2016 and what followed: the 2018
  Audiencia de Navarra verdict, the protests (the largest of them in Pamplona
  itself), the 2019 Supreme Court reclassification, the 2022 consent law, and
  the 2020–21 cancellations, the first since the Civil War. It closes back onto
  plot_00: _el que guarda un paso no es el que lo cierra, es el que responde de
  lo que ocurre dentro._

**Then the mechanics.** `navarre` renames to `es_navarre`, migrates to
`@chbrain/khai-cultures-es-navarre`, and the four inbound links to the tongue
are rewritten to the specifier.

Counts, run rather than assumed: sub-national cultures not yet conforming
55 → 54; production packages 92 → 93; umbrella cultures 227 → 226; tongues
varieties 137 → 138 with the language count unchanged. `es_navarre: 0 dead,
0 waived, 14 in Company`.

Two observations reported, not repaired, because the move carries the file
unchanged. The tongue's body is written without accents throughout, as
`es_es_ct` is — a second instance of that fault. And the play still declares
`language: es` for a culture whose north speaks a language isolate, which makes
five of eleven Spanish subdivisions carrying the finding opened at #640.
