**`npm run next` had `de_thuringia` at the head of the queue on the "wrong" rung**,
for a plot line that ran backwards. It ran backwards because there was barely a line:
three plots, numbered 1775, 1521, 1990, with no origin and no present.

## The fault the counters could not see

`AGENTS.md` gives the test: take the words the play uses about itself and ask which appear
in a plot.

| what the play says it is                  | where it says it | had a plot |
| ----------------------------------------- | ---------------- | ---------- |
| "eine echte Thüringer Rostbratwurst"      | Arc, first line  | **no**     |
| "die Glaskunst des Thüringer Waldes"      | Arc              | **no**     |
| "die weltberühmte Optikindustrie Jenas"   | Arc              | **no**     |
| "der bewaldete Freistaat, das grüne Herz" | Name             | **no**     |
| "Dürreschäden und Borkenkäferplagen"      | Stakes           | **no**     |

Five for five. Weimar and the Wartburg had a plot each; everything else this culture
claims to be went unstaged, and every counter was green.

## Eleven plots, forwards, origin to present

|               | before | after               |
| ------------- | ------ | ------------------- |
| plots         | 3      | **11**              |
| runs forwards | no     | **yes**             |
| `plot_00`     | absent | **der Kamm**        |
| `plot_99`     | absent | **die Fichte geht** |
| state Cues    | 1 of 3 | **2 of 11**         |
| places        | 2      | **6**               |

The bratwurst enters as what it actually is in the record: **one groschen for sausage
casings**, in the Arnstadt convent provost's account of 20 January 1404, found in 2000.
The glass gets the 1597 glassworks and the 1847 bauble. The optics get Abbe's 1896 statute,
the 122 paragraphs with which he disinherited himself. The forest gets `plot_00`, the ridge
that decides what can be made here at all, and `plot_99`, the spruce going off that same
ridge - 38% of the state's forest area, planted where it does not belong.

**Buchenwald is staged**, on the Human's explicit call. Its Cue is the SS clearing the beech
wood and leaving one oak because Goethe was said to have sat under it; it burned on
24 August 1944. That is one of the two state Cues, and it is not decoration.

The Bauhaus is the one plot not traceable to the play's own words. It earns its place
because Weimar produced the school and the Thüringer Landtag drove it out inside six years,
which is the argument about inheritance that Goethe and the Ettersberg are also having.

## What else was wrong underneath

Four places did not exist for plots that had nowhere to stand: the Rennsteig, Lauscha,
Weimar, the Ettersberg. And two units pointed at the wrong plot outright - the Lauschaer
Weihnachtskugel was staged in **Luther's Bible translation**, which is what a coverage
counter accepts and a reader does not. Every such pointer now names the plot that holds it.

## Sourced, including where the source is a legend

Every date was checked against a public source and recorded in `REFERENCES.md`, which also
answers the Cue-subject question per plot so the next hand does not have to ask. Where the
record is trade legend rather than document - the poor glassblower who could not afford
apples in 1847 - the row and the plot both say so.
