Sorbian enters the house, both of them, and neither as a German dialect. Germany has
four constitutionally recognised minority languages and the house finished all
sixteen Länder without one of them — the same shape as Bavaria's fourth tribe, a
people the play never acknowledged. Upper and Lower Sorbian are West Slavic, their
nearest relatives are Czech and Polish, and nothing in either file is stated as a
departure from German, because that frame is what turns a minority language into a
footnote to a majority one. What Upper Sorbian carries is the dual, a whole number
category for exactly two, on the noun and the verb both; what it loses is stranger
and is the office fact no other tongue here has, that it has no monolingual speaker
at all, so a conversation can switch at any moment — and the dual is the first thing
younger speakers drop, so the tongue loses first what most distinguishes it. Lower
Sorbian is written as its own language and not as a variant, and checked mechanically
for exactly that fault: five of five Lower markers and none of five Upper, against
four of five Upper in the other file, sharing 30% of tokens. Its Loses is its own —
transmission in the family largely broke, so most speakers learned it in a nursery
rather than from a mother, and every word is learned rather than found. Both flagged
for native review. Upper Sorbian passes khai's own gate as Upper Sorbian; Lower
Sorbian is exempt, because franc has an hsb model and no dsb model, so it reads as
its sibling and there is nothing honest to gate it against. Ten languages, so 0.8.0
becomes 0.10.0.
