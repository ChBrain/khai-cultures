**The scaffold asks what acts, at the only moment it is free.**

`tests/new_culture.mjs` now prints the Cue-subject test from
`order_the_passport.md` against the plot titles it has just been handed, and
writes it into the new culture's `REFERENCES.md` as a table with one row per
plot: the plot, the subject of its Cue, and whether that subject is the state or
one of its instruments.

The moment matters. Three cultures were staged as passports in a week, eighteen
plots with sixteen of them a state acting, and all eighteen titles were chosen in
a single invocation of this script before a word of prose existed. Changing a
title then costs nothing; changing it after costs a restaging.

It also corrects the stub itself. The `REFERENCES.md` the scaffold wrote asked
only the coverage tell - do the words the play uses about itself appear in a plot

- which is the tell that cannot find a passport, because a play whose Arc, Name
  and Stakes are themselves constitutional passes it. That sentence was in the stub,
  so every culture written from the scaffold inherited it, and all three passports
  quoted it back in their own REFERENCES while failing. The stub now carries both
  questions and says what each one can and cannot see.

Printed and asked, never counted: the order records two counters measured against
the whole house that both fail. Ships nothing.
