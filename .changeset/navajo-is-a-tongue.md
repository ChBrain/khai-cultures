Navajo joins the tongues as `nv`, the sixty-eighth language. The house was
staging a plot about this language's military value while holding no file for it.

**Found while preparing Arizona to leave.** `arizona` carries `plot_02`, _"The
Code Talkers Contribution 1942"_, whose whole subject is that Navajo could not be
read by the other side. It casts Yazhi, a weaver in Monument Valley, whose mother
tongue the play names as Navajo — **linking nowhere**. Her second language,
Arizonan English, linked correctly.

That is the fourth unlinked mother tongue, after `es_aragon` at #664,
`es_asturias` at #666 and `es_balearic_islands` at #669, and it is the sharpest
of the four: the other three staged a culture whose language was absent, and this
one stages _the usefulness of a language it did not hold_.

**What it holds.** An Athabaskan grammar whose nearest relatives are two and
three thousand miles north, in Alaska and northwestern Canada. The verb is the
sentence — one word carrying who acts, who is acted on, the mode, the aspect and
a classifier that changes what the verb does with its participants. And the thing
anyone who has met this language remembers: **classificatory stems.** There is no
general verb for handing something over. The stem says what kind of object it is —
solid and roundish, slender and flexible, slender and rigid, flat and flexible, a
mushy mass, a set of separate items, a load carried, an open container with
contents — about a dozen such sets, and the shape of the thing is inside the verb
and cannot be left out. Plus a ranking of beings that constrains which
participant may stand as agent, so some sentences have to be recast rather than
forced; high and low tone; and a large aspect system in place of a simple tense.

**What it loses**, stated plainly because the cause is documented rather than
mysterious: children were punished for speaking it in government and mission
boarding schools, and the generation that learned that lesson did not pass it on.
It is still the most widely spoken Indigenous language north of Mexico, on the
order of a hundred and seventy thousand speakers, and a minority of Navajo
children now learn it first. _A language can be the largest of its kind on a
continent and still be going._

And the overlap it cannot make comfortable: **in the same years the United States
was suppressing this language in its schools, it valued it as a code no enemy
could read.** Both were happening, to the same families. The usual telling also
flattens the work — the men who did it built a substitution code _inside_ the
language, because the language alone was not a cipher.

**Written in English, and that is a deliberate exception worth naming.** Every
other anchor in this package is written in the language it describes — Latin in
Latin, Asturian in Asturian, Aragonese in Aragonese. The one prior exception,
`bfi`, is in English because British Sign Language has no written form. Navajo
has one. The reason here is different: **I cannot write three hundred words of
Diné bizaad to the standard the other anchors are held to**, and a badly written
anchor in a language with a living revitalisation movement is a harm, not a
placeholder. So it is in English, carries `review: "native"` in its own
frontmatter, and its provenance note says that review here means _rewriting it in
the language_, not checking it. I would rather that be visible than discovered.

Counts, run rather than assumed: tongues languages 67 → **68**, varieties 149 →

every dependent with no content change in any of them. `tongues: 150 varieties, 0
finding(s)`; flat files unchanged at 46.

**Yazhi is not wired here, deliberately.** I wired her first and the house
refused, in three walls at once: `company-coverage`, `subnational-conformance`
and `links`. Touching one sentence in one persona makes Arizona an **authored**
culture, and an authored culture must come out at zero, come out conforming, and
have every address resolve — which Arizona cannot, because it still owes a dead
entry, two blocking findings and the stale REFERENCES table found at #674. That
is exactly the finding #674 recorded about Arizona, and I walked into it anyway.

So the tongue stands alone, which is what this package is for: `tongues_standalone`
exists to say a variety is held by the speech community that speaks it and not by
a culture, and `eo` already sits here linked by nobody. Yazhi gets her link inside
Arizona's own change, where the full recipe is being paid regardless.
