**One line of the Basel-Landschaft prose pass kept; the branch is otherwise
rejected.**

`piece_chienbaese` said the heat "bedroht" the windows of the half-timbered
houses. It now says the paint on the doors blisters. That is what the Chienbäse
actually does, and it is checkable rather than atmospheric.

Nothing else in the pass is kept. It rewrote 70 lines across 13 files and every
one of them was an intensifier: `Feuerbrauch` became `Feuerrausch`, a `hübsches
Törli` became a `wehrhaftes Törli`, `das politische Herz` became `das pulsierende,
sture politische Herz`. Two changes go further than voice:

- `position_culture_baselbieter_kultur` acquired the line that the canton
  painfully lacks intellectual and cultural lighthouses, and that its people are
  rooted "tief im Schlamm der eigenen Scholle". A culture-position is where this
  house says what a culture is; it does not belittle its subject.
- The same file added that the Baselbiet wants recognition as a "moralisch
  überlegener" partner, which the original did not claim and which is not the
  canton's own account of itself.

Elsewhere a doom narrative was applied uniformly: the cherry trees are the last
guardians of a dying landscape, the hills are eroding, the recreation is
desperate, the identity is crumbling and kept alive artificially. Some of that is
arguable and none of it was in the culture before.

The branch also could not be rebased: main had meanwhile conformed this package
to the canon in `Basel-Landschaft: conform the package to the canon`, and the
pass rewrites the pre-conformance text of the same files. Main's version is the
correct base, so this change is written onto it directly rather than replayed.
