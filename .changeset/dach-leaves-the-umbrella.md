**DACH becomes a package.** The first group in the house to leave the umbrella,
and the proof that the path works.

A group pulls on its members and its members pull on the tongues, so the
dependency graph sets the migration order: a group cannot become a package until
every culture it casts already is one. Measured across the nineteen, DACH is the
only one that qualifies today - Germany, Austria and Switzerland are all
packages, which is also why it was the only group whose Company could be written
with package specifiers in the first place. `the_americas` waits on all
thirty-five of its members.

`packages/khai-cultures-dach` declares `khai.group` rather than `khai.production`,
so it is a play that ships like any other and is not counted in the minor. Its
dependencies are what it actually casts: the three members, the tongues, and the
language engine. The umbrella depends on it and no longer contains it.

It declares no bump, which is the precedent every culture migration set: France
left the umbrella on an empty changeset and so did the fifty after it. A
migration moves files between boxes and adds no content to the house, and the
count - the only thing that moves this house's version - does not change.

The registry still lists nineteen groups, DACH's entry now naming its own
package with the empty path that says the package root IS the unit, and the
culture count does not move.
