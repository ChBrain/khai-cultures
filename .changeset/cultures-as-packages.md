Record the package split as an order and a design of record. The house is registered in the khai house registry as material other productions draw on, and drawing on it is all or nothing: a play set in Bavaria would depend on all 290 cultures to cast one, which is why no house has ever tried. The plan splits the house into a package per culture, group and crossing, one for the tongues, and an umbrella that keeps the culture count, and it commits to executing slow and steady rather than in bulk. The method is a fan-in walk: the 320 language varieties migrate narrowest first, about ten cultures to a pull request, each batch paying its link rewrites, its dead Company entries, its clean identifier and its parent nesting together, so the house is never red and the ratchet never needs an exemption. Names are frozen before any publish and identifiers are cleaned rather than patched. Two tiers protect it, the touch ratchet and a publish gate that lets nothing dirty leave. The blocker is a canon question in khai and is named as such. Governance only; the house ships nothing.
