something that is not so".** Three plots covered 941 years with an **893-year

## The hole was the writing, not the record

The same shape San Marino turned out to have. Between the voyaging canoes and
the 1893 overthrow this house had nothing, and Hawaiian tradition and the record
have a great deal:

| plot                         | what it is                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| ---------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **02** Pāʻao, c. 1200        | Tradition says a priest from Kahiki brought the walled heiau, the prostrating kapu and human sacrifice. **Archaeology does not find him**, and many Hawaiians hold him real and hold him responsible. Both facts are staged; only one is a fact about the twelfth century. |
| **03** Māʻilikūkahi, c. 1480 | Oʻahu cut into ahupuaʻa running ridge to reef, the first code of laws, human sacrifice forbidden, and a makaʻāinana's right to leave an unjust chief - four centuries before any constitution, enforced by exit.                                                           |
| **04** the ships, 1778       | Iron and firearms arrive wanted, disease arrives and cannot be refused, and the islands become a place other nations have interests in.                                                                                                                                    |
| **05** Kamehameha, 1795      | The unification the muskets bought, and the reason a single palace in a single city is enough in 1893.                                                                                                                                                                     |
| **06** the ʻAi Noa, 1819     | The kapu broken from the inside by the court that most benefited from it - **months before the first missionaries landed**, not by them.                                                                                                                                   |
| **07** the Māhele, 1848      | Land made private to make the kingdom harder to annex. Two years to file; the makaʻāinana end with under one percent.                                                                                                                                                      |

**1778 was added for coherence, not for the arithmetic.** It only cuts the hole
from 315 to 298 years. But plot 05 referenced cannon and European gunners and
plot 06 referenced epidemics, and the line staged neither.

## The origin is a relationship, not a founding

`plot_00_haloa.md`. Papa and Wākea bear the islands; a stillborn child is buried
and the first kalo grows from the grave; the next child lives and everyone
descends from him. **The islands are the eldest sibling, kalo the next, people
the youngest.** In a society that ordered everything by seniority that is a legal
position, not sentiment: the land outranks the people in it, and the obligation
runs both ways. Every later plot is a question about that relationship - who may
divide the land, who may sell it, and whether a people can be dispossessed of a
relative.

## The present is the one count that rose

`plot_99_olelo_hawaii.md`. About **1,500** Hawaiian speakers when the first
Pūnana Leo nest opened in the mid-1980s; around **30,000** at varying fluency
now. Official language of the state since the 1978 convention, immersion
schooling from nest to doctorate, and a 2019 state supreme court holding that
the constitution _requires_ reasonable access to it. A record, not a forecast -
and the plot says plainly that a revival measured in speakers does not answer a
question about land.

## The nesting sentence, which is the blocking finding

Every other US state carries the same line: _"It is a way of being American, not
a second passport."_ **Hawaii cannot carry it flat**, because this house's own
`position_hawaiian_sovereignty` says the kingdom was overthrown with US marines.
The rule checks for the link, not the wording, so the link is there and the
sentence is true:

> It is a way of being American that the United States has itself conceded was
> not freely entered: Public Law 103-150 apologises for the 1893 overthrow and
> records that the Hawaiian people _"never directly relinquished to the United
> States their claims to their inherent sovereignty..."_. The nesting is the fact
> of the fiftieth state; the consent underneath it is the thing still argued, and
> the apology settles no claim.

That last clause is in the resolution itself, which states that nothing in it
serves as a settlement of any claim.

## What this does not do, and what it cannot claim

**It does not migrate the culture out of the umbrella.** That is rung 4 and a
pure move; it authors nothing and belongs in its own pull request.

**The 298-year hole between 1480 and 1778 is left standing**, and named. ʻUmi-a-Līloa
and the Hawaiʻi-island chiefly line sit in it. It is the honest edge of what was
researched here rather than a claim the period was quiet.

**Nobody in this house is a Native Hawaiian reader.** This stages a living culture
with an active sovereignty claim, a contested origin figure, and a disputed
population collapse. The sources are cited below; the judgement of whether the
staging is right is not something a wall can hold, and this asks for that reading.
