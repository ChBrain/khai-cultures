Arizona conforms and leaves the umbrella. The first of the forty-eight, and the
one that could not be done in halves.

**Why it was forced.** At #674 Arizona was the single culture excluded from the
free rename, because its `REFERENCES.md` pointed at two plot files whose numbers
had been swapped at some point and `links` fired the moment a rename made it a
charged unit. The repair is two words in a table — and editing that file makes
Arizona an **authored** culture, which must then come out at zero, come out
conforming, and have every address resolve. So the cheapest possible touch cost
the full recipe, and this is it, paid in one change:

- the stale table repaired, the two rows pointing at the files that exist

  American state variety there, steps 3 and 4 riding together under #608

**Two plots.**

- `plot_00_the_canals_dug_twice.md` — a man in the 1860s is clearing silt out of
  a ditch in the Salt River valley, and the ditch is already there. He did not
  survey it. Between roughly the middle of the first millennium and the fifteenth
  century the Hohokam built the largest irrigation system in pre-contact North
  America: hundreds of miles of hand-dug main canals carrying river water far
  enough from the river to feed tens of thousands, and it worked for eight
  hundred years. Then floods tore out the headworks, a long drought came, and -
  quietly, underneath - salt: irrigate a desert for centuries and the water
  leaves its minerals behind every time it evaporates, until the ground the
  canals made productive stops producing. _The system that made the place
  habitable is also what made it, in the end, uninhabitable._ Four hundred years
  later the next arrivals did not solve it again; they cleared the old alignments
  and used them, and named the settlement Phoenix, after the bird that rises from
  the ashes of the thing that burned. **The city's name is a confession that they
  knew what they were building on.** Meanwhile the state's greatest river is
  unreachable, a mile down a trench cut through two billion years of rock.
- `plot_99_the_order_of_the_cuts.md` — in 1968 the Central Arizona Project was
  authorised, the third and largest attempt at the problem in plot 0, and the
  price of authorisation was priority: California would not agree otherwise, so
  this state's canal water was made junior. **The canal exists because Arizona
  agreed in writing to be the first to lose it.** The shortage arrived on
  schedule — the first federal declaration on the Colorado in August 2021, with
  Arizona taking the deepest cuts of any state, exactly as the bargain specified.
  Then the ground: in June 2023 the state stopped certifying new subdivisions in
  parts of the Phoenix area because the hundred-year assured supply could no
  longer be shown. Then the heat: thirty-one consecutive days at or above a
  hundred and ten in 2023, and the county's worst year for heat deaths. Copper
  went the other way, becoming strategic rather than merely valuable — though the
  smelting left, so concentrate crosses an ocean and comes back as metal, and the
  next great ore body sits under a site the San Carlos Apache hold sacred. And
  the hardest fact: around a third of homes on the Navajo Nation have no running
  water, in a nation bordered by the river, and in June 2023 the Supreme Court
  held the United States owes it none under the treaty of 1868.

The two ends meet on what kind of limit each was. _The Hohokam were stopped by
salt and flood - physical facts that arrived without anyone's signature. This
time the limit is a clause._ There is no flood to rebuild after; the water is
going where the paper says it goes. The third attempt at the same canals is the
first to fail by contract rather than by nature.

**One thing recorded rather than fixed.** The provenance note for `en_us_az` says
plainly what these state varieties are: a file like `es_es_nc` or `ca_ib`
describes a grammar or a phonology that differs from its anchor, while this one
describes _"the Western cadence of a safety briefing on a mine floor"_ — a claim
about an occupation more than about a tongue. It moves unaltered, because a move
is not the place to relitigate it, and the question stays open in
`order_voice_from_inside.md`. The walk ahead will repeat this forty-seven more
times, so the shape is on the record once.
