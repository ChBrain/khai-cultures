**Corsica was a passport; restaged.**

Six plots, five of them a state acting on a people, become nine with three of
them so. The line now opens on the land: malaria held the plain, so the island
lived at a thousand metres in two villages a year; Genoa ordered chestnuts
planted and the interior ate flour for three centuries; and with no justice
within reach the villages ran their own, with the voceru - the women's lament
that names the deed and delivers the charge in public - as its instrument. Paoli,
Ponte Novu and the emigration are kept. 1991 loses its own plot and joins 2003
and 2021 in plot_99, where the three court defeats belong together.

The plot that reorganises everything is the DDT. Five years of spraying, 1944 to
1949, turned two thousand years of uninhabitable plain into the island's best
farmland, and from 1962 it was settled by repatriates from Algeria. That is what
makes Aleria legible: the 1975 siege was at a repatriate's cellar on that plain.
The first staging had the siege without the plain - the politics of the event and
not its ground.

The Stakes no longer ask about belonging to France. They ask what becomes of a
country where every improvement has arrived for somebody else: the plain became
healthy and went to others, the chestnut flour came back as a luxury bought by
people who never lived on it, and the language is not forbidden, it is worth
nothing, so giving it up costs nothing. One grievance with four instances, and
the constitutional version could see only the last.
