The United States gets a plot line and leaves the umbrella. It is the keystone,
and taking it first is a correction to the plan I proposed last turn.

**Why usa and not Arizona.** I said Arizona should go first, being forced anyway
by its broken REFERENCES table. Measuring instead of assuming showed that would
have stranded it. The nesting sentence every sub-national culture must carry
links `../usa/position_culture_american_way.md` — a relative path into the
umbrella — and **a packaged unit carries no `../`**. So all forty-eight states
could conform where they stood and not one could migrate until `usa` itself was a
package. Authoring Arizona first would have produced a finished culture that
could not leave, and the wall would only have said so at migration time, after
the plots were written. Same shape as the `ar_ma` blocker found at #668, one
level up.

**What it unblocks.** Two things, and the second was already whole and waiting:

- the forty-eight states, which can now conform _and_ migrate in one change each
- the **NATO** group, whose thirty-two members were all specifiers except `usa`,
  a relative reach into the umbrella. `nato: 0 finding(s)` before and after; what
  changed is that it is now packageable.

Fifteen inbound links were rewritten to the specifier, including the nesting
sentences of the three states that already conform.

**Two plots.** Six dead entries, the most of any culture in this walk.

- `plot_00_three_months_behind.md` — a farmer in a doorway with a letter in his
  hand carrying an instruction from three thousand miles away about something
  that must be settled this week. Under sail the crossing runs six to ten weeks
  each way, so an order is a season old on arrival and a question takes half a
  year to answer. A settlement that must decide today decides: the town meeting
  that sets the rates, the congregation that picks its minister, the assembly
  that votes the governor's money and thereby owns him. _None of that begins as a
  philosophy. It is what distance does to administration, practised until it is
  ordinary._ The theory arrives later to explain a habit older than anyone alive,
  which is why an empire trying to govern closely is not introducing a rule but
  breaking one. And behind the coast is the reason anyone stays: the largest body
  of first-rate farmland on earth, drained by the largest system of navigable
  inland water in the world, so grain grown a thousand miles inland floats to the
  sea for almost nothing — _the frontier is not an idea about character, it is a
  river system with soil on top of it._ But it was not empty, and why it looked
  empty is the darkest thing in this origin: Old World disease travelled inland
  ahead of settlement, so much of what newcomers walked into had been emptied
  within living memory of the survivors. Fields already cleared, villages gone.
  What was written down as wilderness was in many places a graveyard.
- `plot_99_the_count.md` — the decade the country lost its agreement about
  arithmetic, and the arithmetic was about the plainest things a nation keeps.
  More than a million of its own dead from one disease, the highest recorded
  national toll anywhere. Life expectancy falling two years running, the sharpest
  fall in a century, since partly recovered. Overdose deaths past a hundred
  thousand in a year and then, in 2024, falling sharply for the first time. Each
  number disputed while it was being taken. In 2020 the largest wave of protest
  in the country's history; in January 2021 the counting of an election
  interrupted in the building where it was being certified; in June 2022 a right
  recognised for half a century removed, so that something lawful in one state
  became a felony across a line on a map.

The two ends meet on the mechanism rather than on a verdict. _A country that
learned to decide locally because no instruction could reach it in time is
designed to give different answers in different places._ It has always done that
about policy and mostly held. What is new is how many questions now answered
differently are not policy at all — what happened, how many, who won. A
federation can carry fifty policies; whether it can carry fifty accounts of the
same week is the open question, and nothing in the design was written with that
in mind.

Written from inside and without a side: the events are recorded, the disputes are
named as disputes, and no party is adjudicated — the posture this house took for
Cyprus, Ceuta and Melilla.
