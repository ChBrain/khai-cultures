## The hole was the writing, not the record

San Marino's shape, and Hawaii's. Six plots now stand between 1278 and 1950:

| plot                                    | what it is                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| --------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **02** Consell de la Terra, 1419        | The bishop authorises the men of the valleys to meet and elect their own each year. **The assembly is not created, it is legalised** - and whoever sits does so as a head of house, so for four and a half centuries this chamber represents properties, not citizens. |
| **03** the Treaty of the Pyrenees, 1659 | The border is fixed along the range and the Pyrenean valleys are parcelled out between France and Spain - **all save this one.** What changes is what the co-princes _are_: two feudal lords become the heads of two sovereign states with a frontier between them.    |
| **04** the Manual Digest, 1748          | Fiter i Rossell gathers the customs and privileges into one volume. A country with no army arms itself with the only wall it has: papers both lords must acknowledge because they granted them.                                                                        |
| **05** Napoleon restores it, 1806       | The Revolution had abolished the French lordship as feudal. **The Andorrans asked for it back** - because what protects them is not either lord but that there are two.                                                                                                |
| **06** the Nova Reforma, 1866           | The vote reaches beyond the heads of houses. To move the internal division of power, someone had to go and fetch the outside authority that guaranteed it.                                                                                                             |
| **07** the Revolution of 1933           | The Joves Andorrans demand suffrage, France occupies the country, and the first elections under universal _male_ suffrage follow. Women wait another thirty-seven years.                                                                                               |

**1659 was added on a verified fact and not to pad the arithmetic**: every other
Pyrenean valley was divided by that treaty and this one was not, which is the
moment the co-principality stops being a medieval inheritance and becomes a
geopolitical position. It also cut the hole from 329 to 240 years.

## The origin is the grass

`plot_00_els_prats_comunals.md`. High pasture that **cannot be divided without
ceasing to yield** - too small, too short a season, too easily overgrazed - so the
valleys hold it in common and take it in turn. Not a founding: nobody arrives,
nobody is crowned. But to share what belongs to everyone you must decide together,
so when two foreign lords turn up in 1278 they meet communities that already know
how to deliberate and already have something concrete to defend. **They negotiate
as owners, not as subjects.**

It casts `place_madriu` and `position_pirineu`, which were the two orphans.

## The present is a price, not a lord

`plot_99_l_habitatge.md`. On **16 May 2026, about 1,500 people** marched over
housing - **the largest demonstration in the country's history** - in a country of
some 90,000 residents that takes close to nine million visitors a year. Bank
secrecy ended in January 2017; the automatic-exchange protocol has been in force
since 1 January 2026; the EU association agreement got the Council's approval on
16 July 2026 and goes to a referendum here.

The arc closes on itself: it began with a scarce thing the valleys knew how to
share, and ends with a scarce thing they do not. **A country that managed to share
the grass for eight centuries has not managed to share the flats in seventy years.**
Recorded, not forecast - the referendum's outcome is not predicted.
