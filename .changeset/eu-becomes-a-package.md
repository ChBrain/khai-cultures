**The EU group leaves the umbrella. Step 5, and the walk is finished.**

Eight members were run one at a time under the working order - italy, slovenia,
croatia, bulgaria, malta, cyprus, romania, greece - and with greece out the group
carried no `../../cultures/` link at all. This change moves the group itself.

## It owed nothing

Every previous group in this walk was measured before it moved, and three of the four
completed ones **each owed four findings of the same shape**. That pattern is why the
measurement is done first rather than after.

The `eu` group broke it. Measured before the move:

```
group_coverage      eu: 0 finding(s)
staging             0 finding(s)
Triggers            6 bold entries, one per plot, each a link
plot line           plot_00 .. plot_04, plot_99 - origin and present both present
umbrella links      none
```

**The first group in this walk that owed nothing at all** - which is not luck either.
`plot_00_the_licence_to_teach_anywhere.md` and `plot_99_the_returners.md` were written
when the group was staged, and `persona_the_returner` and `position_the_licence_to_move`
were built with them. The debt the other groups carried was the debt of groups that had
been assembled as lists of members before the origin-and-present rule existed.

## The move

```
eu -> @chbrain/khai-cultures-eu  (group)
  members  27, from austria to sweden
  depends  28 culture packages plus @chbrain/khai-engine-language
  rewrite  0 own link file(s), 0 inbound
```

**Zero rewrites in either direction.** Nothing had to be repointed, because the eight
member migrations had already repointed everything as they went - each of them rewrote
this group's prose on its way out. The last step of a five-step order costing no edits
is what the order was for.

The 28 declared dependencies are the reachability claim made honest, on the #603
precedent: the play reaches 27 members by specifier and `plot_02_the_widening.md`
reaches the United Kingdom, which left the Union and did not leave the history.

is either the three Balkan members, which are ordinary work, or the United States debt,
which is not.
