**Casting by consequence gets a name, a home, and the one gate it can have.**

A contemporary persona cannot stand in a scene that predates them, so the
coverage ratchet's usual answer is a waiver. The house had a second answer it
had never written down: a plot may cast a persona in its **Tension** as what the
event produced. Ohio's Harper works out of the Cleveland the collapse left
behind; Pennsylvania's John is a retired steelworker in a city with no steel.

The test was already written too — in the waiver of the culture that _refused_
the device. `us_virginia/coverage-waivers.json` names both precedents and rules
Sasha out as **"invention rather than consequence"**. That is the rule, fully
formed, sitting where no writer would find it, which is why two agents staging
Austria and Switzerland independently re-derived it last week.

So it is placed by rung rather than written down once more:

- `management/orders/order_casting_by_consequence.md` carries the doctrine — the
  Virginia test, the Tension placement, and the two things the device must never
  do: stand in for a plot that ought to be written (Croatia's Ivan, Serbia's
  Stefan), or name an atmosphere instead of a mechanism.
- `CLAUDE.md` points at it and does not restate it.
- The script takes only what it can decide.

**Measured, not asserted:** of 941 personas cast anywhere in the house, exactly
three are cast only in a Tension — Harper, John, and Austria's Lukas. Practice
and placement turn out to be the same fact. Switzerland's Gian and Marco are
_not_ among them: that branch put them in the `Action` and the `Stage` and undid
it with `obwohl beide diesen Tag selbst nicht erlebt haben`. The prose is honest,
the placement is not, and the order says so.

**The gate:** a waiver for something a plot now casts is an error. That is the
drift this device produces — the plot gains the persona and the waiver stays
behind, stating the opposite about the same entry; both DACH branches had to
remove theirs by hand. Verified by committing a superseded waiver and watching
the gate exit 1.

**The queue:** `--consequences` lists the Tension-only castings so a person can
read each against the Virginia test. Reported, never gated, for the same reason
the tongue-drift queue is: no script can tell a real thread from a written one.
