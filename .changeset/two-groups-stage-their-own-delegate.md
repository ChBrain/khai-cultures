**The two debts the group ratchet named, paid.**

`dach` carried `persona_die_delegierte` and `the_four_nations` carried
`persona_the_secretary`, both declared in the play's Company and staged in no
plot. They are the same persona twice over — the one seat a country holds in a
body only the group has — and each was written for its group's `plot_99` and
never put in it.

`order_the_group_ratchet` said each was one pull request. They are one here,
because they are one fault in two places, the same lane, and the new wall now
charges both groups independently and both come out at zero.

**`dach` / `plot_99` drei Standards, eine Schreibung.** The plot argues that the
writing community is now exactly what it was at the start and nothing more, while
everything spoken drifts. The Delegierte is what carries that: she sits for the
country that needs the Rat _least_ — Switzerland dropped the ß long ago and made
the orthography its own anyway — and reads every submission line by line because
one of them once had a ß in it. **That is where the office actually is.** A shared
orthography does not hold because it is useful; for Switzerland it barely is. It
holds because someone who does not need it makes the journey and keeps the chair
occupied. She never says _wir_ for the three. She says _die drei_.

**`the_four_nations` / `plot_99` Four Teams, Four Rules.** The plot's Cue now
opens twice: four sides before four crowds, and four people in a room with no
crowd at all voting on the laws of the game for the whole world. The oldest part
of the arrangement is the part nobody sees — four of the eight votes on the laws
board, one each, so nothing passes without them. The Secretary reads every
proposal for what it does to a Saturday at home before anything else, then votes
with the other three far more often than any of them expected. She knows the
arrangement could not be justified from first principles and defends it anyway,
because the alternative offered has never once been a better rulebook, only one
seat instead of four.

Both are archetypes, and both REFERENCES now say so, with the point that makes
them the group's and not a member's: **the office exists only where the members
sit down together**, so it is staged in the group's own plots and never in a
member's.
