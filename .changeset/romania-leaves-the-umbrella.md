## plot_00: Drumul oilor

`plot_01` is Trajan defeating Decebalus, which is the culture's own passport: Rome
gave us our Latinity. It is true, and it is not the origin. **The Latin island was
not made by the conquest. It was made by what happened after Rome left.**

In autumn a shepherd brings the flock down from the Carpathians along the road his
father walked, a track nobody drew on any map. He cannot read, and there is nothing
to read: **nothing has ever been written in what he speaks.** What he speaks is Latin,
worn down by centuries of mouths.

Rome held Dacia for less than two centuries and withdrew under Aurelian around 271.
After that everyone crosses this ground - Goths, Huns, Gepids, Avars, Slavs, Bulgars,
Magyars, Pechenegs, Cumans, Tatars, later the Ottomans. **The plain is a road. The
mountain is not.** The Carpathians curve through the middle of the country like a
spine, and the people with the flocks move along and across them, up in spring and
down in autumn, over ground the armies go round.

And for nearly a thousand years the language is not written at all. **The first
surviving sentence in Romanian is a letter of 1521**, from Neacșu of Câmpulung, a
merchant sending word about the Turks. Until then the liturgy is Slavonic and the
chanceries write Slavonic, and the Latin language lives only in mouths.

What kept it was not a state, not a book and not a church in its own tongue. It was
sheep, a mountain and a song. **`Doina` is exactly that**: one person, free rhythm, no
accompaniment - the shepherd at the flock, the woman at work, the one left behind -
and the whole of `dor` fits inside it.

Much later, when the nation wakes and claims its place, the proof gets cast in bronze:
`lupoaica`, the Capitoline she-wolf on a plinth in town squares, given by Rome or by
Italy. **A people that lived a thousand years without writing a line proves its
descent with a statue, because a statue is the kind of proof the others accept.**

The Tension: a people that keeps its language with no state, no book and no church in
its own tongue learns that what holds is not what is written but what is sung and
carried mouth to mouth. **That is where the patience comes from, and `a răzbi`. And it
is the shadow: whoever lived a thousand years without paper learns that what is
written hardly matters, only how you manage - and learns to ask others for the proof,
to put the she-wolf in the square and say again and again where he comes from instead
of saying what he is now. A people that must always prove where it came from ends up
finding it hard to say where it is.**

## The middle: Brâncuși into plot_02

The seventh dead element was `Brâncuși`, who died in 1957 and belongs to neither end.
A new numbered plot for him would have meant renumbering the chronology and every
cross-reference, and `order_plot_zero.md` fixed the origin at `00` precisely to avoid
paying that. **He is folded into `plot_02` instead, on the belgium precedent** - one
paragraph and one Stage line, in the plot that already holds the century.

It is the right place for a reason beyond convenience. `plot_02` is Eminescu taking
the language to its summit. Brâncuși does to form what Eminescu did to language: a
boy from a Gorj village who refuses Rodin's studio and makes the twentieth century's
sculpture out of peasant woodcarving, then raises the Endless Column at Târgu Jiu for
the war dead - **a Gorj porch pillar repeated to the sky.** Both of them prove the
same thing with different material: **the village world that carried the language for
a thousand years is not a museum, it is stock to work in.**

## plot_99: Hora de august

In August, at the village feast, the fiddlers start and the people take hands.
`Maria` steps into the circle between her children, home for two weeks from Italy and
Spain. **The circle is bigger than on any other day of the year. In ten days it will
be smaller than it has ever been.**

Romania joined the Union in 2007, and since then more people have left than in any
other stretch of its history. The 2021 census counts about nineteen million, over a
million fewer than ten years earlier and nearly a quarter fewer than at the start of
the nineties. Millions of Romanians now live in Italy and Spain - **which is to say
among precisely the Latin cousins the nation has always called its own.**

**And there is the turn nobody had reckoned on.** Latinity was for centuries the proof
that this people belongs to the West, and the proof held: `Andrei` picks up Italian
almost without learning it, it is that close - and because it is that close, it
swallows his own language without his noticing. **Only, being Latin among Latins did
not make him one of the household. It made him useful.**

At home the villages empty and the schools close. `Hora`, in which everyone has a
place and nobody dances alone, is now formed once a year, when the ones who left come
back. And `ia` went out into the world too, and did better than the people: painted by
Matisse, copied by the fashion houses - once, in 2017, so closely that the craftsmen
of Bihor answered with a label of their own - and inscribed in the world's heritage.
**The blouse is worn at festivals from Turin to Madrid, by women who come home in
August.**

The Tension: the people that held a Latin language for a thousand years without paper,
in order to prove at last that it belongs to the West, moved to the West - and the
proof held. It was not a story: the Italian really is intelligible, the cousins really
are cousins. **Only, being related is not the same as being at home.** `A răzbi` is the
name of a plan and not of a victory: the resourcefulness that carried this people for
a thousand years on the sheep road now carries it onto building sites and into other
people's houses. The decade does not decide whether Romania becomes a country of
return and not only of departure. It says only this: **`ia` is worn today in more
cities than ever, `hora` is formed in fewer and fewer, and both belong to the same
people.**
