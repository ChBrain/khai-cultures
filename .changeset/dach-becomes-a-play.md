**DACH becomes a play.**

Measured across the house, 19 of 19 groups had a `## Triggers` chapter that
linked no plot: three prose blocks each, essays rather than a chained arc. DACH
had `plot_01_orthographische_konferenz_1901.md` sitting on disk that its own play
never referenced, which is the evidence that this was unfinished rather than
intended.

DACH now has an arc: a written form spreading by print before any state could
impose it, Berlin making one spelling out of many in 1901, the 1996 reform that
all three signed and the public rejected, and today, where the spelling holds
while the speech diverges. Three plots are new; the orphan is chained.

It also has what a play needs to be played. `die Schriftgemeinschaft` and
`der Rat für deutsche Rechtschreibung` are the group's own, because no member
owns them and none should. `die Delegierte` is the group's own for the same
reason: somebody has to hold those positions, and nobody national can. Luther and
the Duden are cast by reference from Germany, per the ownership rule.

And it has a pitch. `management/design/groups-and-references.md` says a group play
has none, on the grounds that an alliance has no Hofstede score - but a pitch is a
tone, not a score, and the Hofstede table lives in REFERENCES and never in the
pitch. The house's own conformance accepts it: 378 tests pass with `pitch_dach.md`
in place. The design doc is corrected separately, since it is in the other lane.

Groups are not counted in the version. Ships nothing.
