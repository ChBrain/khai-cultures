Rebuild Virginia around the people who turn river, labor, money, music, and infrastructure back into authorship.

The new plot line begins with living tribal continuity instead of English arrival, follows tobacco as the maker of Virginia's racial labor order, gives the 1861 Fort Monroe action to Frank Baker, Shepard Mallory, and James Townsend, stages Maggie Walker's newspaper and bank as usable Black institutions, and restores Black exchange to the Bristol Sessions before reaching the lived data-center present.

Five grounded personas and their places, practices, positions, and pieces replace the former docent-and-contractor pairing. The result keeps the Chesapeake plan and the current infrastructure record while retiring the generic colonial objects, presidential dynasty, and invented Yorktown-to-shipbuilding line.
