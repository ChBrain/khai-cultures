**Corsican joins the tongues.**

The house held no Corsican and no culture could bring it in: the island has no
play yet, so there was nothing to migrate it out of. It is written here instead,
which 21 of the entries in `provenance.json` already do.

Every chapter is the office and not the map. What it keeps that Tuscan let go,
the article from ILLU and the `ghj`/`chj` no neighbour writes the same way, and
polynomy - two great varieties with no correct one between them, a norm never
written because nobody wanted it. What its grammar makes a speaker mark: the
initial consonant that changes with whatever stands before it, so no word can be
said alone, and in Pumonti the cacuminal that puts the speaker's own end of the
island into every sentence. What it cannot say: the administrative and technical
vocabulary, which arrives in French unaltered because Corsican was never an
official language for a single day, and speech from nowhere, since a tongue
without a norm offers nobody a place to hide. And how it sorts the world, by
holding one's own parlata as one among several equally right.

Flagged `review: native`. The prose is not written by a Corsican speaker and
wants one before anyone treats it as settled.

`build.mjs --write` took the package to 0.28.0 and 73 varieties and updated the
dependency range in the 50 packages that declare it. The branch is in the
`culture` lane, not `governance`: `khai-guard` owns `packages/khai-cultures-tongues/**`
to `culture`, which it said when the first attempt was named by hand - the very
thing `CLAUDE.md` tells this house not to do.
