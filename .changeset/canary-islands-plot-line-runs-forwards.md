The Canary Islands' plot line runs forwards. First tick of the ratchet added in
#675, and it is the change that wall was written for.

**Mine to fix.** `es_canary_islands` shipped at #670 with `plot_01` dated 1496
and `plot_02` dated 1492 — Columbus's provisioning stop at Gran Canaria and La
Gomera numbered _after_ the conquest it preceded by four years. I wrote a plot_00
and a plot_99 around that middle and never read the middle as a sequence. The
wall refuses #670 when replayed against it, which is why it exists, and this is
the first thing it asked for.

**Renumber, not redate.** The wall reports the pair and cannot choose the remedy;
this one is a reading and the reading is clear. Columbus provisioned in 1492
while the conquest was still running; Tenerife fell in 1496 and ended it. Both
dates are right and the numbering was wrong, so the numbers move and no prose
about either event was touched.

`plot_01_conquest.md` → `plot_02_conquest.md`, and `plot_02_columbus.md` →
`plot_01_columbus.md`. Git records both as pure renames. Every content change in
this diff is a link or a label: the two Triggers entries relabelled and put in
their new order, the two REFERENCES rows swapped to match the files, four inbound
links from a piece, a place and a position retargeted, and the registry rebuilt.
The plots' own declared names and headings are untouched, because a plot is named
for what it is and not for where it sits.
