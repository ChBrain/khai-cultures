`npm run skeleton -- --shapes` asks the overlap question of the whole house at
once, and corrects a pluralisation ("2 processs").

The single-culture report asks whether two cultures sharing a shape is a
coincidence. Asked of three hundred at once it is a different question, because
the answer is already visible: the house carries 44 distinct shapes, 213 of 300
cultures (71%) share the three commonest, and four of the six Company dimensions
barely vary at all - 2 pieces in 95% of cultures, 2 places in 93%, 2 processes in
89%, 1 plan in 92%.

The 57 identical sub-national cultures the overlap report first surfaced are the
second-largest cluster, not the anomaly. Nothing is swept here and nothing is
gated: the finding is printed, with its question, and the answer is content work
that belongs to the cultures as they are touched.
