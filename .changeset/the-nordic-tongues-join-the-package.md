**Five Nordic tongues leave their countries, and the fifth mislabelled anchor
turns up.**

Making the Nordics a package needs its five members to be packages, which needs
each of them to hold no tongue. This is that tier, and it clears all five at once.

| tongue  | held by | goes to                         | kind                            |
| ------- | ------- | ------------------------------- | ------------------------------- |
| `is`    | iceland | `is/position_language_is.md`    | anchor, straight move           |
| `fi`    | finland | `fi/position_language_fi.md`    | anchor, straight move           |
| `no`    | norway  | `no/position_language_no.md`    | anchor, already named right     |
| `sv_se` | sweden  | `sv/position_language_sv.md`    | **anchor mislabelled, renamed** |
| `sv_fi` | finland | `sv/position_language_sv_fi.md` | variety, hangs beneath it       |

**`sv_se` is the fifth**, after `da`, `sco`, `ga` and `ar`. Titled _the Swedish
Language_, declared **svenskan**, and every chapter language-wide: definiteness
suffixed onto the word so `bilen` and `huset` carry it in the body rather than in
front; the tonal accent that separates two identical spellings by melody alone,
`anden` the bird against `anden` the spirit, which most Germanic languages lack
entirely; and free compounding. Nothing in it is Swedish-of-Sweden as against
Swedish-of-Finland. No `sv_se` is written — the `ga` shape rather than the `da`
one, because there **is** a sibling and it hangs beneath.

**`no` is the exception in a walk of corrections**: already the anchor and already
correctly named. Declared **målstriden**, and its Has is the thing itself, that
Norwegian has no normed spoken standard at all, only two written norms, with
dialect permitted in school, in court and on the state broadcaster. A tongue whose
defining fact is that it refuses to have a standard could never have been filed
under a country without the file arguing with itself, and it never was.

**`fi` is the only tongue in this walk that is not Indo-European**, and it is the
reason the Nordics group keeps having to account for Finland: four of the five are
in one family and this one is in another, so every shared arrangement in that
group was built across the fact rather than on top of it.

**One found thin and moved anyway.** `sv_fi` opens `Vad svenskan låter säga` and
then lists the suffixed article, compounding and the verb-second clause — the
anchor's material, now sitting directly above it, with not one word distinguishing
Finland Swedish from Sweden Swedish. Same fault as `en_gb_eng`. What it should hold
is unusually clean, being the anchor's list minus one item: **Finland Swedish has
no tonal accent.** The pitch distinction the anchor names as one of Swedish's
rarities is simply absent, and it is the first thing a Swede notices. The rewrite
follows inside the package.

**Everything moved byte for byte, deliberately.** The five members carry
thirty-eight dead Company elements between them — denmark 6, sweden 9, norway 7,
finland 8, iceland 8 — and one edited byte during a tongue move stops the change
counting as a rename, authors the culture and charges it for all of them. That
trap was hit on #562 and again on #576; not a third time.

`build.mjs --write` took the package to **0.40.0 at 40 languages and 94
varieties** and updated the declared range in every dependant. Thirty files
relinked across the umbrella.

**What it unblocks.** All five members now report `READY` with no blockers,
including denmark, which held no tongue of its own and was held instead by two
cross-culture links in `persona_inger.md` that became specifiers when Norwegian
and Swedish moved. The group still refuses, correctly, and says why: _5 of 5
members are still under the umbrella._ Migrations next, then the group.
