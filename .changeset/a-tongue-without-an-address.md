**A persona carries the depth. A tongue is what is wired.** Nauru ships the line
"Nauruan is the miner's mother tongue", wires three channels of depth to it, and holds no
`position_language_na.md` anywhere — its only language position is `en_nr`, English as
spoken there. The miner's own tongue has no address.

`order_a_tongue_without_an_address.md` records the rule and, first, disproves the instinct
behind it. This is not a problem of dead tongues needing their own production type: `la`,
`cu` and `lzh` are already held and wired like any other, and none has a native speaker.
Nor does a tongue carry a flag. Latin's state is read off the personas — 27
`reading_followed`, 17 `speaking_carried`, 5 `reading_deciphered`, and **zero**
`speaking_mother_tongue` across 75 wirings. That distribution _is_ the deadness, and
nobody declared it. A tongue with a surviving written form is wireable whatever became of
its speakers; the house is text and is not making sounds.

What actually separates the cases is whether the house can see the hole:

| tier | shape                                           | found by     | today |
| ---- | ----------------------------------------------- | ------------ | ----- |
| 1    | depth carried, file wires no tongue             | **a rule**   | 2     |
| 2    | wires some tongues, names another in bare prose | **reading**  | Punic |
| 3    | never named at all                              | **a person** | Sámi  |

Only tier 1 can be a ratchet, and it starts at two. Tier 2 is `persona_septimius.md`,
which wires Latin and Arabic then carries Punic at `speaking_carried` with the tongue
named only in Arabic prose — a file-level rule passes it. Tier 3 is Sápmi, which appears
nowhere in this house despite Sweden, Norway and Finland each shipping a culture over
ground the Sámi live on; the whole-house search returns one mention of Lapland, in Spain's
REFERENCES, about population density.

Also recorded so it is not raised twice: the 57 bare language codes referenced outside the
tongues house and not held by it are **not faults**. Every one resolves locally, which is
the convention — `position_language_he.md` lives in `israel`.
