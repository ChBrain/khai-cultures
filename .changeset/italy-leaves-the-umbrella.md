**The EU, member one of eight.** Italy out — and it is the first culture in this
entire walk that needed **no content work at all**.

Every other member of every group so far arrived owing something: dead Company
elements with no scene to stand in, a plot line missing its origin, missing its
present, or both. The measure before the move is always the same three numbers,
and Italy's read **0 dead Company, 8 plots, both `plot_00` and `plot_99`
present**.

That is not luck. Italy was authored at `.changeset/italy.md` under
`order_the_passport.md` — the pass that found four words the Arc used about
itself and staged none of them: _campanile_, _famiglia_, _la televisione_, _Nord
e Sud_. `plot_00_italia_prima_dello_stato.md` answers the origin with a name
rather than an empire; `plot_99_il_paese_che_invecchia.md` records the first
peacetime population decline since unification. The passport pass left nothing
behind, so the migration is only a move.

## The move

`italy -> @chbrain/khai-cultures-italy`, **0 own link files and 4 inbound**.

The four inbound are all group prose, and they say what Italy is to this walk:
two in `eu` (the Company list in `play_eu.md`, and the founders sentence plus the
Stage of `plot_01_rome_1957.md`) and two in `nato` (`play_nato.md` and
`plot_01_founding_1949.md`). Italy is a founder of both — the third large
founder of the one and one of the twelve of the other — so the rewrite reaches
into two groups at once, which no other member of this walk has done.

Its `it` tongue was already the anchor, and #630 moved the rest of the EU's, so
nothing was blocked at step 3.
