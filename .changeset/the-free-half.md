Forty-seven of the forty-eight American sub-national ids take their parent's
code, in one relink-only change. This is the open Target in
`management/orders/order_stage_the_united_states.md`: **take the free half before
any of them migrates.**

**Why now.** The id is what the npm name follows, and an npm name is permanent.
That order established by probe at #649 that a bare directory rename is
content-identical — a relink, authoring nothing — and that it is free _only until
these cultures become packages_. Once one ships as `@chbrain/khai-cultures-...`
the name is spent. The Spanish eleven were renamed one at a time inside their own
changes because each was being authored anyway; the American forty-eight have no
such change to ride, so the free half is taken once, for all of them at once.

**What the diff is.** 1,008 files changed and **exactly one has any content in
it**: `registry.json`, rebuilt. Every other path is a pure rename with zero
insertions and zero deletions. No prose was touched and no link was rewritten —
there were no inbound `../<name>/` links to any of them.

**Why forty-seven and not forty-eight.** `arizona` is left where it is, and the
reason is worth recording because it is the house working exactly as designed.

Its `REFERENCES.md` points at `plot_02_central_arizona_project.md` and
`plot_03_code_talkers.md`; the files on disk are `plot_02_code_talkers.md` and
`plot_03_central_arizona_project.md`. The two were swapped at some point and the
table kept the old numbering. That fault is old and it was invisible, because
`links` charges only the units a change writes and nothing had written Arizona in
a long time. Renaming it made it a charged unit and the wall fired at once.

The repair is two words in a table. But editing that file makes Arizona an
**authored** culture, and then `company-coverage` and `subnational-conformance`
both fire in turn: it must come out at zero, and it must come out conforming.
Both were tried and both refused, correctly. So Arizona cannot take the free half
at all — the cheapest possible touch on it costs the full recipe: the nesting
sentence, its dead cleared, and the two plots that sentence wakes.

Arizona therefore gets its own change, with its rename inside it, like any other
culture that is being written. Its broken table is reported here and repaired
there, not patched in passing.
