Record the defining question as an order. Coverage is a counter and cannot tell whether a play is true: Germany passed every counter while carrying its whole memory apparatus and staging no plot between 1871 and 1949. The order directs that whenever a culture is touched, the question is asked with it, what defines this culture and does the play stage it, and it mandates the asking and never the answer. It is a dialogue and not a rule by design, because the house already answers differently culture by culture and is right to: Poland, Spain and Russia name their catastrophe while Japan, Cambodia, Rwanda, China and Austria fold theirs into recovery or omit it, and some of those are evasions while others are the truth of the culture. Governance only; the house ships nothing.
