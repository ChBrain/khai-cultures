**A mother tongue here is what the language engine says it is, and the engine says
neither acquisition nor dominance.** `@chbrain/khai-engine-language` ships the grip,
so it owns the word. `process_speaking_mother_tongue.md` is _"Initiated by: Not
need, not task, not the room. **Collapse.**"_ - the width _"surfaces when the
position can no longer manage itself. It was running under the other languages the
whole time."_ `process_thinking_mother_tongue.md` is _"the language the persona
returns to when effort relaxes"_, and its Direction is four words: _"The language is
the persona."_

**The criterion is what is LEFT.** Not when it was learned, not how much of the day
it carries.

`united_kingdom/persona_aisha.md` is what that costs. English is _"already on her
lip before she reaches for it"_ and carries _"every public hour of her day"_, and
the file wires it **`worn`**; her parents' language she was _"never schooled in"_
and keeps _"for the family table"_. What she is left with when she can no longer
manage is a read of her own prose, not a policy this house fixes once for three
hundred and nineteen cultures.

## The rule that follows, which needs no heuristic

Nearly everyone is left with one language when they can no longer manage. So a
Projection that names grips and never names that one has left out the fact the
other three rules stand on - and that is decidable by looking, with no distance
test and no guessing.

**It is a default, and weaker than a draft of this said.** That draft called two
mother tongues _"possible and very unlikely"_, and the research does not support
it: about half the world is functionally bilingual and the majority of those
bilinguals are native speakers of _both_ their languages, the native bilingual
communities of South America, Africa and Asia may have no monolingual norm at all,
and Canada's census recognises more than one mother tongue outright. Across 319
world cultures, two is not an exception.

So the rule asks only that the file **say**: name the language, or say this persona
holds two, or say they hold none. What it refuses is silence. A persona who really
holds two has no single language their file must be written in, so the rule above
already declines them.

**And the fork this order carried as open is retired, by the engine, which answers
neither side of it.** "Mother tongue" and "dominant language" are different things

- L1 is order of acquisition, mother tongue and heritage name family and culture,
  dominance names institutions and present proficiency - and they diverge in exactly
  one population, heritage speakers, whose dominance shifts with schooling. The house
  had this queued as the Human's call, with the rest of the campaign waiting on it.
  It is not a policy question: the engine's criterion is collapse, which is a
  reader's call about one person. The campaign no longer waits.

## Two mother tongues, admitted, in two senses

**Nothing in the engine caps the floor at one language.** Every sentence that sounds
like it does is a _floor_ claim and not a _count_ claim: _"There is no width below
this. There is no language the persona can think in that sits closer"_ says nothing
sits lower, and two can be level. The law slides a persona under pressure toward
_"the width it holds most firmly"_, not toward a unique language. This is now a
contract in `house.test.mjs` rather than a side effect of rule 3 declining
multi-tongue links.

**And per channel, which is sharper and is the ordinary Swiss shape.** The engine's
card: _"One channel may sit at a different width than another in the same language;
widths do not move together"_, and its playwright instructions: _"Do not collapse it
to one fluency label; the channels are separate loops."_ So a persona can stand at
the floor in one language for speaking and at the floor in another for writing -
two mother tongues with no bilingual childhood at all.
`ch_st_gallen/persona_der_abt.md` speaks Alemannic at the floor and writes _"in
Dokumenten auf Latein"_ at `polished`.

## A narrowing that was measured and refused

A draft keyed the written-language rule on the **writing** channel alone, reasoning
that a persona file is a written object. Measured: it dropped the rule's reach from
**476 personas to 46** and cleared **three of the four** open findings - der Abt and
the Stickereibaron, who write at `polished`, and `switzerland/persona_tell.md`, who
carries no writing grip at all and whose prose says so, _"er ist in der Mundart
geboren und gehört im Geschriebenen keinem"_.

**Which channel decides a house FILE is not the engine's question.** The engine
describes a persona using language, not a repository describing a persona. A
tenfold silent narrowing bought with an inference the engine does not make was not
taken, and all four findings stand.

What the channel does change is what a finding **asks for**, so it now rides along
in the message: der Abt's fix is not Alemannic prose alone, because his Projection
wires a writing grip with no tongue under it.

## Two shapes, and only one can be cleared by wiring

**The dominant language is there under another grip.** Aisha's case: the prose is
already right and the grip is wrong.

**The dominant language is one this house cannot hold.**
`libya/persona_septimius.md` grew up in Punic - _"لغة أهله ومدينته"_, carried as one
carries the language of home - which is extinct and unheld, so his file stands in
Arabic, a language that reached that coast centuries after he died. No wiring
clears that and writing him in Punic is not available. It is the same shape as
Bijago, and as a BC plot in `order_a_plot_line_runs_forwards.md`: a thing the rule
wants that the world does not supply.

Both new tests were probed by removing what they assert, and both fail without it.
