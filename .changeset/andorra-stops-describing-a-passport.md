**Andorra shipped this morning with seven of eleven Cues a state acting.**
`order_the_passport.md` calls that a passport, and its test is one line: name the
subject of each Cue and count how many are a state or its instrument.

|            | before      | after       |
| ---------- | ----------- | ----------- |
| state Cues | **7 of 11** | **2 of 11** |

## The events did not change. What acts first did

The order is explicit that the fix is not proportion - _"the expected fix was
proportion: too many state plots, add cultural ones. That is not what happened in
any of the three."_ No plot was added, removed or redated. Five Cues were rewritten
from the material side:

| plot        | acted before                        | acts now                                                                                                                                 |
| ----------- | ----------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **02** 1419 | the bishop of Urgell **authorises** | the houses, already meeting for generations to divide summer pasture and water turns. 1419 is the year that custom gets a signature      |
| **03** 1659 | **France and Spain sign**           | a new line, and thirty-three Cerdanya villages waking up in France. Llívia does not: the text ceded _"pobles"_ and Llívia was a **vila** |
| **04** 1748 | the **General Council commissions** | law living scattered - parchments per parish archive, copies that disagree, the rest in the memory of old men who are dying              |
| **05** 1806 | **Napoleon restores**               | some Andorrans walking to Paris to ask for a lord back                                                                                   |
| **06** 1866 | the **co-prince decrees**           | Andorrans who pay, who work, and who sit nowhere                                                                                         |

## What that changed, which is the order's actual finding

_"Putting the land first changed what the political plots meant."_ It does here.
With the bishop acting in 1419 the line reads as a sequence of concessions
granted from above. With the houses acting, the same eleven plots read as **a
community that already administers something, repeatedly having to fetch an
outside signature for what it already does** - and 1866 and 1933 stop being two
unrelated suffrage reforms and become the second and third time that happens.

That reading is not available while the state is the only subject, which is the
order's point: _"a plot line with the state as its only actor has only one question
available to it: what was done to us, and on what terms."_

## Two state Cues are kept on purpose

`plot_01` the pareatges and `plot_09` the Constitution. The order is equally clear
the other way - _"politics is not the fault... a line with no state plots at all
would be as wrong as one with nothing else"_ - and the house sits at roughly three
in six to nine. These two are the line's real constitutional moments and they stay
constitutional.

## Llívia is one clause, not a digression

It is a neighbour, not Andorra. It earns its place in a single sentence because it
shows the mechanism: a word in a document decided which side of a new border a
place woke up on, and Andorra survived the same treaty by being a category the text
had no slot for. If it needed a paragraph it would be padding.

No plots added, no dates moved, no links changed; the Action chapters lost the
sentences the new Cues now carry. Rung stays 2, hole stays 240y.
