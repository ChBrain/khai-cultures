## Two arrived already correctly named

`eu` and `ca` are the **first anchors in this whole walk that did not need renaming**,
and the reason is worth recording: neither is anybody's national standard, so nobody
had filed them under a country code in the first place. Every anchor before them —
`ar` from `ar_sa`, `ro` from `ro_ro`, `pt` from `pt_pt`, `nl` from `nl_nl`, `el` from
`el_gr` - had been a country's copy of a language.

**Basque** could not be a variety of anything: an ergative-absolutive case marking
the doer apart from the undergoer, where the Spanish and French on either side of it
name both the same way; and one auxiliary verb carrying subject, object and indirect
object at once. **It is an isolate, related to nothing else on the continent** - which
is the fact the Iberia group has to keep accounting for: the peninsula's oldest
tongue is the one that came from nowhere else.

**Catalan** carries the periphrastic past, _vaig fer_, replacing the simple preterite
in ordinary speech in a way no neighbouring Romance language shares, and the weak
pronouns _hi_ and _en_ that stand in for a place and a quantity where Spanish repeats
the noun. `andorra` holds `ca_ad` and `valencian_community` holds `ca_vc`; both are
genuine varieties that now have an anchor to hang from.

## And one is the `sv_fi` fault whole

`ar_dz` is labelled **Algerian Arabic**, and read against the `ar` anchor it is not
Algerian at all:

| chapter | what it says                                 | whose material |
| ------- | -------------------------------------------- | -------------- |
| Has     | the triliteral root deriving dozens of words | the anchor's   |
| Orders  | obligatory gender, and the dual              | the anchor's   |
| Loses   | the short vowels that are not written        | the anchor's   |
| Drives  | hearing the root echo in every new word      | the anchor's   |

**There is no French borrowing, no Berber substrate, no Maghrebi vowel reduction —
nothing that would tell the Arabic of Algiers from the Arabic of anywhere.** Where
`pt_br` had one borrowed chapter out of four, this is all four.

It is **not repaired here**, and that is the care point doing its job rather than a
convenience: algeria is not a member, and rewriting it would author a culture this
walk never opened. The repair belongs to algeria's own migration - the pull request
that will author it anyway.

**Nineteen Arabic country files remain in their cultures, and this is the first one
read closely.** On this evidence at least some of the others are carrying anchor
material too, which is exactly what the `ar` note predicted when it moved.
