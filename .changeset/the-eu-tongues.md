**Step 3 for the European Union, which has quietly come within reach.**

When this walk started the `eu` group was **sixteen members under the umbrella and
eighteen tongue moves away** - the second most expensive group in the house after
`the_americas`. Four completed groups later, without anyone aiming at it, it stands
at **eight members and five tongues**, because czechia, hungary, poland, slovakia,
portugal, spain, belgium, the netherlands, luxembourg and the rest went out for other
reasons.

This takes the five. **After it, all eight remaining members plan clean.**

| tongue  | from        | becomes             | why                                 |
| ------- | ----------- | ------------------- | ----------------------------------- |
| `el_cy` | cyprus      | variety of `el`     | member's own                        |
| `tr_cy` | cyprus      | variety of `tr`     | member's own                        |
| `mt`    | malta       | **new `mt` anchor** | member's own                        |
| `es_uy` | **uruguay** | variety of `es`     | italy reaches for it                |
| `sr`    | **serbia**  | **new `sr` anchor** | slovenia _and_ croatia reach for it |

**Nothing is edited.** The pass serves cyprus and malta at once, so the #608 limit
binds; and two of the five belong to cultures that are not EU members at all.

## Three of the eight needed nothing

`greece`, `bulgaria` and `romania` were already clear - their tongues went into the
package at #620, #598 and #609 respectively, each for a different group's walk. **The
EU is being unblocked mostly by work done for other groups**, which is the strongest
evidence yet for the maximum-choices reading: an anchor placed once serves every group
that touches the language.

## The care point, and a dependency worth naming

`sr` is here because **slovenia and croatia both reach for it** - two EU members that
cannot become packages until a non-member's tongue is placeable. That is the whole
difficulty of the region compressed into a dependency graph, and the rule holds
anyway: serbia is not in this walk, so the file lands unchanged.

`es_uy` is stranger still. It is here because **italy** reaches for it - the Italian
emigration to the Río de la Plata is in italy's own material, and roughly half of
Uruguay claims Italian descent. So an EU member is blocked on a South American
variety, and uruguay, which belongs to mercosur and latin_america, has a file moved
for a group it is not in.

## Two new anchors, both already correctly named

That is now the third and fourth in this walk after `eu` and `ca`, and the pattern
holds: **a language nobody treats as a national variety never got filed under a
country code.**

**Maltese** carries one of the genuinely singular facts in European language: **the
only Semitic language written in the Latin alphabet**, holding two inheritances in
every word - the triconsonantal root building a broken plural on the Semitic side,
with Romance and English vocabulary laid over it.

**Serbian** carries seven cases, so every noun holds its own role without a
preposition doing the work.

## And a third `sv_fi`

`tr_cy` is labelled **Cypriot Turkish** and its `## Has` opens on _Türkçenin gücü_ —
agglutination, suffix upon suffix from one root. **That is pan-Turkish and it is the
`tr` anchor's material.** What is genuinely Cypriot - the Greek loanwords, the
retained q, the intonation a mainland speaker hears at once - is not in the chapter.

Recorded, not repaired: the pass serves two member cultures and an edit would charge
both. It belongs to cyprus's own migration. **That makes four found so far** - `sv_fi`,
`pt_br` in one chapter, `ar_dz` whole, and now this - which is starting to look less
like a series of accidents and more like what happens whenever a culture writes down
"its" language.
