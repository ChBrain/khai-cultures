# Nidwalden: a khai culture

Nidwalden staged as a full khai play, ISO CH-NW (a Swiss half-canton; the website derives its parent, `CH`, from the subdivision code), language de. The smallest half-canton, which has said no and paid for it: its history as the plot line, its people as the cast, the office of refusal, its places, its practices, and its defining objects, all tuned by a single pitch.

## Estate

[khai-cultures](https://github.com/ChBrain/khai-cultures): the Cultures house that holds this culture and answers for it. This culture logs the house as its Estate; a culture with no Estate is not yet a production.

## The production

- **Anchor:** [play_nidwalden.md](play_nidwalden.md), the culture itself.
- **Pitch:** [pitch_nidwalden.md](pitch_nidwalden.md), written from Switzerland's profile inflected by a community that once found out what standing by its word costs, and naming no dimension.
- **Plots (history):** the strip below the wood with the lake as its road, the 1291 charter sealed under a shared name, Winkelried at Sempach in 1386 and the monument of 1865, the Stans agreement of 1481, the assault of 9 September 1798, Pestalozzi's five months in the ruined town, the two Wellenberg referendums of 1995 and 2002, and the aircraft works and the summit since 2016.
- **Personas:** Ursi (who builds a device and does not supply its purpose) and Seppi (who makes his own decision and does not pass on the consequence).
- **Positions:** die Nidwaldner Kultur (a way of living Swiss culture, nested on the position and not on every persona) and das Nein (the office of keeping the competence that costs you something).
- **Places:** Stans (the square where three European events happened), der Bürgenstock (the mountain that is better known than the canton), der Wellenberg (the mountain that was twice not given up).
- **Processes:** die Älplerchilbi (the autumn no one is invited to).
- **Pieces:** das Winkelrieddenkmal (an assertion in bronze), der Stanser Brief (a failed orphanage's report that founded modern pedagogy), der Pilatus Porter (the machine this canton is known for, and the question who flies it).
