# Place: Nation Ford

## Taxonomy

Parent group: place of [the Catawba](play_catawba.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-cultures

## Shown

A crossing on the Catawba River, and now a name on a road and a rail line. There is nothing at the water to tell you.

## Holds

It holds the 1840 treaty, signed here, in which the Nation gave up a hundred and forty-four thousand acres for land the state undertook to buy and then largely did not.

## Offers

It offers the exact place where the paper went wrong, and the paper going wrong is what the Nation eventually won on: a treaty made with a state and not ratified federally is a treaty with a hole in it.

## Withheld

It offers no apology and no marker anybody put there on the Nation's behalf. What it is, mostly, is a ford that traffic crosses.
