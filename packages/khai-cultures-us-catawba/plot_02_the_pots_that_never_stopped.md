# Plot: The pots that never stopped

## Taxonomy

Parent group: plot of [the Catawba](play_catawba.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-cultures

## Cue

[The clay holes](place_the_clay_holes.md) in the cut banks, a walk from the settlement, giving up the same two clays they had always given up, in a generation with too few people left to keep most things going.

## Action

The women kept making pots. Through the collapse, through the leases, through the removals that took other nations west, through a century in which the nation was too small to appear in most accounts of the state at all. [Coiled and burned](process_coiling_and_burning.md), never thrown, never glazed, [rubbed with a stone](piece_the_burnishing_stone.md) until it shone. They sold them door to door in the upcountry and to the mill towns as those arrived, and the selling is not a corruption of the tradition, it is how the tradition ate. There is no decade in the record without Catawba pottery in it. There is no other unbroken thread in this culture at all.

## Tension

What came through was the thing nobody was guarding. No treaty protected the pottery, no school taught it, no agency funded it, and it is the only part of this culture that never had a gap. Everything that was formally protected has a gap in it.
