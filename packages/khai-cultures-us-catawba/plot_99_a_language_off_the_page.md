# Plot: A language off the page

## Taxonomy

Parent group: plot of [the Catawba](play_catawba.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-cultures

## Cue

A language coming back off the page, in a classroom with a whiteboard and eleven children, out of a photocopy of a transcription of a man who has been dead longer than the teacher has been alive.

## Action

[Junie](persona_junie.md) [teaches from paper](process_teaching_from_paper.md) because there is no other way: pronunciation reconstructed and argued over, grammar taught as rules the way Latin is taught, nobody in the building able to say that a sentence sounds wrong. The children learn words their grandparents did not have. Down the corridor [Ada](persona_ada.md) is teaching two apprentices to [coil and burn](process_coiling_and_burning.md), hand to hand, exactly as she was taught, with nothing written down. The two rooms are twenty feet apart and they are not doing the same kind of thing, and everybody in the building knows it.

## Stage

- `process`: [teaching from paper](process_teaching_from_paper.md)
- `persona`: [Junie](persona_junie.md)
- `persona`: [Ada](persona_ada.md)

## Tension

One tradition is being passed and the other is being reconstructed, and only the reconstruction gets called revival. Whether what comes off the page is the language or a careful model of it is a question the people doing the work ask themselves out loud, and answer by going back in on Monday.
