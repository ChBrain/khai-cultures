# Process: teaching from paper

## Taxonomy

Parent group: process of [the Catawba](play_catawba.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-cultures

## Initiated by

Somebody opens a transcription made by a linguist who is dead, of a speaker who is dead, and reads it out loud.

## Direction

The words come off the page in the order the page has them. Pronunciation is reconstructed from the recordings and from the notation, argued over, settled provisionally. Grammar gets taught as rules, the way Latin is taught, because there is no other way left to teach it: nobody can say "that sounds wrong" from the inside.

## Lever

It is the only route there is. The alternative is not a better method; the alternative is that the language stays closed.

## Echo

Children in the Nation now know Catawba words their grandparents did not. That is a real gain and it is not the same thing as the language coming back, and the people doing the work are the first to say so.
