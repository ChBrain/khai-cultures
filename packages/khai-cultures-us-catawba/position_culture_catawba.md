# Position: the Catawba way

## Taxonomy

Parent group: positions. It stands inside [the American way](@chbrain/khai-cultures-usa/position_culture_american_way.md), because the country was assembled around this river and the people on it did not move.

## Owner

- Project: khai-cultures

## Has

It has a nation with a seal, a roll and a council, and a language it cannot hold a conversation in. It has the oldest unbroken pottery tradition in the country and no potter under thirty in most years. It has more paperwork about itself than any comparable people in the Southeast, because every century somebody has come to write the Catawba down.

## Orders

It orders that the Nation decides who the Nation is. The roll is kept here, not in Washington and not by a genealogist from a university, and the argument about who belongs is an internal argument that has been running longer than the state.

## Loses

It lost the mouth. A person can be raised Catawba, live on the reservation, serve on the council and never hear the language spoken by somebody who learned it from their mother, and that has been true since 1959. It also loses its young to Charlotte, forty minutes up the road, which takes them the same way it takes everybody else's.

## Drives

It drives a patience that looks like slowness and is not. This nation was terminated in 1959 and restored in 1993 and spent the thirty-four years in between filing. A people who got their recognition back by waiting will wait for other things too.
