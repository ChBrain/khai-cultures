# Place: the clay holes

## Taxonomy

Parent group: place of [the Catawba](play_catawba.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-cultures

## Shown

Cut banks along the river, and nothing marking them. A stranger walks past. You would have to be shown.

## Holds

They hold the two clays the work needs, a pipe clay and a pan clay, which get mixed. Which hole gives which, and in what proportion, is knowledge held inside families and not written down anywhere the Nation did not choose.

## Offers

They offer the one raw material in this culture that never had to be bought, replaced or approved by anybody. Two hundred years of pots came out of these banks.

## Withheld

Most of them are not on Nation land. Reaching them means asking, and asking means the answer can be no.
