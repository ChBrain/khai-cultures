# Plot: The last mouth

## Taxonomy

Parent group: plot of [the Catawba](play_catawba.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-cultures

## Cue

The last man who can fill a linguist's notebook, in his eighties, in a chair, with the recorder already running and the questions already written down.

## Action

Samuel Taylor Blue died in 1959, and the record names him as the last fluent speaker. He had been the source for [the wordlist](piece_the_wordlist.md) for decades: texts, vocabulary, paradigms, the language taken down in pieces by people who came to get it before it went. In the same year, Congress terminated the federal relationship with the Catawba. The rolls were closed, the assets divided, the nation legally ceased to be a nation. The mouth and the standing ended inside twelve months of each other, and nobody planned that; two entirely separate processes simply finished at the same time.

## Tension

A language and a legal status both stopped, and only one of them could be filed for. The nation spent the next thirty-four years getting the status back, because that is the one with a procedure. There is no procedure for the other, and the paperwork that consumed a generation was entirely directed at the half that was recoverable.
