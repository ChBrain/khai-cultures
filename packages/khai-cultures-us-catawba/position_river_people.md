# Position: river people

## Taxonomy

Parent group: positions

## Owner

- Project: khai-cultures

## Has

A body who holds this knows the Catawba River the way other people know a street. Where it floods and how far, which bends hold fish, which cut banks give up clay and which give up nothing but sand. The name the people call themselves has the river in it.

## Orders

It orders that you take from the river and you do not sell what you take from it. Clay is dug, not bought, and a potter who buys clay is doing something else. That line has held through two centuries of every other thing being for sale.

## Loses

It loses when the river is not theirs. Most of the old clay holes sit on land the Nation does not own, and getting to them is a conversation with a landowner rather than a walk.

## Drives

It makes a person measure time in water. Floods, seasons, the century it takes a bank to move. A river person hears a thirty-four year wait for federal recognition and does not think it was especially long.
