# Piece: the wordlist

## Taxonomy

Parent group: piece of [the Catawba](play_catawba.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-cultures

## Place

In an archive, in a published volume, and now in photocopies and scans passed around the Nation.

## Load Bearing

It is what a linguist wrote down while a man who spoke Catawba sat and answered. Texts, vocabulary, paradigms. Everything anybody now knows about the language is downstream of pages like these, and the teaching in the Nation today is reading them back.

## Apparent

It looks like scholarship, filed under somebody else's name.

## Yearbook

It was made because an outsider thought the language was about to end and he was right. That is an uncomfortable debt and the Nation carries it without pretending otherwise: the record exists because somebody who was not Catawba came to take it down.
