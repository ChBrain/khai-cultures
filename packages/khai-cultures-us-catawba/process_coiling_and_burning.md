# Process: coiling and burning

## Taxonomy

Parent group: process of [the Catawba](play_catawba.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-cultures

## Initiated by

The clay comes home in a bucket and gets worked by hand until the grit is out of it.

## Direction

The pot is built up in coils, never thrown, because there is no wheel in this tradition and never was. Then it is scraped thin, dried, and rubbed with a stone until the surface takes a shine no glaze produces. Then it is burned in an open fire, not a kiln, and the fire decides the colour: the blacks and the orange mottling are what the flame did, not what the potter chose.

## Lever

It passes from hand to hand and cannot pass any other way. The rubbing in particular is a feel, not an instruction, and every potter who has tried to write it down has said so.

## Echo

It is the only thing in this culture that has no gap in it. There is no decade since the eighteenth century in which nobody was doing this.
