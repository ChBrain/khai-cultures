# Persona: Junie

## Taxonomy

The language programme at the Nation's cultural centre, a desk, a photocopier and a standing budget line.

## Owner

- Project: khai-cultures

## Projection

Thirty-four, in a classroom with eleven children and a whiteboard, running a lesson off a photocopy of a page a linguist typed in the nineteen thirties. [American English](@chbrain/khai-cultures-tongues/en/position_language_en_us.md) is [what she was raised in and argues in](@chbrain/khai-engine-language/process_speaking_mother_tongue.md). Catawba she can [read along the surface of](@chbrain/khai-engine-language/process_reading_followed.md) after ten years of it, and speaks only [as far as the room needs](@chbrain/khai-engine-language/process_speaking_borrowed.md). She belongs to [the Catawba way](position_culture_catawba.md) through the paper, which is the only door that was left open.

## Action

She [teaches from paper](process_teaching_from_paper.md), she argues with a linguist by email about a vowel, and she drives to [Wendell](persona_wendell.md)'s porch with a recorder.

## Shadow

She has started to hear her own reconstructed pronunciation as correct, and she is the only authority left who could rule on it. Nobody can tell her she is wrong, and she has stopped noticing that this is a loss rather than a promotion.

## Tell

She says "we would have said" and then stops and says it again as "they said."
