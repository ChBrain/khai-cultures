# Plot: The war party came home

## Taxonomy

Parent group: plot of [the Catawba](play_catawba.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-cultures

## Cue

Smallpox, riding home in a war party returning from the north, in a nation of a few thousand that had spent sixty years absorbing everybody else's survivors.

## Action

It went through the towns in a season. The year is 1759, and what the accounts agree on is the scale: something close to half the nation, and then a second wave a few years on. The council that came out the other side governed a few hundred people. The absorbing stopped that year and never restarted, because a town of a few hundred cannot take anybody in. Everything afterwards in this culture is a smaller number managing a larger inheritance: the same treaties, the same neighbours, the same river, and a tenth of the hands.

## Stage

- `position`: [the Catawba way](position_culture_catawba.md), which from here forward is a nation that counts itself

## Tension

The thing that made them, taking people in, was ended by the thing they had taken in from. Nobody did it to them and nobody can be blamed for it, and the loss is as complete as if somebody had. A people who understand this do not look for a villain in their own history, and the outside keeps offering them one.
