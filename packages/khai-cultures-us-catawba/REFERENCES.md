# References

This is a creative staging; sources are generic historical encyclopedias for the
Nation Ford Treaty of 1840, the land claim settled in 1993, the termination of
1959, and the Mormon mission of the eighteen-eighties. Frank G. Speck,
_Catawba Texts_ (1934), is named in the staging as the wordlist and was not read
for it.

## The defining question

**What defines Catawba, and does the play stage it?**

What defines it is that a people was assembled out of its neighbours' survivors,
kept one craft unbroken through everything that followed, and lost the mouth
while keeping the hands. The Arc says: the town that took the others in, the
smallpox that ended the taking in, the clay that never stopped, the language in
a notebook. Each clause is a plot. The coalescence is `plot_00`, the war party
is `plot_01`, the clay is `plot_02` and `plot_99`, the notebook is `plot_05` and
`plot_99`.

The Name chapter says the river is in the older name and not in the newer one.
That is `position_river_people.md`, and the river is what `plot_02` and
`plot_03` both turn on: the clay comes out of its banks and the land sold at
Nation Ford is its valley.

The Stakes ask whether a people is the thing it can still do or the thing it can
no longer say. `plot_99` is that question staged as two rooms twenty feet apart,
and it is deliberately not answered.

**Two of eight are partly the state, and both are staged from the material
thing.** In `plot_03` what moves first is a rent nobody will collect, not a
treaty: the treaty is the consequence. In `plot_06` what moves first is a flaw
sitting in twenty-seven thousand deeds, which had been sitting there for a
hundred and fifty years before any lawyer picked it up. The house runs at about
three state plots in six to nine, so this line sits at or just under the house
habit, which is the right side for a nation whose entire modern history is
litigation: the temptation here was to write eight plots about standing.

`plot_05` is the one to watch. It carries the termination of 1959 in its Action
and the Cue is a man in a chair with a recorder running, because the language
ending and the nation being dissolved are two separate things that happened to
land in the same year, and staging the Cue from Congress would have merged them.

## Not staged

- **The Catawba language as a tongue file.** There is no `chc` in
  `@chbrain/khai-cultures-tongues` and this package links none.
  `order_a_culture_without_a_map.md` allows exactly this: tier one binds what a
  culture links, not what it narrates. The language is dormant, not
  undocumented, and a dormant tongue is writable in this house, which holds
  Punic at full length. It gets written when somebody has read the sources and
  can stage a speech community. Nobody here has read _Catawba Texts_. Composing
  a tongue file out of a bibliography would repeat, in this house's own voice,
  what Red Thunder Cloud did: a man with no Catawba ancestry learned the
  language from Speck's materials and was accepted as its last speaker for
  decades. The English route is closed too, and closed by this house's own
  argument: English is for a tongue nobody **can** write, and Catawba has an
  orthography and a corpus. So the tongue is held back, and `plot_05` and
  `plot_99` narrate its absence, which is the honest staging of it.
- **The reservation.** The Catawba hold a real, federally recognised
  reservation on real acreage, and this package is still mapless. A reservation
  is not an ISO code and cannot be painted from one: painting `us_catawba`
  would mean taking York County's fill or inventing a code, and both are
  claims the house does not get to make. No geo, no fill.
- **The casino at Kings Mountain (2021).** The largest thing to happen to this
  nation's finances in a century and left out on purpose. It sits across a
  state line in North Carolina, it turns on a federal land-into-trust decision,
  and staging it would make `plot_99` a plot about a building. What the present
  looks like from inside the Nation is a classroom and a kiln, and that is what
  `plot_99` stages.
- **The Catawba deerskin map (c. 1721).** A famous object, drawn by a Catawba
  hand, now in London. It belongs with `plot_00` and is not in it, because the
  plot is about people arriving and the map is about how a later century reads
  them.
- **Removal.** The Catawba were not removed west, and the absence of a removal
  plot is the point: this nation stayed on its river and lost the land around
  it by contract. `plot_03` is the dispossession, and it is signed, not marched.

## What this package does not have

No `geo.json`, no ISO code, no `khai.languages` declaration. The prose is
English and the detector will read it as English, which is correct: the people
staged here speak English and have since before anybody now living was born.
That is the difference from `us_gullah_geechee`, which needed the declaration
because no machine can check Gullah. Here there is nothing to exempt.

What this package does not have, and cannot honestly acquire yet, is a line of
Catawba anywhere in it. A culture whose central loss is its language, written
entirely in the language that replaced it, is an uncomfortable object. It is
also an accurate one.
