# Persona: Ada

## Taxonomy

[river people](position_river_people.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-cultures

## Projection

A woman of fifty-eight with clay under her nails on a Tuesday, working at a table in her own kitchen because the studio is cold. [American English](@chbrain/khai-cultures-tongues/en/position_language_en_us.md) is the tongue she [thinks and dreams in](@chbrain/khai-engine-language/process_thinking_mother_tongue.md) and the only one her house has ever run on. The Catawba words on the sheet taped to her cupboard she [takes apart one at a time](@chbrain/khai-engine-language/process_reading_deciphered.md), and the few she says out loud she [says because the moment asks for them](@chbrain/khai-engine-language/process_speaking_borrowed.md) and no further. She belongs to [the Catawba way](position_culture_catawba.md) through the hands: she was taught at this table by the woman who owned it before her.

## Action

She [coils and burns](process_coiling_and_burning.md), she drives out to [the clay holes](place_the_clay_holes.md) twice a year and asks the landowner first, and she takes two apprentices a season whether or not they finish.

## Shadow

She thinks the pottery is the culture, and she would deny saying so. It is what she reaches for when a reporter asks, it is what she teaches, and it is the reason she has never once sat down with [the wordlist](piece_the_wordlist.md) in thirty years of meaning to.

## Tell

When somebody says a Catawba word to her she answers in English and picks something up off the table.
