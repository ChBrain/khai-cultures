# Plot: The towns that came in

## Taxonomy

Parent group: plot of [the Catawba](play_catawba.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-cultures

## Cue

The survivors of the other towns, walking in off the Piedmont with nothing, in the decades either side of 1700, when the slave raids and the pox had already gone through the upcountry twice and there was no town left small enough to be safe alone.

## Action

The river town took them. Not as guests and not as subjects: as Catawba. The Esaw, the Sugaree, the Waxhaw, the Wateree, the Shoccoree, town after town folded in, and within two generations the names of those towns were history and the people carrying them were one people with one council and one name. That is what the Catawba are. Not a tribe that survived, a tribe that was assembled out of the ones that did not, on the bank of a river that gave the assembly its name. Early traders noted that several languages were still being spoken in the settlement, and then they stopped noting it, because the settlement had settled on one.

## Tension

A people made by taking in refugees has no origin it can point to, and every other nation around it does. When the paperwork came, and it came for two centuries, the question was always the same: what were you before? The answer is that the before is exactly what got dissolved to make the after, and no clerk has ever found that a satisfying reply.
