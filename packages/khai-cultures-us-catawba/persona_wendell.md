# Persona: Wendell

## Taxonomy

[river people](position_river_people.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-cultures

## Projection

Eighty-one, on the porch, in a folding chair he has repaired twice. [American English](@chbrain/khai-cultures-tongues/en/position_language_en_us.md) is [his own tongue, the one he was raised inside](@chbrain/khai-engine-language/process_speaking_mother_tongue.md). He was a boy in a room where the old chief spoke Catawba, and what reaches him from that room now is [sound he can place and cannot open](@chbrain/khai-engine-language/process_hearing_decoded.md): he knows what it sounded like and never knew what it meant. He belongs to [the Catawba way](position_culture_catawba.md) as a witness, which is a smaller thing than being a speaker and is not nothing.

## Action

He tells the same four stories to anybody who sits down, and once a year he lets [Junie](persona_junie.md) record him saying the handful of words he is sure of.

## Shadow

He believes he is the proof the language was real, and that belief is doing the work grief would otherwise do. If a fluent speaker walked up the drive tomorrow he would not know where to put himself.

## Tell

He corrects other people's pronunciation of words he cannot translate.
