# Plot: A cloud on every deed

## Taxonomy

Parent group: plot of [the Catawba](play_catawba.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-cultures

## Cue

A cloud on the title of every house, church and filling station in a county-sized tract of South Carolina, sitting there quietly since a treaty was signed at a ford and never sent to the Senate.

## Action

The nation sued. The claim was not that the land had been stolen, it was that the sale had never been valid, because a state cannot buy land from a tribe without Congress. Twenty-seven thousand landowners found that the deed to their house was arguable. Litigation ran for years and the pressure it applied was not moral, it was commercial: nothing in the tract could be cleanly mortgaged while it lasted. The settlement in 1993 restored federal recognition, provided a fund for land and services, and extinguished the claim. The nation got itself back, on paper, by making everybody else's paper uncertain.

## Stage

- `place`: [Nation Ford](place_nation_ford.md), whose defective treaty was the whole case

## Tension

The instrument that worked was the one the nation had been injured by. A hundred and fifty years of bad title was the only asset left, and spending it meant signing away the claim for good. The land the fund bought is a fraction of the tract, and the tract is now, finally and permanently, somebody else's.
