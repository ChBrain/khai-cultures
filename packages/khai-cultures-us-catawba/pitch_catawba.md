# Pitch: the Catawba Key

## Taxonomy

Parent group: pitch for [the Catawba](play_catawba.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-cultures

## Tenor

Dry and understated, and not sad. A people who have been counted out on paper four times and are still filing forms do not talk about themselves tragically.

## Undertow

Everything here has been written down by somebody else: the treaties, the rolls, the language, the pottery in museum catalogues. The Nation reads its own life in other people's handwriting, and has learned to work with that rather than resent it.

## Nerve

The pot and the word, side by side. One came through unbroken and one did not, and nobody can explain why the hands kept what the mouth lost. Ask a Catawba what survived and watch which one they reach for.

## Echo

Clay. It is in the origin, in the work, in the ground that got taken, and in the present. When this culture wants to say what it is, it picks something up.
