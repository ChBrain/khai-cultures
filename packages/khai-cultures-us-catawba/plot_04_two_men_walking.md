# Plot: Two men walking

## Taxonomy

Parent group: plot of [the Catawba](play_catawba.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-cultures

## Cue

Two Mormon missionaries on foot on the road from Charlotte, of no consequence to anybody, arriving at a settlement of a few hundred people that every other denomination in the state had visited, counted and left.

## Action

They arrived in 1883. They stayed, they baptised, and then the converts baptised, and inside a decade most of the nation was Latter-day Saint, which is a thing that happened almost nowhere else in the American Southeast. The reasons offered afterwards are all partly true and none of them sufficient: that the missionaries came to the house rather than summoning people to a building, that a church of restored covenants reads differently to a people holding a treaty nobody honoured, that the Mormons were themselves being run out of places and said so. What is certain is that it took, and it is still taking: the meetinghouse is the building this nation shows up at.

## Tension

The most durable institution this culture has was brought in by two strangers walking, at the exact period when everything the nation had made for itself was being taken. A people can be told they are disappearing and answer by adopting something entirely new and holding it for a hundred and forty years, and no account of cultural survival that expects continuity knows what to do with that.
