# Piece: the burnishing stone

## Taxonomy

Parent group: piece of [the Catawba](play_catawba.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-cultures

## Place

In an apron pocket, or a coffee tin with the rest of the tools.

## Load Bearing

It is a smooth river pebble, and rubbing the leather-hard pot with it compresses the surface until it shines. That shine is the whole look of Catawba pottery and there is no substitute for it: no slip, no glaze, no polish does what a stone and an hour do.

## Apparent

It looks like a pebble somebody forgot to throw back in the river, and that is exactly what it is.

## Yearbook

Stones get handed down. A potter will often be using one that belonged to the woman who taught her, and can tell you whose it was, which is more provenance than most museum pieces carry.
