# Plot: The Shared Ground

## Taxonomy

[play_iberia](play_iberia.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-cultures

## Cue

A muleteer walks west along a paved road that was laid to reach the ocean. Nobody stops him and nobody asks anything. The speech around him changes a little every few days, and he could not say at which village it stopped being one thing and started being another.

## Action

Rome makes the whole peninsula one administrative space, Hispania, and binds it with roads that still decide where the towns are. Al-Andalus holds most of it for centuries and leaves its words in both languages that come after. Then the long push south begins, and it goes in parallel columns: Castile down the middle, Aragon down the east, and a county on the Atlantic edge that becomes Portugal down the west. **The columns become kingdoms, and the line between them is simply where two armies going the same way at the same time happened to stop touching.** Fixed at Alcañices in 1297, it is the oldest continuous border in Europe. [Spain](@chbrain/khai-cultures-spain/play_spain.md) and [Portugal](@chbrain/khai-cultures-portugal/play_portugal.md) do not meet as strangers; they separate as halves of one motion.

## Stage

- [Spain](@chbrain/khai-cultures-spain/play_spain.md): the layered ground that never finishes agreeing which of its layers is the real one.
- [Portugal](@chbrain/khai-cultures-portugal/play_portugal.md): the western column, which reached the sea first and kept going.

## Tension

These two are not neighbours who grew apart. **They are one movement that split lengthwise**, which is why everything structural is shared - the reconquest, the turn to the ocean, the empire, a long dictatorship each, the return to Europe on the same day - and why almost nothing everyday is. A border that old stops being a frontier and becomes a habit: there is nothing to negotiate along it, nothing to fight about, and therefore no reason to look. The peninsula gives them one geography and does not give them one another.
