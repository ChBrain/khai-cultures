# References: Obwalden

## The pitch layer

The pitch is written from Switzerland's national Hofstede profile, inflected by what it does to a polity of forty thousand that was always the half of something: the same low power distance and high individualism, read through a community whose influence depended on being come to. No dimension is named in the prose.

Source: Geert Hofstede, Hofstede Insights (the Country Comparison tool and the six-dimensions model of national culture), the national profile for Switzerland. No separate sub-scores for the cantons are claimed.

## Two decisions, recorded

**Brother Klaus is staged and not cast.** He is the one figure of national standing this landscape has produced, and casting him would be the easy move. It would also be the wrong one twice: he belongs to the Confederation as much as to this canton, and what he actually did - sit still, say nothing that was written down, and let people come - cannot be given a Projection without inventing the interior the sources deliberately do not supply. So the Ranft is a place, the wheel picture is a piece, the counsel is a plot, and the man is described rather than performed.

**The origin is a direction and not a date.** Asked where this culture comes from, the available answers are 1291 and 1815, and both are constitutional facts of the kind the house order on the defining question (`management/orders/order_the_defining_question.md`) warns against staging as the culture. What explains this place is older and simpler: it is the half above a wood, it never was a whole, and it has never tried to become one. `plot_00` is that.
