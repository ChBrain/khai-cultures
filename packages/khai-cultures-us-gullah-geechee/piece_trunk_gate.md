# Piece: de trunk gate

## Taxonomy

Parent group: piece o [de Gullah Geechee](play_gullah_geechee.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-cultures

## Place

Set een de bank wey de field meet de tide creek, unda de mud.

## Load Bearing

E a wood box wid a door pon bof en, an e let de ribba een pon de field wen de tide come up an let um out wen de tide go down. Dat how you grow rice pon a tide ribba widout no pump an widout no rain. De whole Lowcountry rice ting stan or fall pon dis one piece o wood, an de knowledge o um ain come fom Englan.

## Apparent

E look like nottin. A plank een de mud. Folk paddle right ober um an neba know e deh.

## Yearbook

De bank an de gate still unda de marsh all cross de Lowcountry, ten tousan acre o um, mek by han. Dey de bigges ting anybody build een dis state fo de railroad, an dey ain got one name pon um nowhey.
