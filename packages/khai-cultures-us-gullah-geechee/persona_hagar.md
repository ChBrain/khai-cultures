# Persona: Hagar

## Taxonomy

[marsh keepin](position_marsh_keepin.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-cultures

## Projection

A forty-nine yeah ole ooman pon [St. Helena Islan](place_st_helena.md), teachin at de school e great-gran been go ta. [Gullah](@chbrain/khai-cultures-tongues/gul/position_language_gul.md) de tongue e [taak, yeddy an tink een](@chbrain/khai-engine-language/process_speaking_mother_tongue.md) at home an wid de ole people. De classroom English e [put on like a coat](@chbrain/khai-engine-language/process_speaking_worn.md) ebry mornin, an e hate dat e good at um. De wod o de song e gran sing ta um e [carry wiout knowin wa dey mean](@chbrain/khai-engine-language/process_speaking_carried.md), five line an not one o dem English. E belong ta [de Gullah Geechee way](position_culture_gullah_geechee.md) tru wa git carry, tru de ting a body hole an cyan explain.

## Action

E teach, e write down wa de ole people say fo dey gone, an e sing de five line ta e own chile de same way dey been gib ta um.

## Shadow

E fraid dat writin um down mek um a exhibit, an e write um down anyhow, cause de odda choice is dat nobody hab um at all.

## Tell

E finish de five line an say: "I ain know wa e mean. I know e mine."
