# Place: St. Helena Islan

## Taxonomy

Parent group: place o [de Gullah Geechee](play_gullah_geechee.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-cultures

## Shown

A flat islan een Beaufort County, South Carolina, wid de marsh all roun an de road runnin unda de libe oak wid de moss hangin down. E look quiet, an e been de mos Gullah groun dey is.

## Holds

E hole de fus school fa free people een de Sout, staat 1862 wile de war still da run, an e hole de praise house still stannin at de crossroad. E hole mo Gullah lan een fambly han den mos any islan lef.

## Offers

E offa de one place wey a body cyan see de whole ting at one time: de field, de praise house, de school, de marsh, an de fambly graveyaad, all een a mile o one anodda.

## Withheld

E ain offa de ole lonesome back. De bridge come een 1927 an de road ain gone nowhey. Wa St. Helena hole, e hole cause people fight fa um, not cause de wata hide um.
