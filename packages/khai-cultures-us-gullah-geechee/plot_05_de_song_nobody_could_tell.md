# Plot: De song nobody could tell

## Taxonomy

Parent group: plot o [de Gullah Geechee](play_gullah_geechee.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-cultures

## Cue

A ooman at Harris Neck een Georgia sing a five-line song ober e wuk wa ain got one English wod een um, an e ain know wa de wod mean, cause e mama ain know needa.

## Action

A linguist come tru de Sea Islan een de tirties wid a recordin machine, lookin fa wa people been sayin fa two hundud yeah been broke English. E fine de song an e write um down, an e fine fo tousan mo African name an wod een de mout o people wa neba been tole dey been sayin anyting but English. E publish um een 1949 an de "broke English" ting ain neba recoba. Fifty yeah on, somebody carry de recordin ta Sierra Leone an play um, an a ooman een a village up de riba say: dat we song, we sing um wen we bury somebody. De singer daughta go oba an yeddy e own mama song sing back ta um by people e neba seen. Dis de way [Hagar](persona_hagar.md) hole e own five line: e [carry um wiout knowin wa dey mean](@chbrain/khai-engine-language/process_speaking_carried.md), an e carry um right.

## Tension

De song trabel two hundud yeah tru people wa could not translate one wod o um, an e arribe correc. Dat prove a inheritance an e ain gib a kin: de village hab de song an ain hab dis people, an dis people hab de song an ain hab dat village. Wa come back een 1997 is a fac, an a hug, an nottin you cyan write pon a paper.
