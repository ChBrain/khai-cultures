# Persona: Cuffee

## Taxonomy

[marsh keepin](position_marsh_keepin.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-cultures

## Projection

A seventy-fo yeah ole man pon [St. Helena Islan](place_st_helena.md), oysterman befo de bed git close, deacon een de praise house since e been forty. [Gullah](@chbrain/khai-cultures-tongues/gul/position_language_gul.md) de tongue e [taak, yeddy an tink een](@chbrain/khai-engine-language/process_speaking_mother_tongue.md). De church English fom de big church pon de mainlan e [put on like a coat](@chbrain/khai-engine-language/process_speaking_worn.md) wen de bishop come. De talk o de resort board bout access an easement [reach um like a echo way off](@chbrain/khai-engine-language/process_hearing_followed.md). E belong ta [de Gullah Geechee way](position_culture_gullah_geechee.md) tru de flo o de praise house, wey e larn fa shout fo e larn fa read.

## Action

E open de praise house Sunday an Tuesday, e lead [de ring shout](process_ring_shout.md) wen dey nuff people fa mek a ring, an e still tek de boat out tru a creek dat now run behin a gate.

## Shadow

E ain sho de young one da come back, an e keep de praise house open anyhow, an some Tuesday e set een deh by eself an ain tell nobody.

## Tell

E point at de flo wid e stick an say: "Keep you foot. Cross um an e a dance, an we ain dancin."
