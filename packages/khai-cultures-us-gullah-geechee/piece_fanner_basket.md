## Load Bearing

E de tool wa sepate de rice fom de chaff. You trow de rice up out de flat wide basket an de win tek de light ting way an de heaby ting fall back. Widout dis, de crop ain food. Dis basket come fom de Rice Coast wid de people wa know how fa use um, an de coil ain change.
