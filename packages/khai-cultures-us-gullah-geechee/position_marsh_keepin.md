# Position: marsh keepin

## Taxonomy

Parent group: positions

## Owner

- Project: khai-cultures

## Has

A body wa hole dis know de creek betta den de road. E know wen de tide da come an wen e da go widout lookin at no papa, e know wey de oyster bed lay, e know wich marsh grass cut you han an wich one coil. Dat ain hobby knowledge. Dat wa feed a fambly wen de crop ain nuff.

## Orders

E order dat de marsh belong ta nobody an ta ebrybody. You tek wa you need an you lef de bed fa grow back, an de one wa strip um clean ain welcome de nex time. Dey ain neba been no fence out deh, an dat de whole point.

## Loses

E lose wen de marsh git fence. A dock go up, a gate go cross de pat ta de wata, an de creek wa been open fa two hundud yeah now behin somebody lawn. De oyster still deh. De pat ain.

## Drives

E mek a body slow an watchful, an e mek um sho o wa dey know. A marsh keepin body ain aks wedda de wata da rise. Dey been watchin um.
