# Plot: De lan wid no paper

## Taxonomy

Parent group: plot o [de Gullah Geechee](play_gullah_geechee.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-cultures

## Cue

A man die pon e own lan een 1912 widout no will, cause de lawyer een town ain fa people like him an de praise house ain write deed.

## Action

So de lan pass ta all e chile at once, undivided, an den ta all dey chile, an so on. Six generation on, tirty-one people own one piece o groun an ain one o dem cyan say wich paat is dem. Fa a hundud yeah dat ain matta: somebody lib pon um, ebrybody know de arrangement, an de arrangement hole. Den one day de groun git wort somethin ta somebody fom off. An unda de law, any one heir out o dat tirty-one cyan sell dey share ta a stranger, an dat stranger cyan aks de cote fa a partition, an de cote sell de whole ting at auction an split de money. One cousin een anodda state wa neba seen de place cyan en fo hundud yeah o libin deh. [Binah](persona_binah.md) hole a deed een a tin box wid name pon um o people e neba met. Dis how de mos o de Sea Islan lan gone, an e ain gone by no gun. E gone by a filin fee.

## Tension

De same ting wa kep de lan een de fambly is de ting wa let um go: nobody eba write down who own wa. Trus wuk til de day somebody outside de trus cyan buy a way een, an den de whole hundud yeah o trus is de weakness. Fa forty yeah atta 1912 dis ain cos nobody nottin, cause de lan ain wort takin. De machine set deh oil an quiet, waitin pon a reason. Dey ain no way fa fix dis backwards. Ebry fix staat wid writin down wa de praise house neba needed fa write.
