# Plot: De bridge

## Taxonomy

Parent group: plot o [de Gullah Geechee](play_gullah_geechee.md)

## Owner

## Cue

1956, de fus bridge open ta Hilton Head, an a islan wa been reach by boat all e life now reach by cyar.

## Action

De resort staat de nex yeah. Wa follow ain a mystery an ain neba been one: de lan wort mo, an de undivided deed wa been settin quiet since 1912 now wort a lawyer time, de tax go up pon de same acre a fambly been hole since de tax sale, an a fambly wa mek e libin fom de creek an de field git a bill dey haffa sell groun fa pay. Islan atta islan, de same order - bridge, survey, resort, tax, sell. [Cuffee](persona_cuffee.md) remember de oyster bed fo dey close um, an e remember de yeah e haffa aks permission fa cross a pat e granfadda walk. De wata been de thing wa keep dis people whole, de way de summa skeeter been: e mek reachin dem expensive. De bridge ain tek nottin by eself, an dat de ting fa understan bout um. E ain a bulldozer. E jus mek de groun wort takin, an de paper been ready fa fifty yeah.

## Tension

Ebry ting wa kep dis culture whole been a kine o poverty: bad wata, bad air, bad road. Dat ain a ting a people cyan aks fa mo o, an nobody sane would. So de question ain how fa git de lonesome back. E how a people hole wa dey hole wen de las wall roun um come down, an ain nobody got a good answer ta dat yet.
