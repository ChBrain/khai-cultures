# Plot: De basket wa outlib de rice

## Taxonomy

Parent group: plot o [de Gullah Geechee](play_gullah_geechee.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-cultures

## Cue

Rice off de field still hab de chaff pon um, an somebody haffa git um off fo anybody eat.

## Action

So de [fanna basket](piece_fanner_basket.md) git coil: flat, wide, shallow, jus de shape fa trow de rice up an let de win tek de light ting way. E come cross de wata een de head o de people wa mek um, an de coil ain change one stitch een tree hundud yeah. [Coil de basket](process_coil_de_basket.md) pass fom a ooman ta a chile settin side by side, an dat de only way e eba pass, cause you cyan larn a coil fom a picture. Wen de rice wuk finally die atta de storm an de boll weevil an de cheap Louisiana crop, de basket ain die wid um. De han keep de shape wid nottin fa put een um. Pon de highway een [Mount Pleasant](place_mount_pleasant.md) de stan go up, an de same coil wa hole de rice staat holin bread, den holin nottin at all an jus sittin pon a shelf.

## Tension

A tool wa outlib e own wuk is eeda a art or a ghos, an wich one e be depen pon who still know how fa mek um. De basket win dat one: de han neba stop. Bot de ting e hole now is money fom a stranger, an a craft wa lib pon dat hab a diffunt kine o root den a craft wa lib pon rice.
