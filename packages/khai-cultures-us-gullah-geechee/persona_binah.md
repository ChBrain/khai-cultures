# Persona: Binah

## Taxonomy

[marsh keepin](position_marsh_keepin.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-cultures

## Projection

A sixty-one yeah ole ooman coilin basket at a stan pon de highway een [Mount Pleasant](place_mount_pleasant.md). [Gullah](@chbrain/khai-cultures-tongues/gul/position_language_gul.md) de tongue e [taak, yeddy an tink een](@chbrain/khai-engine-language/process_speaking_mother_tongue.md), de one e fambly run pon. De English fom de school e [put on like a coat](@chbrain/khai-engine-language/process_speaking_worn.md) fa de tourist an fa de county office. De lawyer wod een a partition paper [reach um like a echo way off](@chbrain/khai-engine-language/process_hearing_followed.md), an dat de place e feel um mos: e hole a deed e cyan read gainst people wa read fa a libin. E belong ta [de Gullah Geechee way](position_culture_gullah_geechee.md) tru de han, tru wa git pass settin side by side.

## Action

E [coil de basket](process_coil_de_basket.md) an sell um, an e keep de fambly papa een a tin box, an e drive ta de county office wen de tax notice come.

## Shadow

E ain tell de grandchile how bad de lan ting is, cause e want um fa come home, an de not tellin is how de fambly git ta tirty-one heir wid nobody knowin who own wa.

## Tell

E hole up a basket an say: "Dis yah de onlies ting I got dey cyan mek me sell."
