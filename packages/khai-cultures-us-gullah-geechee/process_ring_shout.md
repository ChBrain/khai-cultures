# Process: de ring shout

## Taxonomy

Parent group: process o [de Gullah Geechee](play_gullah_geechee.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-cultures

## Initiated by

De stick hit de flo, an de baser staat fa sing, an de shouter fall een behin um.

## Direction

De ring go roun counta-clock, an de foot neba cross. Dat de rule an dat de whole rule: cross you foot an e a dance, an a dance ain allow een de praise house. Keep de foot shufflin an e wuship.

## Lever

E de ting wa let a people wuship dey own way unda somebody else roof. De wod been Christian an de body been African, an de rule bout de foot is wa hole de two togeda wiout nobody haffa argue bout um.

## Echo

De counta-clock ring da turn still, een Bolden an Sapelo an a handful mo. E de ole-es African ting still gwine een dis country, an e still ain a dance.
