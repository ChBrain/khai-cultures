# Plot: De sweetgrass

## Owner

## Cue

De sweetgrass ain grow nowhey but de dune an de back o de marsh, an mos o dat groun behin a fence now or unda a parkin lot.

## Action

So de basket maker een [Mount Pleasant](place_mount_pleasant.md) buy de grass. E come up by truck fom Florida, sell by de bundle, an e cos mo ebry yeah. [Binah](persona_binah.md) still [coil de basket](process_coil_de_basket.md) de same way e been larn um settin side e gran, an de stitch ain change, an de grass ain fom yah no mo. Dat de shape o de whole ting now: de craft hole, de tongue hole, de shout hole een a handful o place, an de groun unda all tree o dem da go. De county put up a sign bout heritage pon de same road wey de stan set. De young one come back some, an some ain. De ting wa decide um ain gwine be de sign.

Dat ain a endin an e ain a sabin. E de question dis people libin een right now, an de answa ain been wrote yet.
