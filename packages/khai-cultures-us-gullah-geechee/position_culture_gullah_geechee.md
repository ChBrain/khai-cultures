# Position: de Gullah Geechee way

## Taxonomy

Parent group: positions. E stan een [de American way](@chbrain/khai-cultures-usa/position_culture_american_way.md), cause dis groun been mek American roun we an we ain go nowhey.

## Owner

- Project: khai-cultures

## Has

E hab a tongue, a shout, a basket an a name, an ain got no acre wa hole um. De Gullah Geechee way stan een de United State an ain stan een no place else: e ain a piece o Africa kep ober yah, an e ain a piece o Englan gone wrong. E de ting wa git mek right yah out o wa been bring, an dey ain nobody cross no wata hole de odda en o um.

## Orders

E order dat wa git carry count mo den wa git write. De basket name pass fom de fambly an neba fom de cote house; de shout larn by de foot an neba fom a book; de wod pass fom de mout. A body wa hole dis way know dey own ting by wa dey been gib, not by wa dey cyan show pon paper.

## Loses

E da lose de groun unda um. De islan wa been fa we git sell by de acre, an de law ain tek um wid no gun, e tek um wid a deed nobody een de fambly eba mek. An wen de people scatta, de shout an de tongue ain hab no house fa stan een.

## Drives

E dribe a body fa hole two ting at one time: fa be American, wid de school an de tax an de flag, an fa be Gullah Geechee, wid de basket an de praise house. E ain a split. E how dis people been American de whole time, an de mistake is fa tink dey haffa pick.
