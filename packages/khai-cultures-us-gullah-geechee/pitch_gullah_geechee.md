# Pitch: de Gullah Geechee Key

## Taxonomy

Parent group: pitch fa [de Gullah Geechee](play_gullah_geechee.md)

## Owner

## Tenor

Slow an level, an neba hurry. Dis yah a people wa been lef lone fa two hundud yeah an larn fa tek dey time wid a ting. Nottin git say loud.

## Undertow

Ebryting yah da git tek, an ebrybody know um, an nobody say um straight out. De lan, de grass, de wod, de name. De taak stay calm ober top o dat.

## Nerve

De one ting wa cyan be bought. Wen a Gullah Geechee body taak bout wa dey hole, dey ain taak bout de acre; dey taak bout de basket an de shout an de wod, cause dem is de ting wa ain got no deed pon um.

## Echo

De wata. E come back een ebry plot: e keep we, den e git bridge, den e rise. A tongue wa grow up pon a islan soun like de wata wen e taak bout time.
