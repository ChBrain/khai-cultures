# Plot: De rice wa bring we

## Taxonomy

Parent group: plot o [de Gullah Geechee](play_gullah_geechee.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-cultures

## Cue

De rice ain grow by eself een de Carolina tide marsh, an de people wa come fom Englan ain know de fus ting bout mekin um grow.

## Action

So dey buy fa de skill. De Charleston paper fom de 1700s aks by name fa people fom de Rice Coast, fom de Gambia down ta Sierra Leone an de Windward shore, an say so right out een de advertisement, cause dat wey rice been grow pon a tide fa a tousan yeah. De [trunk gate](piece_trunk_gate.md) an de bank an de field wa mek de Lowcountry rich git dig by han by people wa know de wuk fo dey eba see dis country. Dat is de whole ugly arithmetic o um: buckra ain want a people, buckra want a knowledge, an fa git de knowledge dey haffa tek de people wa hole um, an tek um fom one stretch o coast steada fom ebrywey. Dat one choice, mek fa money an fa nottin else, is why so many people fom close-by place lan up pon de same islan at de same time. Ebrywey else een dis country de people git scatta an de ole tongue dissolve een one generation. Yah dey ain scatta.

## Tension

De ting wa mek dis people a people is de same ting wa been done ta dem, an dat cyan be say two ways at once wiout one o dem soun wrong. De rice ain a gif. De concentration ain a mercy, e a shippin decision. Bot wa grow out o um ain belong ta de one wa mek de decision, an tree hundud yeah on, de tongue is yah an de rice money gone.
