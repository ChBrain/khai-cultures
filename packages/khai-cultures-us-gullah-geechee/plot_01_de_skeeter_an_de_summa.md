## Cue

## Action

So fom bout May ta fros, buckra lef. Ebry planter fambly wa could afford um gone ta Charleston or Newport or up de country, an de islan git lef ta de people wa wuk um, wid a oberseer or two an sometime not eben dat. Half de yeah, ebry yeah, fa a hundud an fifty yeah. Dat de whole secret an ain nobody eba plan um. A tongue need two ting fa hole: people nuff fa taak um, an time widout nobody standin ober you correctin um. [St. Helena Islan](place_st_helena.md) an de res o de Sea Islan hab bof, an de marsh an de creek mek de res o de yeah hard fa reach too. De way o bein wa hole de marsh, [marsh keepin](position_marsh_keepin.md), git larn een dat lonesome: wich bed ta tek an wich ta lef, wen de tide da turn, wa de wata gib you wen de crop ain nuff.
