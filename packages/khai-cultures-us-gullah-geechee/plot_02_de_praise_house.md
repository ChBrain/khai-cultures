# Plot: De praise house

## Taxonomy

Parent group: plot o [de Gullah Geechee](play_gullah_geechee.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-cultures

## Cue

De big church pon de mainlan too far fa walk ebry Sunday, an wa go on eenside um ain de way dis people wuship anyhow.

## Action

So ebry settlement build e own: a one-room clapboard house, no steeple, no bell, sometime no windah, big nuff fa forty body. An de praise house ain jus fa Sunday. E de cote. A quarrel bout a boat or a fence or a man an a ooman git settle deh by de elda, an de whole settlement know de verdic by Monday, an de county neba yeddy one wod bout um. Dat self govment, an e been runnin fa a hundud yeah fo anybody gib dis people a vote. [De ring shout](process_ring_shout.md) happen deh atta de service, wen de bench git push back: de ring turn counta-clock, an de foot neba cross, cause cross you foot an e a dance. [Cuffee](persona_cuffee.md) larn dat rule fom a ole man wa larn um de same way, an e cyan tell you wey de rule come fom.

## Tension

A people wid no standin een de law mek a cote out o a shed an run um straight fa a hundud yeah. Wen de real cote finally open e door ta dem, e ain settle a fence line by Monday, an e ain neba yeddy o de praise house. De ting wa wuk got no standin, an de ting wid standin ain wuk.
