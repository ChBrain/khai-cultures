# Persona: Caleb

## Taxonomy

[mountain exchange](position_mountain_exchange.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-cultures

## Projection

A Bristol radio producer and working fiddler who crosses State Street several times in an ordinary day without treating the state line as a cultural border. [Appalachian Virginian English](@chbrain/khai-cultures-tongues/en/position_language_en_us_va.md) is his mother tongue, the language he [speaks, hears, and thinks](process_speaking_mother_tongue.md) in the studio and at a session. He listens for a tune's route through Black string bands, church singing, commercial records, family memory, and neighboring states before calling it traditional. He belongs to [the Old Dominion](position_culture_old_dominion.md) from its southwest edge, where Richmond's preferred account of Virginia can feel farther away than Tennessee across the street.

## Action

He records live sets, repairs arrangements that collapse under a microphone, and builds programs around the sources a familiar country-music story leaves out. In [the microphone on State Street](plot_04_the_microphone_on_state_street.md), he inherits the Bristol Sessions as both a technical breakthrough and an unfinished question of credit.

## Shadow

His instinct to complicate every origin story can keep him from admitting when a plain, beloved song is doing enough work on its own. Attribution can become another performance of expertise if the musicians being credited are never given the microphone.

## Tell

Before he names a tune, he asks who taught it, who recorded it, and who was in the room but not on the label.
