## Owner

## Shown

Windowless data halls, substations, transmission lines, fiber routes, generator yards, construction entrances, office parks, schools, townhouses, and older roads sharing the same rapidly built Northern Virginia ground.

## Holds

- [the grid behind the cloud](plot_99_the_grid_behind_the_cloud.md): global digital capacity made local as load, tax base, worksite, land use, noise, and grid contingency.

## Offers

Dense fiber connections, access to power, proximity to federal and commercial customers, construction employment, and substantial local tax revenue. It also gives [Nadiya](persona_nadiya.md) one place from which to read the system as worker, renter, and neighbor.

## Withheld

The cloud metaphor's promise that digital services have no body. The corridor cannot hide its fans, transformers, diesel reserves, water demand, land consumption, or the homes close enough to hear and see them.
