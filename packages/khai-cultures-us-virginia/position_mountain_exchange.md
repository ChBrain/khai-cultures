# Position: mountain exchange

## Taxonomy

Parent group: positions

## Owner

- Project: khai-cultures

## Has

A Southwest Virginia belonging made across borders by ear. It has tunes carried between Black and white musicians, African and European instruments meeting in Appalachian practice, family repertories changed by churches, dances, radio, and records, and [State Street](place_bristol_state_street.md) as a state line whose traffic matters more than its division.

## Orders

That a tradition name its routes of exchange rather than claim purity. That the player listen before taking a lead, the producer credit a source that the old label omitted, and the local institution understand commercial recognition as one episode in a practice that began before the microphone.

## Loses

When the record market sorts braided music into racial genres, when a birth story makes recording technology the parent of what it merely captured, and when correcting the myth becomes another expert performance that never returns authority or attention to working musicians.

## Drives

[Caleb](persona_caleb.md) to make radio that lets the tune keep its complicated family, and [the string-band session](process_string_band_session.md) to work as conversation rather than recital. In [the microphone on State Street](plot_04_the_microphone_on_state_street.md), it makes the border city a relay instead of a birthplace with a single parent.
