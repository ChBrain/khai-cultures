# Place: the Pamunkey River

## Taxonomy

Parent group: places

## Owner

- Project: khai-cultures

## Shown

A tidal tributary of the York bordered by marsh, field, wooded bank, and the Pamunkey Indian Reservation. Shad and other migratory fish move through water that is at once habitat, route, livelihood, and the named center of a nation's homeland.

## Holds

- [the rivers already had names](plot_00_the_rivers_already_had_names.md): a Virginia origin in inhabited river country rather than English arrival.
- [tobacco makes the labor line](plot_01_tobacco_makes_the_labor_line.md): Indigenous ground beside the export routes along which plantation wealth moved.

## Offers

Clay for [Pamunkey pottery](process_pamunkey_pottery.md), seasonal fishing, ecological observation, and a place where [Lena](persona_lena.md) can show continuity as ordinary work instead of ceremonial display.

## Withheld

The comforting fiction of an empty waterway waiting to become colonial infrastructure. The river does not surrender its older relations merely because a later map uses it to explain a settlement, plantation, or watershed program.
