# Place: State Street

## Taxonomy

Parent group: places

## Owner

- Project: khai-cultures

## Shown

The commercial street through Bristol with Virginia on one side and Tennessee on the other. Storefronts, signs, traffic, venues, and pedestrians occupy one downtown even where the boundary runs beneath them.

## Holds

- [the microphone on State Street](plot_04_the_microphone_on_state_street.md): the 1927 recording rooms to which regional musicians carried songs across several state lines.

## Offers

A border that behaves as a meeting place. Musicians, radio workers, shoppers, and listeners can cross it without leaving the local world, which lets [Caleb](persona_caleb.md) hear Southwest Virginia as part of a larger Appalachian exchange.

## Withheld

The idea that a line on the pavement explains where culture changes. The commercial record can print Bristol, Tennessee or Bristol, Virginia, but the tune has already moved through both and beyond them.
