# Position: Black self-possession

## Taxonomy

Parent group: positions

## Owner

- Project: khai-cultures

## Has

The proposition that freedom becomes real through acts and institutions people can use, not only through declarations made over them. It has the crossing to [Fort Monroe](place_fort_monroe.md), the bank and newspaper in [Jackson Ward](place_jackson_ward.md), and a practice of pooling movement, information, money, and risk until people classified as property or denied equal service can exercise authorship over their own lives.

## Orders

That the person who moves first be named before the official who later recognizes the act. That an exceptional leader leave behind machinery others can operate. That mutual aid answer immediate exclusion while continuing to demand that the excluding public and commercial systems change their terms.

## Loses

When resilience is used to romanticize what made it necessary, when one celebrated entrepreneur is allowed to stand in for a neighborhood, or when discipline and respectability become new tests imposed on people already denied ordinary room for error.

## Drives

[Maggie Walker](persona_maggie_walker.md) to turn small deposits and a fraternal network into a newspaper and bank, and [Aya](persona_aya.md) to ask who controls present-day credit and neighborhood information. It begins in [three men cross the water](plot_02_three_men_cross_the_water.md) as freedom enacted and becomes institution in [put our money together](plot_03_put_our_money_together.md).
