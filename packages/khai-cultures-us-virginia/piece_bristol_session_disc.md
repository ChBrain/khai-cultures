# Piece: a Bristol session disc

## Taxonomy

Parent group: pieces

## Owner

- Project: khai-cultures

## Place

Recorded in Bristol during the 1927 sessions, manufactured by the Victor Talking Machine Company, and carried by shops, mail, phonographs, and later collections far beyond [State Street](place_bristol_state_street.md).

## Load Bearing

The disc makes one local performance portable and repeatable. It converts a roomful of negotiated sound into a commercial object, expanding the audience while fixing the artist name, genre, credit, and market category chosen for the label.

## Apparent

A ten-inch shellac record with a paper Victor label, grooves holding only a few minutes on each side, and a catalog identity that looks more settled than the exchange among musicians from which the performance came.
