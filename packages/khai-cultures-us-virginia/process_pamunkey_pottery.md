# Process: Pamunkey pottery

## Taxonomy

Parent group: processes

## Owner

- Project: khai-cultures

## Initiated by

Pamunkey makers working with knowledge carried through families, the tribal pottery school and guild, and contemporary individual practice.

## Direction

Local clay is gathered and prepared, built by hand or shaped with adopted tools, surfaced, dried, and fired. Each maker decides how inherited forms, available materials, market demands, and a present artistic hand will meet in the finished vessel.

## Lever

Continuity does not require technical stasis. The practice can preserve knowledge while absorbing new firing methods, forms, and uses, making adaptation evidence of survival rather than evidence against authenticity.

## Echo

In [the rivers already had names](plot_00_the_rivers_already_had_names.md), pottery makes the long relation between ground and people tangible. [Lena](persona_lena.md) uses it to refuse the choice between a museum's timeless Native object and a contemporary object emptied of tribal history.
