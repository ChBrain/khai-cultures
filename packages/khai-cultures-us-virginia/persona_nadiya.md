# Persona: Nadiya

## Taxonomy

[systems stewardship](position_systems_stewardship.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-cultures

## Projection

A substation technician who rents a townhouse in Sterling and lives inside the same Northern Virginia load she helps keep stable. [Virginian English](@chbrain/khai-cultures-tongues/en/position_language_en_us_va.md) is her mother tongue, the language in which she [speaks, hears, and thinks](process_speaking_mother_tongue.md) through switching orders, school pickup, and county meetings. On the job she reads the cloud as breakers, heat, reserve, and sequence; at home she hears it as construction traffic, fan noise, and the argument over the next transmission line. She belongs to [the Old Dominion](position_culture_old_dominion.md) by insisting that authority over a system includes an account to the people living beside it.

## Action

She maintains electrical equipment, drills restoration procedures, and translates grid events into ordinary consequences for neighbors. In [the grid behind the cloud](plot_99_the_grid_behind_the_cloud.md), she is both infrastructure worker and resident, unable to pretend the global service and the local cost occur in different places.

## Shadow

Operational discipline can narrow her field of view to what can trip today. She may defend a system's immediate reliability while postponing the harder public question of how much new load the region should accept at all.

## Tell

When a meeting calls a data center invisible infrastructure, she lists what she can see from the road: line, fence, fan, generator, truck, and the houses across from them.
