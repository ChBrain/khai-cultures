# Persona: Lena

## Taxonomy

[tidewater continuance](position_tidewater_continuance.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-cultures

## Projection

A Pamunkey potter who also works seasonal hours around the river and fish hatchery. [Virginian English](@chbrain/khai-cultures-tongues/en/position_language_en_us_va.md) is the mother tongue in which she [speaks, hears, and thinks](process_speaking_mother_tongue.md), and the practical language of orders, museum visitors, fisheries work, and family. She knows words and names carried from the Algonquian language of her ancestors, but she will not pretend fragments are fluency for somebody else's satisfaction. She belongs to [the Old Dominion](position_culture_old_dominion.md) by refusing its favorite starting date: her Virginia begins with a working river and a nation still present beside it.

## Action

She gathers and prepares local clay for [Pamunkey pottery](process_pamunkey_pottery.md), teaches visitors why adaptation is part of the tradition, and brings river observations into [the Chesapeake Bay plan](plan_chesapeake_bay_cleanup.md). In [the rivers already had names](plot_00_the_rivers_already_had_names.md), she is the present consequence of an origin too often narrated as prehistory.

## Shadow

Being asked to prove continuity can make her guard every explanation. She can answer clumsy curiosity as if it were another demand for evidence, closing a conversation before a possible ally understands what was at stake.

## Tell

When someone calls a pot an ancient design, she turns it over and points to the maker's decision: "Old knowledge is not the same thing as a frozen object."
