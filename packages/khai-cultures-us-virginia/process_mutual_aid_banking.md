# Process: mutual-aid banking

## Taxonomy

Parent group: processes

## Owner

- Project: khai-cultures

## Initiated by

Members who place small, regular contributions into a trusted institution and the organizers who make those pooled funds available to the same community as benefits, wages, credit, and loans.

## Direction

Collect contributions, keep a legible account, communicate through meetings and print, evaluate needs and borrowers, lend locally, and return repayment to the pool. Trust becomes durable only when bookkeeping and governance let members see how collective money moves.

## Lever

Many sums too small to command commercial attention can become an institution large enough to finance a home, business, funeral, or wage. The lever is not thrift alone but ownership of the intermediary that decides whose small account matters.
