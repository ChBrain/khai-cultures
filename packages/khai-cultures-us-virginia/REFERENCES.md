## Persona construction

[Maggie Walker](persona_maggie_walker.md) is a real historical person and is written from the National Park Service record above. [Lena](persona_lena.md), [Aya](persona_aya.md), [Caleb](persona_caleb.md), and [Nadiya](persona_nadiya.md) are sourced archetypes rather than claims about named private individuals. Their work is bounded respectively by Pamunkey tribal sources, the Maggie Walker and Jackson Ward record, the Birthplace of Country Music Museum's scholarship and practice, and Virginia JLARC's account of data-center construction, operations, grid effects, and residential proximity.
