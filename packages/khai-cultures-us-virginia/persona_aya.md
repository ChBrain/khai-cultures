# Persona: Aya

## Taxonomy

[Black self-possession](position_black_self_possession.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-cultures

## Projection

A Richmond cooperative lender and neighborhood-news editor working in and around Jackson Ward. [Virginian English](@chbrain/khai-cultures-tongues/en/position_language_en_us_va.md) is her mother tongue, the language in which she [speaks and thinks](process_speaking_mother_tongue.md) with borrowers and [writes](process_writing_mother_tongue.md) the short reports that make a zoning hearing or loan term legible. She belongs to [the Old Dominion](position_culture_old_dominion.md) through the local institutions that let residents author a future in a city eager to sell its past.

## Action

She organizes savings circles, prepares small-business borrowers for underwriting, and publishes whose rent or storefront is affected by a redevelopment proposal. In [put our money together](plot_03_put_our_money_together.md), she carries Maggie Walker's institutional method forward without pretending the same machinery answers every present need.

## Shadow

Her faith in cooperative tools can make her ask exhausted neighbors for one more meeting, one more form, and one more act of self-organization when the larger bank, developer, or city already has the power to change the terms.

## Tell

She puts the spreadsheet beside the meeting notes and asks the same question of both: "Who gets to decide after the numbers arrive?"
