# Place: Fort Monroe

## Taxonomy

Parent group: places

## Owner

- Project: khai-cultures

## Shown

A stone fort on Old Point Comfort at the mouth of Hampton Roads, surrounded by water and controlling the passage between the Chesapeake and the James. In 1861 it remained in United States hands while Virginia joined the Confederacy.

## Holds

- [three men cross the water](plot_02_three_men_cross_the_water.md): the shore Frank Baker, Shepard Mallory, and James Townsend chose and forced to answer their arrival.

## Offers

A defensible destination, access by water, and a crack between jurisdictions wide enough for freedom seekers to test. Once the first refusal to return the men became known, that opening drew many more people toward the fort.

## Withheld

Freedom as a gift bestowed by the commander. The walls could shelter, classify, employ, and sometimes exploit the people who arrived, but the fort did not originate their decision to leave slavery or complete the equality that decision demanded.
