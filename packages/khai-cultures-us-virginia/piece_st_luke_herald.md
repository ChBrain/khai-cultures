# Piece: the St. Luke Herald

## Taxonomy

Parent group: pieces

## Owner

- Project: khai-cultures

## Place

Published in Richmond by the Independent Order of St. Luke beginning in 1902 and circulated among members and readers beyond [Jackson Ward](place_jackson_ward.md).

## Load Bearing

The newspaper gives a mutual-aid organization its own channel for instruction, argument, advertising, and solidarity. It lets [Maggie Walker](persona_maggie_walker.md) address members without waiting for a white-owned paper to decide that their institutions are news.

## Apparent

A regularly printed newspaper whose columns carry organizational notices, editorials, reports, and advertisements for Black businesses. Its ordinary repeatability matters as much as any single issue: an institution can keep speaking next week.
