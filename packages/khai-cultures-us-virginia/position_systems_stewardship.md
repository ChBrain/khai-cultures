# Position: systems stewardship

## Taxonomy

Parent group: positions

## Owner

- Project: khai-cultures

## Has

The worker's knowledge that an abstract service is a chain of physical dependencies: land, feeder, transformer, water, generator, road, permit, maintenance interval, and neighbor. It has the operating discipline to keep a shared system stable and the public obligation to make its local costs visible before reliability is used as a complete answer.

## Orders

That scale be translated into consequences a resident can inspect. That growth account for the grid event, construction job, tax receipt, diesel test, watershed, and bedroom window in the same record. That authority over infrastructure include an answer to people who receive its burden without choosing its service.

## Loses

When an operator treats only the next contingency as real, when a developer calls a windowless building invisible, or when public debate imagines the choices are unlimited expansion and total refusal rather than enforceable terms about siting, power, water, noise, and pace.
