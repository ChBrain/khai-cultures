# Process: the string-band session

## Taxonomy

Parent group: processes

## Owner

- Project: khai-cultures

## Initiated by

Players who bring a tune, instrument, memory, or verse into a room and invite other ears to find a shared arrangement.

## Direction

One player states a melody or pulse, others enter by listening, and the group adjusts tempo, key, breaks, harmony, and length in real time. At a recording session, those choices must also fit microphone position, available time, and the side of a disc.

## Lever

The session lets music travel without remaining unchanged. Technique and repertoire pass between families, communities, racial traditions, churches, dances, stages, and broadcasts, while the ensemble makes those inheritances answer one another in a specific take.
