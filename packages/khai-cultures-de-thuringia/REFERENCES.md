## What defines this culture, and whether the play stages it

Asked when the line was rewritten, per `AGENTS.md`. The honest answer before the rewrite was
no. Taking the words the play uses about itself: the Arc opens on **die Thüringer
Rostbratwurst** and names **die Glaskunst des Thüringer Waldes** and **die Optikindustrie
Jenas**; the Name says **der bewaldete Freistaat, das grüne Herz**; the Stakes name
**Dürreschäden und Borkenkäferplagen**. Not one of those five had a plot. The line held three
plots, ran backwards - 1775 numbered before 1521 - and had neither an origin nor a present.

Each of the five now has one: 01 the bratwurst, 03 and 05 the glass, 06 the optics, 00 the
forest, 99 the dieback. The plot on the Bauhaus is the one addition not traceable to the
play's own words; it earns its place because Weimar produced the school and the Thüringer
Landtag drove it out inside six years, which is the same argument about inheritance that 04
and 08 are having.

The defining personas [Dieter](persona_dieter.md) and [Maria](persona_maria.md) are
archetypes representing the traditional Lauscha glassblower and the modern optical laser
engineer in Jena.
