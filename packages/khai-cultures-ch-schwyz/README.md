# Schwyz: a khai culture

Schwyz staged as a full khai play, ISO CH-SZ (a Swiss canton; the website derives its parent, `CH`, from the subdivision code), language de. The valley that gave a country its name and lost the say over it: its history as the plot line, its people as the cast, the office of being the name, its places, its practices, and its defining objects, all tuned by a single pitch.

## Estate

[khai-cultures](https://github.com/ChBrain/khai-cultures): the Cultures house that holds this culture and answers for it. This culture logs the house as its Estate; a culture with no Estate is not yet a production.

## The production

- **Anchor:** [play_schwyz.md](play_schwyz.md), the culture itself.
- **Pitch:** [pitch_schwyz.md](pitch_schwyz.md), written from Switzerland's profile inflected by a canton that is an origin and a minority at once, and naming no dimension.
- **Plots (history):** the four-century Marches quarrel with the abbey of Einsiedeln that ends in the raid of 1314, the imperial immediacy of 1240, the federal charter of 1291 that this town keeps, Morgarten in 1315 and the name that followed it, three centuries of the mercenary trade, the Sonderbund defeat of 1847, and the canton splitting into two landscapes since 2000.
- **Personas:** Beat (the Muotathal carpenter who lets a matter stay open until it is decided) and Svenja (the Höfe commuter whose canton is a tax rate and a postcode).
- **Positions:** die Schwyzer Kultur (a way of living Swiss culture, nested on the position and not on every persona) and der Name (the office of having supplied the word a country goes by).
- **Places:** der Flecken (the capital and the charter's house), Einsiedeln (the abbey it fought and walks to), die Höfe (the district on the other lake).
- **Processes:** die Wallfahrt (the walk to the abbey, which no quarrel ever interrupted).
- **Pieces:** der Bundesbrief (thirty lines of Latin behind glass), das Kreuz im Banner (the field sign that became a country's flag).
