# Finland: References

The provenance behind this culture: the Hofstede source data that tunes the pitch
(kept here, never in the prose) and the public-domain historical sources behind
the plots, personas, places, and pieces.

## Hofstede source data

The pitch [pitch_fi.md](pitch_fi.md) is written from Finland's profile on Geert
Hofstede's model of national culture and names no dimension and carries no
number. The scores and their source are recorded here so the data is on the
record without entering the prose.

The two defining personas, [Aino](persona_aino.md) and [Eero](persona_eero.md),
are archetypes, not historical individuals: composites that carry the tension
between the older generation (sisu, the sauna, comfort with silence) and the
younger, connected one (technology, the global world). [Solveig](persona_solveig.md)
is likewise an archetype and not a named person: the Swedish-speaking coast, and
Åland in particular, carried by someone who needs a bridge into the Finnish state.

The World Happiness Report ranking cited in
[plot_99_raja_ja_onnellisin_maa.md](plot_99_raja_ja_onnellisin_maa.md) is
published by the Wellbeing Research Centre at the University of Oxford with
Gallup and the UN Sustainable Development Solutions Network; it is referenced,
not claimed, and the plot reads it as a measure of trust rather than of joy.
