# fr_brittany

This package stages Brittany for the khai content house: the country that was a
duchy with its own parliament and its own law until 1789, that joined France by
a treaty with conditions rather than by conquest, and that is today four
departments of the five it counts itself.

The content is written in Breton (`br`), the tongue the culture stages, and the
culture-position nests on France. See [REFERENCES.md](REFERENCES.md) for the
sources, for the defining question, for why `geo.json` carries a `covers` that
disagrees with its anchor, and for the standing note on who has read the Breton.

See the root for instructions.
