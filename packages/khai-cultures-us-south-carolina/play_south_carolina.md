# Play: South Carolina

## Estate

[khai-cultures](https://github.com/ChBrain/khai-cultures): the house that holds this production and answers for it.

## Name

South Carolina, the Palmetto State, on the South Atlantic coast of the United States, Lowcountry below the fall line and Upcountry above it. ISO US-SC, language en.

## Arc

South Carolina did not become a slave society; it landed as one, out of Barbados with the island's code in the baggage, and within forty years its enslaved were the majority. Everything after runs on two lines at once. One is the keeping: the indigo fortune under a founder's name, the fort where the state began the war, the first historic zoning in the country and a city kept in soft lime mortar exactly as it was built. The other is the refusal of the people the code was written against: a march with drums on the Stono road, a pilot in the captain's hat steaming past Fort Sumter, a farmer in Clarendon County asking for a school bus and starting Brown. The state answered each refusal with a law and kept the law better than the refusal. Its present is a flag lowered after nine people were killed at a Bible study, and a museum on the wharf where the ships came in, raised on pillars off the ground it remembers, while the tide comes up the storm drains of the city around it.

## Triggers

**[Plot 0: the colony of a colony](plot_00_the_colony_of_a_colony.md)**

Planters' younger sons from Barbados land on the Ashley River in 1670 with the island's order already in hand; the slave code arrives before the colony needs it, and the enslaved are the majority within forty years.

**[Plot 1: Stono 1739](plot_01_stono.md)**

About twenty men take guns at the Stono bridge and march south toward Spanish Florida with drums and a banner, calling Liberty; the colony answers with a code that forbids the drum and the pen.

**[Plot 2: The Indigo Crop 1744](plot_02_the_indigo_crop.md)**

Eliza Lucas gets indigo to set at Wappoo after five years and a saboteur, on vats built by an enslaved carpenter and worked by people some of whom knew the dye before she did, and gives the seed away.

**[Plot 3: The Planter 1862](plot_03_the_planter.md)**

Robert Smalls, enslaved, takes a Confederate steamer out of Charleston in the captain's hat, gives the signals at Fort Sumter, and goes on to Congress and to the convention that takes his vote away.

**[Plot 4: The School Bus 1947](plot_04_the_school_bus.md)**

Levi Pearson asks Clarendon County for a school bus, is refused, and the petition that follows becomes Briggs v. Elliott, the first of the cases decided as Brown.

**[Plot 5: Mother Emanuel 2015](plot_05_mother_emanuel.md)**

Nine people are killed at Bible study at Emanuel AME, their families forgive the killer in open court, and the flag on the State House grounds comes down within three weeks.

**[Plot 99: the wharf](plot_99_the_wharf.md)**

The International African American Museum opens on Gadsden's Wharf in 2023, raised off the ground where tens of thousands were landed, a few streets from the district the city preserved first, as the tide comes into the low streets of both.

## Stakes

Whether a state this careful with its past can keep the whole of it, the wharf as carefully as the houses, and whether the low city that holds both will still be above the tide when it has.
