# Process: historic masonry

## Taxonomy

Parent group: processes

## Owner

- Project: khai-cultures

## Initiated by

Restoration masons and preservation architects in Charleston, called in when a joint has failed or a past repair in hard cement has started to crack the old brick around it, and only outside the hard frosts.

## Direction

On the wall itself: raking out the failed or cemented joints by hand, mixing lime and sand to match the original, wetting the old brick so it does not drink the water out of the new mortar, and pointing the joint back to the profile it was given when the wall was laid.

## Lever

The craft that lets a city be preserved in its own materials rather than imitated in new ones. It keeps the district's fabric honest and keeps a skilled trade alive, at the price of being slow, expensive, and dependent on a handful of people who still know how.

## Echo

It is the daily work of [Charlotte](persona_charlotte.md) and the reason the district stands, and it reads the walls the way nobody else does: a mason can see in a joint how fast it was laid and how well, and the city is only lately asking whose hands those were.
