# Persona: Charlotte

## Taxonomy

[historic stewardship](position_historic_stewardship.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-cultures

## Projection

A thirty-six-year-old preservation architect south of Broad, the fourth generation of a Charleston family in a city that asks that question before it asks your name. [South Carolina English](@chbrain/khai-cultures-tongues/en/position_language_en_us_sc.md) is the tongue she [speaks and writes](@chbrain/khai-engine-language/process_speaking_mother_tongue.md), and her grandmother still has the old Charleston vowels that Charlotte's own voice has mostly lost. The Gullah of the basket stands on the highway [reaches her only as a sound she knows and cannot follow](@chbrain/khai-engine-language/process_hearing_followed.md), though she has heard it all her life. She belongs to [the Palmetto State](position_culture_palmetto_state.md) by the brick: she can tell you who laid a wall by the joint, and only lately has she started asking whether the man who laid it was paid.

## Action

She repoints the old houses with [lime mortar](piece_lime_mortar.md) by [historic masonry](process_historic_masonry.md), sits before the board of architectural review, and keeps [the Charleston Historic District](place_charleston_historic_district.md) as it was built, and she walks clients past the museum on the wharf without quite knowing what to say.

## Shadow

Her care for the fabric can stand in for care for the people: she will fight a vinyl window for a year and has never once asked who can still afford to live behind the original glass.

## Tell

She runs her thumb along a mortar joint before she looks at the rest of the wall, and says the name of the street the house was built on before she says what it is.
