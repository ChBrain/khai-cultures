# Piece: the indigo cake

## Taxonomy

Parent group: pieces

## Owner

- Project: khai-cultures

## Place

At the drying sheds beside the vats on a Lowcountry plantation, then in casks on the Charles Town wharves bound for the dyers of England.

## Load Bearing

The cake is the crop in the only form that can travel. Leaf rots in a day; the blue that settles out of the steeped and beaten water, dried and cut into blocks, keeps for years and crosses an ocean. Take it away and indigo is a weed in a ditch, and the second fortune of the colony, the one that filled the gaps of the rice year, never comes.

## Apparent

A hard, dark block a few inches across, coppery on the surface where it catches the light and a deep blue when broken, smelling faintly of the fermenting vats it came out of, which smelled far worse.
