# Piece: the flag

## Taxonomy

Parent group: pieces

## Owner

- Project: khai-cultures

## Place

On the State House grounds in Columbia, first over the dome from 1961 and then from 2000 on a pole beside the Confederate monument, and since July 2015 in the state's Confederate relic museum.

## Load Bearing

The flag is what the state chose to keep flying over its government a century after the war it stood for was lost. It was raised for a centennial, at the start of the civil rights movement, and every argument about whether South Carolina had finished with its past was an argument about this cloth. Take it down and that argument has to be held about something else, which is what happened.

## Apparent

The red battle flag with a blue saltire and thirteen white stars, not the flag of the Confederate government but of its armies, on a pole that the compromise of 2000 had made movable only by a two-thirds vote of both houses.

## Yearbook

- Lowered on 10 July 2015 and carried to a museum after the killings at Emanuel, in [Mother Emanuel 2015](plot_05_mother_emanuel.md).
