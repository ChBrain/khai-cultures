# Piece: lime mortar

## Taxonomy

Parent group: pieces

## Owner

- Project: khai-cultures

## Place

In the joints of every old brick wall in Charleston and at Fort Sumter, and in the buckets of the restoration crews who still mix it by the old recipe.

## Load Bearing

Soft lime mortar is weaker than the brick it holds, on purpose: it gives as the walls settle and breathe in the damp, and it fails first, so the brick does not. Repoint an old wall with hard cement and the brick cracks instead. Without it the preserved city could not be repaired in its own materials, and the joints would be the first thing to tell you who built the walls and how.

## Apparent

An off-white, slightly sandy paste of slaked lime and local sand, with crushed oyster shell in the oldest of it, drying soft enough to scratch with a fingernail.
