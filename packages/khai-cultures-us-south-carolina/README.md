# South Carolina: a khai culture

South Carolina staged as a full khai play, ISO US-SC, language en. The Palmetto State: a colony that arrived as a slave society, the long refusal of the people it was built on, and a city that preserved its houses before it marked its wharf, all tuned by a single pitch.

## Estate

[khai-cultures](https://github.com/ChBrain/khai-cultures): the Cultures house that holds this culture and answers for it.

## The production

- **Anchor:** [South Carolina](play_south_carolina.md), the culture itself.
- **Pitch:** [the South Carolina key](pitch_south_carolina.md).
- **Plots (history):** the Barbados settlers of 1670, the Stono rising of 1739, the indigo crop of 1744, Robert Smalls and the Planter in 1862, the Clarendon County school bus of 1947, Mother Emanuel in 2015, and the museum on Gadsden's Wharf in 2023.
- **Personas:** Robert Smalls (real), Charlotte (preservation architect) and Elijah (Gullah basket maker, who belongs to the Gullah Geechee way), written in English.
- **Positions:** the Palmetto State, plantation resistance, historic stewardship, and South Carolina English from the tongues package.
- **Places:** Fort Sumter, the Charleston Historic District.
- **Processes:** historic masonry.
- **Pieces:** the slave code, the indigo cake, lime mortar, the school bus, the flag.
- **Plan:** the salt marsh plan.
