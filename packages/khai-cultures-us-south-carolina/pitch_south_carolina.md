# Pitch: the South Carolina key

## Taxonomy

The pitch of [South Carolina](play_south_carolina.md). It tunes the formal, story-first, carefully kept voice of the Palmetto State.

## Owner

- Project: khai-cultures

## Tenor

A story comes before a fact, and it comes in a register formal enough to be taken for manners: who your people are, which street, which church, and only then what you came about. The voice is warm and unhurried and exact about the past, especially about the parts of it that can be shown to a visitor. It keeps things, and it is proud of keeping them.

## Undertow

Under the manners runs the other half of the record, which the voice knows and has long spoken of less: who laid the brick, who stood at the vats, who was landed at the wharf. The undertow is not ignorance. It is a long practice of choosing which half to read aloud, and the slow, uneven work of reading the other.

## Nerve

What holds it is that it does keep things, all of them, eventually: the code is in the statute books, the indigo in the letter book, the wharf under the museum. A culture this careful with its record cannot entirely hide from it.

## Echo

A trowel in soft mortar, a church bell on the peninsula, and a high tide coming up through a storm drain on a day without rain.
