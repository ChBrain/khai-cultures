# Place: Fort Sumter

## Taxonomy

Parent group: places

## Owner

- Project: khai-cultures

## Shown

A brick pentagon on a made island of granite and rubble at the mouth of Charleston harbour, its walls battered far below their old height by the war, with a ferry from the city every day and a flag on the parade ground.

## Holds

- the last checkpoint of a stolen steamer in [The Planter 1862](plot_03_the_planter.md): the sentries a man in a borrowed hat had to answer before the open sea.

## Offers

The opening of the war, told on the parade ground to visitors who came on the ferry: the bombardment of April 1861, the surrender, and the four years of siege that left it rubble.

## Withheld

That the first shot of the war was the least of what passed here. The fort's brick was laid in part by enslaved masons hired out by their owners, and a year after the bombardment a man the state held as property steamed past its guns in the captain's hat and was saluted. The war began here over who could be owned; the ferry tells the bombardment.
