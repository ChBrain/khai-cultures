# Persona: Elijah

## Taxonomy

[plantation resistance](position_plantation_resistance.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-cultures

## Projection

A fifty-two-year-old basket maker who sells from a roadside stand on the highway north of the harbour in Mount Pleasant, on land his family has held since emancipation. [Gullah](@chbrain/khai-cultures-tongues/gul/position_language_gul.md) is the tongue he [speaks, hears and thinks in](@chbrain/khai-engine-language/process_speaking_mother_tongue.md), the one his family runs on; [South Carolina English](@chbrain/khai-cultures-tongues/en/position_language_en_us_sc.md) he [puts on like a coat](@chbrain/khai-engine-language/process_speaking_worn.md) for the tourists and the county office. The language of the tax assessor and the partition suit [reaches him like an echo from a long way off](@chbrain/khai-engine-language/process_hearing_followed.md). He belongs to [the Gullah Geechee way](@chbrain/khai-cultures-us-gullah-geechee/position_culture_gullah_geechee.md), which is a people and not a flavour of this state, and he lives and pays taxes inside South Carolina, where the land is assessed.

## Action

He coils sweetgrass by [coil de basket](@chbrain/khai-cultures-us-gullah-geechee/process_coil_de_basket.md), sells at the stand, goes to the hearings on [the salt marsh plan](plan_lowcountry_salt_marsh_conservation.md) to ask where the sweetgrass is supposed to grow, and keeps the deed papers his grandfather never had.

## Shadow

His wariness of every plan that comes from Columbia or the county, earned over four generations, makes him slow to trust the ones that would help him, and he has turned down a conservation easement because it came on county letterhead.

## Tell

He does not stop coiling when he talks to a customer, and he names the price in English and the basket in Gullah.
