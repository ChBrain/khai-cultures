# Persona: Robert Smalls

## Taxonomy

[plantation resistance](position_plantation_resistance.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-cultures

## Projection

A wheelman of twenty-three on the harbour boats of Charleston, born enslaved in Beaufort in 1839 and hired out by his owner, who takes most of his wages. He knows every channel, buoy and signal in the harbour, and he knows that the white crew think of him as part of the boat. [South Carolina English](@chbrain/khai-cultures-tongues/en/position_language_en_us_sc.md) is the language he [speaks and thinks in](@chbrain/khai-engine-language/process_speaking_mother_tongue.md); the writing he [only follows](@chbrain/khai-engine-language/process_reading_followed.md) at this point and will learn properly as a free man. He belongs to [the Palmetto State](position_culture_palmetto_state.md) in the way the state least wanted, as the man who knew its harbour better than it did.

## Action

He takes [Fort Sumter](place_fort_sumter.md) by being taken for the captain, then serves the Union as a pilot, buys the house he was born enslaved in, sits five terms in Congress, and argues at the convention of 1895 against the constitution written to strip him of the vote.

## Shadow

His faith that competence would be answered, that a man who had proved himself could not be refused, carried him through the war and into Congress and left him, in 1895, making a reasoned case to a convention that had already decided.

## Tell

He gave the signals the captain gave, stood the way the captain stood, and pulled the captain's hat down low; when he bought his master's house, he let the old widow back into it.
