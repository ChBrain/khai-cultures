# Piece: the school bus

## Taxonomy

Parent group: pieces

## Owner

- Project: khai-cultures

## Place

On the dirt roads around Summerton in Clarendon County, where white children rode to school and Black children walked, and where their parents ran a second-hand bus of their own until it broke down for good.

## Load Bearing

The bus is the request small enough that refusing it showed everything. Nobody in 1947 could call a bus a demand for integration, and the county refused it anyway. Without the bus there is no Pearson case, no Briggs petition, and the first of the cases that became Brown begins somewhere else, later, or not at all.

## Apparent

A used yellow bus bought for seven hundred dollars that the parents had pooled, old enough to need constant repair, carrying children who otherwise walked as far as nine miles each way.

## Yearbook

- Asked of the county by Levi Pearson and refused in [The School Bus 1947](plot_04_the_school_bus.md), which grew into Briggs v. Elliott and then into Brown.
