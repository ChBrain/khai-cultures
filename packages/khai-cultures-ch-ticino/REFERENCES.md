# References: Ticino

## The pitch layer

The pitch is written from Switzerland's national Hofstede profile, inflected by what it does to a linguistic minority on a border that it crosses daily: the same low power distance and high individualism, read through a place that has to explain itself in both directions. No dimension is named in the prose.

Source: Geert Hofstede, Hofstede Insights (the Country Comparison tool and the six-dimensions model of national culture), the national profile for Switzerland. No separate sub-scores for the cantons are claimed.

## Two decisions, recorded

**The Ticinese dialect gets no tongue file.** What is spoken in the valleys is Lombard, not Italian, and it has real distinguishing features. This house does not give a variety a file until someone can name its rule, give the minimal pair on both sides, and say whether it survives in writing - and that is a competence question, not a scheduling one. So both personas hold `it_ch`, which is what they read, write and work in, and the dialect is described where it belongs, in the culture-position, as the thing that enters no office. The gap is recorded here rather than filled badly.

**The magistri are staged and not cast, and no single one is named in the play.** Borromini, Maderno, Fontana and the Gaggini belong to the cities they built in as much as to the valleys they left, and their works are not on this territory. What is on this territory is the stone, the trade and the money that came back, and that is what the plot stages. Naming one of them and giving him a Projection would let this canton claim an authorship it has spent four hundred years not having.
