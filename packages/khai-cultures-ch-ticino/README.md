# Ticino: a khai culture

Ticino staged as a full khai play, ISO CH-TI (a Swiss canton; the website derives its parent, `CH`, from the subdivision code), language it. Lombard by geography, Swiss by decision, and a subject land for three centuries before either: its history as the plot line, its people as the cast, the office of being the border, its places, its practices, and its defining objects, all tuned by a single pitch.

## Estate

[khai-cultures](https://github.com/ChBrain/khai-cultures): the Cultures house that holds this culture and answers for it. This culture logs the house as its Estate; a culture with no Estate is not yet a production.

## The production

- **Anchor:** [play_ticino.md](play_ticino.md), the culture itself.
- **Pitch:** [pitch_ticino.md](pitch_ticino.md), written from Switzerland's profile inflected by a linguistic minority living on a border, and naming no dimension.
- **Plots (history):** the watershed that put a Lombard valley on the northern side, the conquest of 1403 to 1512 and three centuries as eight bailiwicks, the master builders who signed the European Baroque, the choice of February 1798, the emigration to California, Monte Verità in 1900, the Gotthard tunnel of 1882, and the cross-border workers since 2016.
- **Personas:** Marta (who does the arithmetic every month) and Arturo (who keeps a valley open where there is no work).
- **Positions:** la cultura ticinese (a way of living Swiss culture, nested on the position and not on every persona) and la frontiera (the office of a place where a border is a daily matter and not a principle).
- **Places:** Lugano (the square where the choice was made), Locarno (where one leaves from and returns to), Chiasso (where the border touches ground), il Monte Verità (the hill Europe came to).
- **Processes:** il grotto (the stone tavern, which works as long as the table is shared).
- **Pieces:** i tetti in piode (the stone-slab roofs that say which side of the mountain this is), il boccalino (an object living two lives at once).
