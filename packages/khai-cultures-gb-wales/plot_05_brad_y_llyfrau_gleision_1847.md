# Plot: Brad y Llyfrau Gleision, 1847

## Taxonomy

[play_wales](play_wales.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-cultures

## Cue

A child heard speaking Welsh in the schoolroom is hung with a piece of wood on a string and told to pass it to the next child caught, and whoever is wearing it when the day ends is beaten for all of them.

## Action

Into that country come three young English barristers, commissioned to report on the state of education in Wales, none of them speaking a word of the language of the people they are reporting on and much of their evidence taken from Anglican clergy in a country that had largely left the Anglican church. Their three volumes, bound in blue, find the schools bad, which they largely are, and then go further: they find the Welsh ignorant, dirty and immoral, and they name the cause as the Welsh language and the Nonconformist chapel. Wales calls it _the Treachery of the Blue Books_ and does not stop calling it that. The answer it makes is not a rebellion. It is respectability: the chapels swell, the Sunday schools teach a whole population to read, the temperance societies fill, and the eisteddfod is rebuilt into a national institution with a chair and a crown, so that the nation can be seen doing in public the exact opposite of what the blue volumes said of it.

## Stage

- [y capel](position_y_capel.md): the thing the report attacked and thereby made the centre of Welsh life for a century, egalitarian, self-governing, answering to no bishop and no squire.
- [the eisteddfod](process_eisteddfod.md): the answer given in the open, crowning the poet and the singer of any station before the whole nation.
- [yr iaith](@chbrain/khai-cultures-tongues/cy/position_language_cy.md): named in an official report as the cause of a people's degradation, and defended ever after as the proof it was not.

## Tension

The report was refuted by being disproved in public, and the cost of that is written into the culture. A people told it was dirty and immoral answered by becoming relentlessly respectable, and respectability is a hard master: it made the chapel strong and the Sunday closing law and the disapproving deacon with it. And the argument that English is the road out was not defeated by any of it. The parents heard it, and over the next century they acted on it.
