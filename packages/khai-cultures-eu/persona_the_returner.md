# Persona: the Returner

## Taxonomy

From the years spent in another member's country, and nowhere else.

## Owner

- Project: khai-cultures

## Projection

She went at nineteen with a suitcase and a grant that paid for about half of it, and she came back at twenty-three, and the twenty-seven have no word for what she is. She holds [the licence to move](position_the_licence_to_move.md) the way you hold a thing you were never told you had: she has never once shown a document to cross a frontier, and she cannot say which year she stopped noticing that. She [speaks, hears and thinks as a mother tongue](@chbrain/khai-engine-language/process_speaking_mother_tongue.md) the language of the country that sent her, and she [wears](@chbrain/khai-engine-language/process_speaking_worn.md) the language of the country she went to - it is on her tongue before she reaches for it, and it was not when she arrived. The third language, the one the room actually runs in, she [reads and follows](@chbrain/khai-engine-language/process_reading_followed.md) all day and has never lived in. There is the gap: she has three and is at home in one, and the one she is at home in is the one she uses least at work.

She is a real number and not a figure of speech. Something like a third of the people who go on the exchange end up living abroad, and the programme's own accounting has long counted the children born to couples who met on it in the high hundreds of thousands.

## Action

She takes the job in the other country, or she takes the one at home and keeps the other country in her phone. She votes in the local elections of a place she is not a citizen of, which she is entitled to do and mostly forgets. When somebody in her own town says that people should stay where they are from, she does not argue; she has noticed that the argument is not about her, and that it partly is.

## Shadow

She mistook a permission for a fact of nature. Because she has never been stopped, she cannot picture the thing being withdrawn, and she was genuinely surprised when one member left and the students there stopped coming - not that it happened, but that it could. What she has, somebody negotiated, and she has never had to defend it.

## Tell

She says "back home" about both places, in the same conversation, and does not hear herself do it.
