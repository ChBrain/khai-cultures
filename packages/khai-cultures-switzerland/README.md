# Switzerland: a khai culture

Switzerland staged as a full khai play, ISO CH. A **Willensnation**, a nation by
will, not by tongue or blood, held together across **all four national
languages**: German (`de`), French (`fr`), Italian (`it`), and Romansh (`rm`),
and across two confessions since the Reformation split the Confederacy in the
sixteenth century. Its history is the plot line, its people the cast, the roles
its society encodes, its places, its practices, and its defining objects, all
tuned by a single pitch.

## Estate

[khai-cultures](https://github.com/ChBrain/khai-cultures): the Cultures house
that holds this culture and answers for it. This culture logs the house as its
Estate; a culture with no Estate is not yet a production.

## Four languages, two confessions

The point of Switzerland is that it coheres without a shared tongue or a shared
faith, so the cast speaks all four national languages, each persona in its own:

- **German**: Wilhelm Tell, Huldrych Zwingli, Philipp Etter, Vreni, Urs
- **French**: Jean Calvin, Guillaume-Henri Dufour, Henry Dunant, Henri Guisan
- **Italian**: Marco (Ticino)
- **Romansh**: Gian and Menga (Graubünden)

The play and the shared instances are in German (the plurality, the play's
declared language); each region's personas declare their own `language:`. `de`
and `fr` are validated by the local detector; `it` and `rm` are registered in
`khai.languages` and routed to assisted verification.

## The production

- **Anchor:** [play_switzerland.md](play_switzerland.md), the culture itself.
- **Pitch:** [pitch_ch.md](pitch_ch.md), the Hofstede layer, written from
  Switzerland's profile (consensus, patience, distrust of central power,
  precision, the will to be one) and naming no dimension.
- **Plots (history):** the Rütli oath (1291), the Reformation and the Kappel
  wars (1523 to 1531), the federal state (1848), the Red Cross (1863), wealth
  and bank secrecy (1934), the language article (1938), the war years (1939 to
  1945), women's suffrage (1971), and the EEA vote (1992), each casting the
  elements it needs.
- **Personas:** Zwingli, Calvin, Dufour, Dunant, Guisan, and Etter as the
  historic cast, plus defining archetypes across the language regions and
  eras: Vreni and Urs (German), Marco (Italian), Gian and Menga (Romansh).
  Each is wired to the language engine by the crossing it holds (Dufour and
  Guisan carry the other national tongue; Marco wears German; Dunant forged
  the written word; Zwingli, Etter, Vreni, and Urs speak the dialect they
  never write).
- **Positions:** die Konkordanz (consensus power-sharing), der Milizgedanke
  (the citizen who serves), die Neutralität, der Konfessionsfrieden (the peace
  between two confessions since 1531), das Bankgeheimnis (bank secrecy since
  1934).
- **Places:** Bern (the federal city), Genf (Geneva, the francophone,
  humanitarian west and Calvin's Reformation), Zürich (Zwingli's Reformation
  and today's financial centre), die Surselva (the Romansh heartland), das
  Tessin (the Italian-speaking canton), das Réduit (the Alpine redoubt of
  1940).
- **Processes:** die Volksabstimmung (direct democracy), das Jassen.
- **Pieces:** das Taschenmesser (the army knife), das Fondue (one pot, many
  cheeses, the confederation on a plate), die Uhr (the watch, a Huguenot
  refugee's craft turned export wealth).
