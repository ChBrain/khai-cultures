# Instructions: Cultures

How a Playwright wires a culture into a play. You wire by linking your play's own
content to this house's positions; the house's files stay untouched. Which tongue
a persona holds is the tongues package's instructions, and how well they hold it
is the language engine's. This covers only which culture they belong to.
Authoring guidance, not runtime content, and it does not go on tour.

## Human

- The human decides which cultures are in the room, and that a persona may
  belong to more than one. Two belongings are not a contradiction and not a
  second passport: a Navarrese who speaks Basque at home holds both, and which
  one answers depends on who is asking.

## Agent

- From your play's own content, link the culture-position of every culture a
  persona belongs to. Where a sub-national culture applies, link that one and
  not its parent as well: the nesting lives on the position, which already
  carries the culture above it, so linking both says the same thing twice.

## Collaboration

- What belonging to a culture gives, orders, costs and drives is the
  culture-position's; which tongue the persona holds is the tongues package's;
  the width of their grip on it is the language engine's. A fact belongs to
  exactly one of them.

## Knowledge

- Each culture here is a full play staged as a culture: its own plots, its own
  cast, its own pitch, anchored by its own `play_`. You wire to its positions,
  never into its production. That is not a style rule but what containment
  means, and the house holds to it: of the cross-culture links it carries, every
  single one points at a position and none at a plot, a persona, a piece, a
  place or a process. A culture's cast is cast in its own scenes and answers to
  its own key; borrowed into yours it would answer to neither.

## System

- Do link the culture-position of every culture a persona belongs to, and more
  than one where that is true.
- Do link the sub-national culture where it applies, and let its own position
  carry the parent.
- Do not link a culture's plots, personas, pieces, places or processes. They
  belong to the play that stages them.
- Do not restate in your persona what the culture-position already says; carry
  only what is that person's.
- Do not edit this house's files; wire only from the play's side.
