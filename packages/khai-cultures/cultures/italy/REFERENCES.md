## The defining question

Per the house order on the defining question
(`management/orders/order_the_defining_question.md`), this play fared better than
most: Rome, the Renaissance, the Risorgimento and the boom are a civilisation arc
and not a constitutional one. The tell still found four holes, and all four were
words the Arc used about itself. **Campanile** and **famiglia** were named as
stronger than the state and neither was in a plot. **La televisione** was named as
what spread the common tongue and was in no plot at all. And **Nord e Sud** stood
in the Stakes with nothing staging it. All eight dead Company entries sat in those
four holes.

`plot_00` answers the origin with a name rather than an empire. Rome is already
staged as an empire at 01; what was missing was the reply to where this culture
comes from, and it is older and stranger than the empire. The Italic allies fight
Rome in 91 BC in order to JOIN it, and within two generations the peninsula has
one legal status and, under Augustus, one name. Italia exists as a word for
nineteen centuries before it exists as a government, which is why 1861 could
invoke a nation that nobody had to invent.

`plot_02` answers the campanile, which was the largest hole. After 1100 the real
political unit of the peninsula is the sworn city with its own walls, coinage,
militia and bell tower, and it stays so for seven hundred years. Campanilismo is
not a defect of character here; it is an accurate description of how power was
organised, and neither the competing courts of the Renaissance nor the difficulty
of the Risorgimento nor a family more solid than the state reads without it.

`plot_06` answers the television, and it is the one moment in this play where what
the country set out to do actually succeeded: Manzoni chose a spoken Florentine a
century before there was a medium able to carry it, and an appliance did in ten
years what the school had not done in ninety. What is gained and what is lost are
the same event, which the plot says rather than celebrates.

`plot_99` records the decade still resolving and hands the Stakes a narrower
version of their own question: not whether north and south hold together, but
whether there will be enough people to hold them.

The numbering is closed rather than gapped, as in the United Kingdom and
Liechtenstein: inserting the communes moved the Renaissance, the Risorgimento and
the boom by one each.
