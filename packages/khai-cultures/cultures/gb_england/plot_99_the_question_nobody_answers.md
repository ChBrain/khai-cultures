# Plot: The Question Nobody Answers, since 1999

## Taxonomy

[play_england](play_england.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-cultures

## Cue

The local shuts. On a street in a town whose work went a generation ago the pub closes and is not reopened, and around a quarter of the country's pubs have gone the same way since 2000, while the cafe along from it still does a cooked breakfast on a Saturday for people who want somewhere to be at nine in the morning.

## Action

The three other nations get their own bodies in 1998 and 1999 and England does not, and the attempts to give it one come to nothing: the North East is offered an elected regional assembly in 2004 and rejects it by close to four to one, and English votes for English laws is put into the rules of the Commons in 2015 and taken out again in 2021 having settled nothing. Meanwhile the ordinary institutions carry on carrying it. In the village [Edith](persona_edith.md) lays on the teas and keeps the parish ticking over and says mustn't grumble to anything that is asked of her; in the town [Liam](persona_liam.md) keeps the club, the round and the joke going in a place whose mills are flats now. Neither of them is waiting for a constitution.

## Stage

- [the pub](process_the_pub.md): the room where English belonging was audible in the buying of a round, closing at a rate the country notices one street at a time.
- [the full English](piece_full_english.md): the plate that is the same on a workman's table and a country-house sideboard, and is now the last cheap ceremony of a Saturday morning.
- [Edith](persona_edith.md): the village holding steady on unpaid, uncredited work and declining to say so.
- [Liam](persona_liam.md): the north keeping the loyalty and the humour after the work that made them has gone.

## Tension

England is the only one of the four with no body that speaks for it by name, and it has been asked twice whether it wants one and has not said yes. What it has instead are the institutions in this scene, which are older than any assembly and are shutting. Whether a country can go on knowing what it is through the pub, the parish and the game once those are the things going quiet is the question, and nobody in the scene is answering it, because answering it out loud would be making a fuss.
