# Plot: The Fry and the Gate, since 2007

## Taxonomy

## Owner

## Cue

The gate in the peace wall is still shut at night, twenty-five years after the guns stopped and past the year the walls were supposed to be gone, and on both sides of it the same breakfast is going on to the same pans, bacon, egg, sausage, soda farl and potato bread, with nobody on either side calling it anything but the fry.

## Action

The two hardest men of the two traditions sit down as First and deputy First Minister in 2007 and are photographed laughing. The institutions then go down in 2017 and come back in 2020, and go down again in 2022 and come back in 2024, and the place governs itself in the gaps by not very much. In 2021 the census reports more people of Catholic background than Protestant for the first time since the state was founded, and reports in the same breath that the largest single answer to the identity question is British, the next Irish and the next Northern Irish, which is three answers and no majority for any of them. Around a hundred barriers are still standing. Fewer than one child in ten is taught in an integrated school. [Davy](persona_davy.md) keeps the kirk and the Twelfth and wishes his neighbours no harm; [Niamh](persona_niamh.md) keeps the club and the language and wishes hers none either; each of them hears the other's heritage tongue as a flag rather than as speech, and they say all of it to each other in the same English.

## Tension

The settlement did what it was built to do, which was to take the gun out of the difference, and it did not do what nobody promised it would, which was to end the difference. What the last twenty years actually record is a place that has become normal in the ways that matter most and stayed divided in the ways that are easiest to keep: the government stops and starts, the walls stand, the schools are separate, and the breakfast is identical. Whether the shared ground can grow from the pan outward when the institutions above it keep falling over is the question, and both of the people in this scene are already living as though the answer is yes.
