# Plot: the decade of leaving, since 2016

## Taxonomy

[play_united_kingdom](play_united_kingdom.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-cultures

## Cue

On the twenty-third of June 2016 the country votes by fifty-two to forty-eight to leave the European Union. England and Wales vote to go, Scotland and Northern Ireland vote to stay, and the union that the play's Stakes said would be tested from within is tested by a majority of the whole against a majority of two of its parts.

## Action

Leaving takes four and a half years, three prime ministers and a parliament that cannot pass its own treaty, and it settles nothing about the border in Ireland that the Good Friday Agreement had made invisible: the protocol of 2019 puts a customs line down the Irish Sea, and the Windsor Framework of 2023 rewrites it rather than removing it. In 2020 the health service founded in 1948 becomes the thing the country claps for on its doorsteps at eight o'clock on Thursdays, and the same government that asked for the clapping is later found to have held parties during the rules it wrote. In September 2022 the Queen dies after seventy years, and the crowd that queues to file past the coffin stretches for miles along the Thames for four days and polices itself, which the country takes, correctly, as a portrait of itself. Then a prime minister lasts forty-nine days, prices rise faster than at any time since 1981, nurses and doctors strike for the first time in the health service's history, and in July 2024 the government changes by the largest swing since 1945. Scotland's case for leaving has receded since the Supreme Court closed the legal route in 2022; the case for Irish unification has not.

## Stage

## Tension

This is a record of a decade still resolving and not a forecast of it. What the vote settled was that the country would leave; what it did not settle was what the country is, and every institution in the play has been asked that question since and answered differently. The queue along the Thames and the queue for a hospital appointment are the same country a week apart. Where it goes from here belongs in the play's Stakes, which is a question, and not in a plot, which is a record.
