# Play: the United Kingdom

## Estate

[khai-cultures](https://github.com/ChBrain/khai-cultures): the house that holds this production and answers for it.

## Name

The United Kingdom of Great Britain and Northern Ireland, an island state off the north-west coast of Europe, a union of four nations under one crown and one parliament. ISO GB, language en. This is the union-wide parent culture; England, Scotland, Wales, and Northern Ireland follow as its constituent nation cultures.

## Arc

An island arrived at for two thousand years and never emptied, layered by every wave that took it, and left after the last conquest with two words for everything and an order of rank carried in how a person says them. It turned an offshore kingdom into a union of nations and then into an empire of a quarter of the world, and it learned to govern itself by binding the crown to the law rather than by overthrowing it. From Magna Carta to the union of the parliaments, from the jubilee that showed the empire to itself to the ships that brought it home, from standing alone in 1940 to building a welfare state for all and then to voting itself out of Europe, the British story is one of reform without revolution and continuity carried through change, told by a country that has usually settled things by taking back what it had offered. Under it runs a temperament of understatement and irony, a faith in fair play and the orderly queue, and an island self-sufficiency that looks outward to the world even as it keeps its own counsel.

## Triggers

**[Plot 0: The Conquest and the Doubled Tongue, 1066](plot_00_the_conquest_and_the_doubled_tongue.md)**

The last conquest replaces the whole ruling class and leaves the island with a French-speaking top and an English-speaking bottom, fusing after three hundred years into one tongue with two words for everything and [class](position_class.md) written into the choice between them.

**[Plot 1: Magna Carta, 1215](plot_01_magna_carta_1215.md)**

At Runnymede the barons force King John to set his seal to a charter that binds even the king beneath the law; [Westminster](position_westminster.md) and the rule of law trace their root to this moment, and [the Crown](position_the_crown.md) is told for the first time that it is not above it.

**[Plot 2: The Acts of Union, 1707](plot_02_acts_of_union_1707.md)**

The parliaments of England and Scotland join to make the kingdom of Great Britain, [Westminster](position_westminster.md) in [London](place_london.md) becomes the single seat of the new union state, and [the Union Jack](piece_union_jack.md) draws the joined kingdoms as one device.

**[Plot 3: The Diamond Jubilee, 1897](plot_03_diamond_jubilee_1897.md)**

The empire parades itself through [London](place_london.md) at its high-water mark, with [a cup of tea](process_tea.md) on every kitchen table grown on land taken for it, [the tongue](persona_shakespeare.md) gone out on the same ships, and Kipling in the same summer warning that it will pass.

**[Plot 4: The Finest Hour, 1940](plot_04_finest_hour_1940.md)**

With Europe fallen, the island stands alone under the Blitz; [Winston Churchill](persona_churchill.md) gives the resolve words, [London](place_london.md) takes the bombing and does not break, and rationing teaches [the queue](process_queuing.md) that orders itself without a marshal.

**[Plot 5: The Founding of the NHS, 1948](plot_05_nhs_1948.md)**

Out of the war comes a welfare settlement, and from [London](place_london.md) the union builds a health service free to all at the point of use, an institution shared across the four nations and the ordinary condition of things for everyone born after it.

**[Plot 6: Windrush, 1948 to 1971](plot_06_windrush_1948_1971.md)**

A fortnight before the health service opens, the Empire Windrush docks at Tilbury; the empire comes home by invitation, is met with lodging-house windows and a rivers-of-blood speech, and in 1971 the door is closed behind those already through it.

**[Plot 99: The Decade of Leaving, since 2016](plot_99_the_decade_of_leaving.md)**

The country votes to leave the European Union while two of its four nations vote to stay, and the decade that follows tests every institution in this play - the border in Ireland, [the Crown](position_the_crown.md) changing hands after seventy years, and a queue along the Thames that the country reads, correctly, as a portrait of itself.

## Stakes

Whether a union of four nations, held together by law and crown rather than by force, can keep its faith in fair play, reserve, and the common good as the world it once ruled changes around it and the bonds of the union are tested from within.
