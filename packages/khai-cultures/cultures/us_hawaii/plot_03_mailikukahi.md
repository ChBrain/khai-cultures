# Plot: Māʻilikūkahi divides Oʻahu, c. 1480

## Taxonomy

[play_hawaii](play_hawaii.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-cultures

## Cue

Māʻilikūkahi takes Oʻahu with its land boundaries in confusion and orders the whole island cut into named pieces: moku, then ahupuaʻa, then ʻili, each with an overseer answerable for it. The lines he draws are still on the maps.

## Action

The cut is not administrative tidiness, it is a water engineering decision written as law. An ahupuaʻa runs from the ridge to the reef, so that one holding contains rain, stream, taro terrace, shore and fishing ground. Nobody ends up with only the wet part or only the dry part, and nobody upstream can starve the people below without starving themselves. The population had been spreading into drier country and the resource arguments had begun; this is the answer to them.

He is credited with more than the survey. Tradition gives him the first code of laws here, the forbidding of human sacrifice on his island, and a rule that a makaʻāinana serving an unjust chief may simply leave.

> A commoner who can walk away is a limit on a chief, and it is the chiefs who wrote it down.

That last one matters more than it sounds. It makes the relationship between chief and commoner conditional on the chief's conduct, four centuries before any constitution, and it does it by the only mechanism available in a society with no courts above the aliʻi: exit. The ahupuaʻa is what makes exit survivable, because the next valley is a whole economy too.

This is what fills the long quiet between the settlement and the kingdom. It is not a silent period; it is an unwritten one, and its institutions are legible because they are still in the ground.

## Tension

A land system that ties every household to a whole watershed and makes neglect of the upstream immediately felt downstream - the strongest answer to a commons problem anyone here has produced. And its exposure: it has no written title behind it, only knowledge of where the boundaries run and who is answerable. When a legal order arrives that recognises only deeds, everything this achieved becomes invisible in an afternoon.
