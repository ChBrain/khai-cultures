# Plot: ka ʻōlelo Hawaiʻi, the count of speakers

## Taxonomy

[play_hawaii](play_hawaii.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-cultures

## Cue

When the first Pūnana Leo language nest opened in the mid-1980s, the estimate of Hawaiian speakers was about fifteen hundred, and most of them were old. The present estimate is around thirty thousand at varying fluency. It is the one number in this line that has moved the other way.

## Action

The record, not the hope. Hawaiian was made an official language of the state in the 1978 constitutional convention. The immersion preschools opened from 1983, and teaching in Hawaiian had to be made lawful again before they could run. The schooling now goes from the language nest to the doctorate, taught in Hawaiian throughout, which is held up elsewhere as the model for reviving a language. In 2019 the state supreme court held that the constitution requires reasonable access to that immersion education rather than merely permitting it.

What that buys is narrow and real: children who acquire the language at home and at school rather than studying it later. Everything before this plot is a count going down.

> This is the only plot in the line where a loss was reversed rather than absorbed.

It does not settle the rest. Thirty thousand at varying fluency is still an endangered language, the land questions the Māhele opened are open, and the apology of 1993 settles no claim. The people in it live with all of that at once.

[Leilani](persona_leilani.md) works the archive end, reading the kingdom's records in the language they were written in and arguing that sovereignty is carried in the stories and the tongue as much as in a border. [Keanu](persona_keanu.md) works the other end, on the water, holding the language through ceremony and the hull rather than the page - and the two of them barely follow each other's register, which is its own finding about a culture recovering in two places at once.

## Stage

## Tension

A language taken off the floor by the people who still had it, against the odds and on the record. And the size of what is still owed: the count is rising and the language is still endangered; the ʻāina of plot_00 is still mostly held by somebody else; and a revival measured in speakers cannot by itself answer a question about land. What this plot records is that the direction changed, not that the argument ended.
