## The defining question

Per the house order on the defining question (`management/orders/order_the_defining_question.md`), the Arc, Name and Stakes chapters were read against the plot line.

- **Answered by this pass, twice.** Nothing answered where the culture itself comes from beneath 1848; [plot_00_donar_eiche_geismar_723.md](plot_00_donar_eiche_geismar_723.md) now does, staged as the telling a hagiography gives. And nothing staged what the play's own present decade actually was: a first pass staged Rhein-Main rent pressure, which is real but was the comfortable answer, not the defining one. Between June 2019 and February 2020 this Land was the site of three linked events the play omitted entirely: the assassination of Regierungspräsident Walter Lübcke for defending refugee policy, the racist murder of nine people in Hanau, and the "NSU 2.0" letters that traced stolen victims' data back to Hesse's own police computers. A culture with a Gemütlichkeit position built on "alle gleich am Tisch" and a Demokratie position built on 1848 cannot pass over the one decade in which both were violently tested; [plot_99_rechter_terror_2019_2020.md](plot_99_rechter_terror_2019_2020.md) now stages that, as a record of what happened and who it happened to, not a forecast of where it leads.
- **A deliberate no.** The Name chapter's "stiller Odenwald" names a real, unstaged low mountain range in southern Hesse. It was checked and left as it stands: the rural/urban contrast it draws is already carried by the Gemütlichkeit and Ebbelwoi material the play stages, and the range's best-known literary association (the Nibelungenlied's Siegfriedsbrunnen tradition near Grasellenbach) is a modern touristic claim laid over a medieval, geographically vague epic, not a documented institutional throughline comparable to Geismar or the Paulskirche. Staging it would have been decoration standing in for a defining fact, so it is left as a descriptive word in the Name and not forced into a scene. "Die Mitte Deutschlands" in the same chapter is a plain geographic description of Frankfurt's centrality, not a claim requiring a scene of its own.
