# Plot: The Count and the Congregation, since 2011

## Taxonomy

[play_wales](play_wales.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-cultures

## Cue

A chapel built to seat five hundred is sold, and the notice in the window says flats; along the same street on a Saturday the rugby club is full, and the hymns the chapel taught are being sung by people who could not tell you what the words mean.

## Action

Wales votes in 2011, by nearly two to one, to let its Assembly make its own laws, and gets a Senedd. Then it counts itself. The 2021 census finds five hundred and thirty-eight thousand Welsh speakers, seventeen point eight per cent, down from nineteen per cent ten years before, and it is counted against a declared national aim of a million speakers by 2050, so the country now has a number to fail by and has published it itself. The last deep mine had already closed in 2008. In the country [Gwen](persona_gwen.md) lives her whole life in the language and still writes the world in English, because that is what the school taught her hand to do, and passes the tongue on to the children as the one thing that cannot be bought. In the Valleys [Rhys](persona_rhys.md) has the club, the place and the pride entire, and of the language a handful of school words and the anthem, and he stands in the eisteddfod his great-grandparents built and the strict metres shut like a door he has no key to.

## Stage

- [Gwen](persona_gwen.md): the square mile, the chapel and the hymn, and a Welsh hand that is slow because nobody taught it.
- [Rhys](persona_rhys.md): the solidarity of the coalfield without the coal, and hiraeth without the words that first named it.
- [the eisteddfod](process_eisteddfod.md): still crowning the poet before the nation, and now doing it in front of a country most of which cannot follow the poem.
- [rugby](process_rugby.md): the one place the whole nation still gathers, singing chapel harmony in a town with no chapel open.

## Tension

Wales is the only one of the four with a target for its own language written down, which means it is the only one that will be able to say precisely how it did. Everything the culture uses to hold itself together is still here and each of them is running on a supply it is not replacing: the hymns without the chapel, the solidarity without the pit, the eisteddfod without the readers. Whether the square mile can hand on what it has when the institutions that made it are the things going is what the Stakes are for, and the count will report either way.
