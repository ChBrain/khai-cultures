The two defining personas, [Colette](persona_colette.md) and
[Yasmine](persona_yasmine.md), are archetypes, not historical individuals:
composites that carry the tension between the older France of the terroir and the
art de vivre and the younger, diverse, urban Republic. The name ' Colette ' is
used here as an ordinary given name for an everywoman, not as a reference to any
real person.

## The defining question

Per the house order on the defining question
(`management/orders/order_the_defining_question.md`), the tell was run on this
play's own words, and it failed on the two the Arc leans on hardest. **Laicite**
is named in the Arc and again in the Stakes and stood in no plot: the principle
this culture is most often described by had no scene, only a position. And **la
langue francaise**, called a bien commun, was equally unstaged - the play held
the position and never showed where it came from. Two more followed: _le gout de
descendre dans la rue_, with `process_la_manif` dead, and _une elegance et un art
de vivre_, with the meal and the baguette dead beside it. Four plots stood here
and the line stopped in 1944.

`plot_00` answers the origin, and answers it with a defeat this country chose
rather than a victory it won. The Gauls are not the ancestors of the French in
any sense a historian would defend; they are the ancestors the Third Republic
selected, put in the manuals, and taught identically in the Cantal and in Dakar.
That is the most French fact available about French origins - that belonging here
is taught rather than inherited - and it is also, in the same sentence, the wound
the Stakes name, because the promise could be taken up or thrown back depending
on whether the child was permitted to mean it.

`plot_01` answers the language. It is a state act twice over, in 1539 for
administrative efficiency and in 1635 for the rules, and everything odd about how
this country treats its language - that its norm is public, argued over, and
defended by an institution - descends from those two. The cost is stated rather
than glossed: what is common is single, and the languages that were there first
retreated before the court and then before the school.

`plot_05` answers laicite with its law, and says what the law says rather than
what it is now taken to say: the Republic guarantees liberty of conscience and
stops recognising, salarying and subsidising any cult. The Tension carries the
part that is not settled - that the text has not changed and the meaning has
changed twice.

`plot_07` answers the street, which is not a mood here but an institution: a
revolt that lost the election and won the mores, after which no government can
govern on the ballot alone.

`plot_99` records the decade still resolving and gives the Stakes their hardest
form: the country defended its principles in millions and divided immediately
over what they oblige.

The numbering is closed rather than gapped, as elsewhere in this house: the two
prepended plots and the two inserted ones moved Versailles, the Revolution,
Dreyfus and the Liberation by one or two each.
