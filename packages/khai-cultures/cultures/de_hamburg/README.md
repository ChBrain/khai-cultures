# Hamburg (Freie und Hansestadt Hamburg): a khai culture

Hamburg staged as a full khai play, ISO DE-HH, language de. The second largest city in Germany: its history as the plot line, its people as the cast, the roles its maritime pride and merchant elite encode, its places, its practices, and its defining objects, all tuned by a single pitch.

## Estate

[khai-cultures](https://github.com/ChBrain/khai-cultures): the Cultures house that holds this culture and answers for it.

## The production

- **Anchor:** [play_hamburg.md](play_hamburg.md), the culture itself.
- **Pitch:** [pitch_hamburg.md](pitch_hamburg.md), the Hamburg key.
- **Plots (history):** the Hammaburg and Ansgar's mission north (c. 808 to 845), the Barbarossa charter of 1189, the 1241 alliance with Lübeck, the construction of the Speicherstadt in 1888, the development of the St. Pauli subculture, the 1943 firestorm, the 1962 North Sea flood, and the expensive decade of 2017 to 2024.
- **Personas:** Helmut (traditional patrician merchant "Pfeffersack" archetype) and Jan (modern automated terminal crane operator), written in German.
- **Positions:** der Kaufmannsstolz (merchant pride / Pfeffersack), das Tor zur Welt (gateway to the world), der Kiez (St. Pauli culture).
- **Places:** die Hammaburg (the buried Carolingian fortress), die Speicherstadt (historic warehouse district), die Reeperbahn (St. Pauli amusement street), Hammerbrook (the firestorm's epicenter, rebuilt as an office district), Wilhelmsburg (the flood island).
- **Processes:** der Fischmarkt (Sunday morning fish market).
- **Pieces:** das Barbarossa-Privileg (the charter of 1189), die Elbphilharmonie (modern concert hall monument), das Mahnmal St. Nikolai (the preserved war ruin), die Rote Flora (the squatted theatre at the center of the 2017 G20 riots).
- **Plan:** die Elbvertiefung (river deepening and port modernization plan).
