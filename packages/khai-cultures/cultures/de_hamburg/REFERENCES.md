# Hamburg: References

The provenance behind this culture: the Hofstede source data that tunes the pitch (kept here, never in the prose) and the public-domain historical sources behind the plots, personas, places, and pieces.

## Hofstede source data

The pitch [pitch_hamburg.md](pitch_hamburg.md) is written from Hamburg's profile inflected by its Hanseatic merchant pride and cosmopolitan worldview, naming no dimension and carrying no number.

Source: Geert Hofstede, Hofstede Insights (Germany comparison, adjusted for Hamburg's mercantile aristocracy, "Pfeffersack" ethos, and harbor subculture).

The defining personas [Helmut](persona_helmut.md) and [Jan](persona_jan.md) are archetypes representing the traditional patrician merchant elite and the modern harbor logistics class; the 1962 and 1943 plots extend them as living memory (Helmut as a child witness, Jan through his family's harbor-district lineage) rather than adding new personas for eras before either was born.
