# Place: Campi Raudii

## Taxonomy

Parent group: places

## Owner

- Project: khai-cultures

## Shown

Flat ground in the Po valley, somewhere near Vercellae in Piedmont. Open, dry in
July, and good for cavalry. Nobody knows exactly where it is: the location has
been argued over since the Renaissance and no site is agreed.

## Holds

- [The wagons at Vercellae, 101 BC](plot_04_the_wagons_at_vercellae_101.md): the end of the people, on ground the other side picked.

## Offers

Everything an army with horses wants and a people with wagons does not. The
Cimbri fought here in the morning sun and the dust, facing east, on a day chosen
for them.

## Withheld

Its own coordinates. The place where a people ended cannot be pointed at, which
is a fact about how thoroughly they ended: there was nobody left for whom it was
the place their family died, and so nobody kept it.
