# Persona: Boiorix

## Taxonomy

Parent group: personas

## Owner

- Project: khai-cultures

## Wears

A name that is not Germanic. Boiorix reads as Celtic - king of the Boii, or
king-of-the-strikers - which is what a federation looks like from the outside
after twelve years on the road: the people who came south out of Jutland picked
up others on the way, and one of them ended up leading.

## Carries

The refusal at Arausio and the choice at Vercellae. He is the one who takes the
victory on the Rhône in 105 and does not march on Rome, and the one who accepts
battle on a plain the Romans chose, in July, on ground that suited cavalry he did
not have.

## Says

Nothing that survives. What he is recorded as saying is what a Roman writer
needed him to say: a challenge to Marius, naming a day and a place, which is a
literary form and not a transcript.

## Wants

Land to stop on. That is the only motive the record gives the Cimbri that is not
an emotion attributed to them afterwards, and it was the first thing they asked
for and were refused.

## Fears

Nothing the record admits, which is itself the tell. A man who exists in the
sources only as an opponent is not allowed an interior, and the house does not
invent one for him.
