# khai-cultures: Cimbri

A people that ended, staged as a khai play out of what the living are holding.

This is the house's first **sunken** unit, and it sits in the collection of that
name beside `cultures/` and `groups/`. The count is taken over `cultures/` alone,
so a sunken play cannot move it by existing, and this one carries no ISO code
because no state recognises the Cimbri and none ever will. The type is defined by
`management/orders/order_the_sunken.md` in the house repository, named and not
linked: this collection ships, and that directory does not travel with it.

## The bar it had to clear

Two halves, both binding:

- **No successor community.** The Cimbri ended at Vercellae on 30 July 101 BC.
  The Cimbrian speakers of Luserna and the Sette Comuni call themselves Cimbri and
  are the descendants of medieval Bavarian settlers; they are a community with the
  name, not a successor to the people.
- **Already carried by a living culture.** Denmark holds the remains and says
  something about them, in `plot_04_gundestrupkarret_1891` and in the place and
  two pieces that plot casts.

## What it collects, and what it adds

It **collects** Denmark's remains by specifier — Himmerland, the Gundestrup
cauldron, the Cimbrian Bull — and **adds** its own company and its own line: two
personas the record actually attests, the plain where the people ended, a position
for what being Cimbri amounted to, and five plots running origin, the people's
end, and custody.

## What it refuses

That Himmerland is the heir. The archaeology finds no clear trace of a mass
migration out of Jutland, the cauldron is Thracian or Dacian work, and the bull is
from 1937. The remains are held, not inherited, and the sources behind every one
of those claims are [documented separately](REFERENCES.md).
