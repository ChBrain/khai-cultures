# Plot: The wagons at Vercellae, 101 BC

## Taxonomy

Parent group: plots

## Owner

- Project: khai-cultures

## Cue

On 30 July 101 BC, on [the Raudine Plain](place_raudine_plain.md) west of the Po,
the line breaks in the heat and the dust, and behind it the women are walled in by
their own wagons with the children and the dogs.

## Action

They do not get through. What the accounts describe next is a decision taken by
the people who were losing: the women kill the children and then themselves, by
the sword and by the wagon ropes, rather than go south as property.

[Boiorix](persona_boiorix.md) dies in the field. So does Lugius. The numbers the
sources give are enormous and unreliable and all point the same way, which is
that afterwards there were no Cimbri.

Some were taken. A people is not the sum of its survivors, and after that
afternoon nobody answered to the name.

## Stage

A plain chosen by the other side, in a season chosen by the other side. A ring of
wagons with no gap in it.

## Tension

This is the end, and it is the only plot in this line where the people are doing
the thing that ends them.

Everything before was done to them by somebody keeping records. Here they act,
and the act is their own extinction, and the account of it still comes from the
army that was on the other side of the wagons. A people that cannot be heard from
gets one moment of agency in the record, and spends it on refusing to survive.

Whether it happened as told cannot be recovered. That it is the one scene the
Romans chose to preserve in detail is itself a fact about who kept the record.
