## Where this play is thin, deliberately

Nothing survives of the Cimbrian language beyond names, so there is no tongue to
perform and the prose is in English, as `nato` and `the_four_nations` are and for
the same structural reason. No law, song, burial rite or ordinary day is recorded.
Every interior in these files is either sourced or marked as absent, and none is
invented to fill the gap.
