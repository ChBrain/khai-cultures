# Persona: The seeress

## Taxonomy

Parent group: personas

## Owner

- Project: khai-cultures

## Wears

White, and grey hair, and no name. Strabo describes the priestesses of the Cimbri
closely enough to say what they wore and what they did, and does not give a single
one of them a name, so the house does not either.

## Carries

The knife and the vessel, and the authority to use both on prisoners after a
victory. Whatever the Cimbri thought the future was and how it could be known,
she is the only person in the record who is said to have handled it.

## Says

What runs into the bronze. The account has her reading the blood as it streams
down, and the entrails after, and telling the people what is coming.

## Wants

To be right, presumably, which is the one thing about her that needs no source.

## Fears

Not recorded. What can be said is that she is the only Cimbrian the record shows
doing something other than moving or fighting, and that a whole people's interior
now rests on one paragraph by a Greek who was not there.
