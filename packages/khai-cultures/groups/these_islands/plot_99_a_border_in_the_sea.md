# Plot: A Border in the Sea, since 2016

## Taxonomy

[play_these_islands](play_these_islands.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-cultures

## Cue

People in Northern Ireland start applying for Irish passports in numbers nobody has seen before, which they have always been entitled to do and mostly had not bothered with, and the queue is not at any border because there is not going to be one.

## Action

One state leaves the union the other stays in, and the two hundred miles of land between them turn out to be the thing neither side will touch: no camera, no post, no checkpoint, on a border where a checkpoint was once a target. So the customs frontier is put in the water instead, between two members of this group, and the arrangement to manage it is renegotiated twice inside four years and satisfies nobody entirely. Everything about people, meanwhile, holds. The free movement carries on, the voting rights carry on, the health cover carries on, and in 2019 the two governments write the whole thing down for the first time in ninety-six years, having realised in the course of the argument that they could not point to the document. Anyone born in Northern Ireland may still be Irish, British or both, and a rising number choose the first while living in the second.

## Tension

The decade put the archipelago's oldest arrangement in front of a machine designed to sort people into unions, and the arrangement won: the frontier went into the sea rather than onto the road, and the movement of people was never seriously on the table. What that proves is smaller than it sounds and stranger. These islands can protect what they built together only by declining to look at it directly, and the moment anyone is asked to define the whole they are back at the beginning, with two states, five cultures, one archipelago and no noun that all of them will use.
