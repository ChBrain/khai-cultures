# Pitch: the key of these islands

## Taxonomy

Rummel about: [these islands](play_these_islands.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-cultures

## Tenor

Domestic. Not diplomatic and not tragic, though there is material here for both: the register of a family that sees each other constantly, has one enormous unresolved thing between them, and gets on with the visit anyway.

## Undertow

That the play cannot say its own subject's name. Every phrase available for this archipelago belongs to one party, so the title is a workaround borrowed from the one place both governments agreed to use it, and the workaround is the most honest thing on the page. A group that has to be introduced by a euphemism is telling you what it is before it starts.

## Nerve

Whether the intimacy is a settlement or an anaesthetic. Two centuries of movement have made these five cultures each other's relatives, and relatives are exactly who can leave a thing undiscussed for generations. This play does not decide whether the free movement healed the relationship or made it possible never to finish the argument.

## Echo

A woman at a boat terminal with two passports in the same bag, taking whichever queue is shorter, not thinking about it.
