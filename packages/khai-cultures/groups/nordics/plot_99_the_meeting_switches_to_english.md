# Plot: The Meeting Switches to English, since 1990

## Taxonomy

[play_nordics](play_nordics.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-cultures

## Cue

Four people in their twenties from four of the five countries sit down together, and after about a minute of everyone gamely speaking their own, somebody says the obvious thing in English and the rest are relieved, and the meeting proceeds in a language none of them was born into and all of them are fluent in.

## Action

The comprehension the students found in 1843 is measurably weaker in their great-great-grandchildren. Surveys of young Nordic speakers find them following each other's languages less well than their parents' generation did, and following English better than they follow their neighbours, and the practical consequence is that English becomes the neutral floor of the region: in business, in universities, in the youth exchanges the Nordic institutions themselves pay for. The right to use your own Nordic language in another's public office is still on the books and is exercised less. Meanwhile the region's other bindings hold or tighten: the passport union survives everything, Nordic television is watched across the borders it is made in, and in 2023 and 2024 two of the five give up two centuries of staying out and join the same alliance as the other three, so that for the first time since these countries existed they are all in the same security arrangement.

## Stage

- [the neighbour tongue](position_the_neighbour_tongue.md): the office that made the north exceptional, now practised by fewer people every decade.
- [Sweden](../../cultures/sweden/play_sweden.md): two hundred years of staying out of everything, ended in a season.
- [Finland](../../cultures/finland/play_finland.md): the member that always had to learn its way into the language family, and is now the one that argues most about whether to keep teaching it.
- [Janteloven](../../cultures/denmark/position_janteloven.md): the shared temper the five still recognise in each other instantly, in whichever language they are speaking.

## Tension

The Nordics are converging on everything except the thing that made them a family. The alliances line up, the markets line up, the model is still there, and the one arrangement no other group of countries ever had, a set of neighbours who could simply understand each other, is being retired by its own beneficiaries for a language none of them owns. Whether a region can go on being one region through a tongue borrowed from outside it is the open question, and the north is running the experiment without having decided to.
