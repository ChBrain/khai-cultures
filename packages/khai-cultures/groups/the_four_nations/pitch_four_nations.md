# Pitch: the four-nation key

## Taxonomy

Rummel about: [the Four Nations](play_the_four_nations.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-cultures

## Tenor

A crowd noise and a committee minute, in the same paragraph, taking each other perfectly seriously. The play is about four sporting associations agreeing a rulebook, and it is also about ninety thousand people singing, and the key is the one in which those are obviously the same subject rather than a joke about scale.

## Undertow

That this is the part of the union that worked. Four cultures spent four centuries being legislated into one and getting nowhere in particular, and the arrangement that actually settled how they live with one another was drafted by club secretaries to stop arguments about throw-ins. Nobody planned it, nobody would defend it from first principles, and it has outlasted two parliaments in Belfast.

## Nerve

The suspicion that a country which knows what it is on a Saturday and not otherwise has mislaid something, held against the evidence that it is doing fine. This play will not settle whether four teams are a substitute for an answer or the answer itself, because the four have not settled it either and have stopped expecting to.

## Echo

Four press conferences on the same afternoon, four sets of numbers, and a phrase borrowed from a fixture list to describe a government.
