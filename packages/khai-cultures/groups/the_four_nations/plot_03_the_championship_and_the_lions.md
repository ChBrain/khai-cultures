# Plot: The Championship and the Lions, 1883 to 1984

## Taxonomy

[play_the_four_nations](play_the_four_nations.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-cultures

## Cue

From the season of 1883 the four play each other every year, all six fixtures, for a trophy, and go on doing it for a hundred and one years, which makes it the oldest international tournament in any sport anywhere.

## Action

For most of a century it is the fixture the calendar is built around, and it does a thing no institution of the Union does: it makes the four look at each other rather than at the centre. Then it stops. The reasons are ordinary and they are also the century's: the fixture list gets crowded, the money moves to European and world competitions, crowds misbehave, and in 1981 England and Wales decline to travel to Belfast, so a tournament for the four is played by three. It is abolished in 1984. What outlives it is the other arrangement, made in rugby and running the other way: a touring side drawn from all four and from the Republic as well, which since the 1880s has gone to the southern hemisphere as one team. The four stopped playing each other and kept the side that includes the country that left, and in the same code Ireland has always fielded one team for the whole island, north and south, through everything.

## Stage

- [Northern Ireland](../../cultures/gb_northern_ireland/play_northern_ireland.md): the fixture two of the others would not travel to, and the place whose rugby has never been partitioned.
- [rugby](../../cultures/gb_wales/process_rugby.md): the code that built the side uniting the archipelago, while the other code was dismantling the one that divided it.
- [Wales](../../cultures/gb_wales/play_wales.md): a nation that assembles itself around a fixture, and had a century of them.
- [England](../../cultures/gb_england/play_england.md): one of the two that would not travel, and the association the abolished trophy had belonged to longest.

## Tension

The four could not keep a competition between themselves going through the Troubles, and in the same decades a side that ignores the border entirely toured every four years without controversy. Sport is not above the politics; it is where the politics is negotiated in a form people will accept. The annual fixture failed because it made the four face each other at the worst possible moment, and the Lions held because it asked them to face outward together, which turns out to be the only arrangement in this archipelago that has never needed defending.
