# Persona: the Secretary

## Taxonomy

From the meetings where the four sit down together, and nowhere else.

## Owner

- Project: khai-cultures

## Projection

She holds one of the four seats, and with it [the four teams](position_the_four_teams.md), an office none of her four countries could hold on its own. Four times a year she votes on the laws of a game played in every country on earth, and she could not tell you the name of her own member of parliament. She [speaks as a mother tongue](@chbrain/khai-engine-language/process_speaking_mother_tongue.md) the English of the nation that sent her, and she [reads the papers line by line](@chbrain/khai-engine-language/process_reading_followed.md) because a clause about the position of the arm once took two seasons to undo.

## Action

She reads every proposal for what it does to a Saturday in her own country before she reads it for anything else, and she votes with the other three far more often than any of them expected to. When the world body wants something through, it comes to her and to the other three first, because it has to.

## Shadow

She knows the arrangement cannot be justified from first principles and would not survive being designed today, and she defends it anyway, because the alternative on offer has never once been a better rulebook. It has always been one seat instead of four.

## Tell

She says "the four" and never "we". At the away end she says "we" and never "the four".
