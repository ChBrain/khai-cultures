# Play: the Four Nations

## Estate

[khai-cultures](https://github.com/ChBrain/khai-cultures): the house that holds this production and answers for it.

## Name

The Four Nations: England, Scotland, Wales and Northern Ireland, the four cultures of one state, taken as the group they form with each other rather than as the state above them.

## Arc

One state that fields four teams, which no other state does, and which the four arranged themselves before anybody thought to ask whether it was allowed. What they made together is not the union, which was made for them and above them; it is a set of codified games and the bodies that govern them, built in the same three decades in taverns and hotel rooms, and still binding on the rest of the world. The first international football match ever played was between two of them. The board that writes the laws of world football is still half theirs. For a century they played each other every year for a trophy, and when that ended the side that outlived it was the one that adds the country that left. The Union gave the four a parliament and told them they were one; the games gave them four teams and told them they were four, and it is the games that have proved harder to reform.

## Triggers

**[Plot 0: The Games Before the Rules, before 1860](plot_00_the_games_before_the_rules.md)**

Parish against parish, with no referee and no two places agreeing: the archipelago is already playing versions of the same game everywhere, and codification will not invent it but decide whose version won.

**[Plot 1: The First International, 1872](plot_01_the_first_international_1872.md)**

The first international football match in the history of the world is played at a cricket ground in Partick between two nations of the same state, and finishes goalless.

**[Plot 2: The Board That Writes the Laws, 1886](plot_02_the_board_that_writes_the_laws_1886.md)**

The four associations form a board to agree one set of laws between them, and a hundred and forty years later that board still writes the laws of world football with half the votes in the four's hands.

**[Plot 3: The Championship and the Lions, 1883 to 1984](plot_03_the_championship_and_the_lions.md)**

The four play each other every year for a century, and when the fixture finally dies the side that outlives it is the one that adds the country that left.

**[Plot 99: Four Teams, Four Rules, since 1999](plot_99_four_teams_four_rules.md)**

Devolution gives the four what the fixture list gave them in 1884, and a pandemic runs the country the way the sport always had: four sets of rules, four announcements, one island in the middle of it.

## Stakes

Whether four cultures that built themselves a way of being four inside one state, and built it out of games rather than out of law, still need the state to hold them together at all, or whether the arrangement they made for the pitch has quietly become the one they live by, with each nation more certain of what it is on a Saturday than on a ballot.
