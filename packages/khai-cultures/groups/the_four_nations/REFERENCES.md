# The Four Nations: References

The provenance behind this group. A group carries no Hofstede data of its own (it
has no single national profile); it is a referencing entry over its member
cultures, and it carries a pitch because a pitch is a tone and not a score.

## The grouping

"The four nations" is ordinary usage for this set in British political and
sporting language, and became the standard term for the divergent public-health
regimes of 2020 to 2022. The membership recorded in the registry is
**build-derived** from the member plays this group casts, not authored by hand.

| ISO    | Nation           | Member culture                                                                     |
| ------ | ---------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| GB-ENG | England          | [gb_england](../../cultures/gb_england/play_england.md)                            |
| GB-SCT | Scotland         | [gb_scotland](../../cultures/gb_scotland/play_scotland.md)                         |
| GB-WLS | Wales            | [gb_wales](../../cultures/gb_wales/play_wales.md)                                  |
| GB-NIR | Northern Ireland | [gb_northern_ireland](../../cultures/gb_northern_ireland/play_northern_ireland.md) |

The state above them is a culture in its own right,
[united_kingdom](../../../khai-cultures-united-kingdom/play_united_kingdom.md),
and stages Britishness. This group stages what the four did with each other, which
is not the Union.

Ireland is **not** a member and is deliberately **not linked**, though plot 3 turns
on it: the touring side that outlived the four's own championship is the one that
adds the Republic, and rugby on that island has never been partitioned. It is
named in prose and nowhere cast, because the registry derives a group's
`references` from exactly what its files link, and the design record treats those
references as the membership. A link here would have put Ireland in the United
Kingdom's group, which is wrong in a way a website would render.

## A note on the arc

`management/design/groups-and-references.md` §2.1.1 warns that a group is the
easy place to get `order_the_passport.md` wrong, "because the obvious material for
an alliance is the alliance". The obvious material here is the Acts of Union,
Westminster and devolution, all of which belong to the culture above rather than
to the four together, and none of which is staged. Every Cue in this group has a
non-state subject: a parish at Shrovetide, four thousand people at a turnstile,
four associations with four rulebooks, a fixture played every year for a century,
and four sides lining up for four anthems.
