# Plot: The Games Before the Rules, before 1860

## Taxonomy

[play_the_four_nations](play_the_four_nations.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-cultures

## Cue

On a saint's day, in a town in any of the four, most of the men turn out and try to carry a ball to one end of the parish while most of the other men try to carry it to the other, and there is no pitch, no referee, no limit on numbers and no written rule anywhere.

## Action

They are all playing it and none of them is playing the same one. Shrovetide football goes through the streets of Ashbourne over two days with the goals three miles apart; the Ba' is thrown up in Kirkwall at Christmas and again at New Year and goes wherever the pack takes it; _cnapan_ is played across the fields of Pembrokeshire with hundreds a side and men on horseback; _caid_ is played across Ireland. The variation is not decoration. Every town has its own settled version and none of them can play another town without an argument first, which is a tolerable state of affairs while nobody travels and an intolerable one the moment the railways mean they can.

## Stage

- [England](../../cultures/gb_england/play_england.md): the Shrovetide games of the market towns, and later the schools that will write the first rules down.
- [Scotland](../../cultures/gb_scotland/play_scotland.md): the Ba' in the northern burghs, played through the streets and still played.
- [Wales](../../cultures/gb_wales/play_wales.md): _cnapan_ in the west, hundreds a side across open country.
- [Northern Ireland](../../cultures/gb_northern_ireland/play_northern_ireland.md): the Ulster end of a game the whole island played as _caid_ before either code came.

## Tension

Nothing here was invented in the 1860s. What the codifiers did was not make a game but settle an argument, and the argument was between four peoples who had each been playing their own answer for centuries. That is why the codes came out four-shaped rather than one-shaped: the bodies that wrote them were already four, and the fact that they agreed on the laws while refusing to merge into a single team is the whole of what this group is.
