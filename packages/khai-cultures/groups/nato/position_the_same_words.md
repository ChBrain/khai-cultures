# Position: the same words

## Taxonomy

Parent group: positions

## Owner

- Project: khai-cultures

## Has

The ability to be understood, in a hurry, by somebody whose language you do not speak, because the two of you were taught the same procedure. It is not a language and it is not translation. It is a much smaller and much stranger thing: an agreed set of words, shapes and fittings, so narrow that no conversation is possible through it and so exact that no misunderstanding is either.

It is written down in several thousand documents that almost nobody has read, agreeing things at the altitude of a hose coupling: the diameter of a refuelling nozzle, the size of a pallet, the marking on a casualty tag, the sequence for handing over control of an aircraft, the dimensions of a rifle cartridge. Each one is trivial. Together they are the difference between thirty-two armies and one.

Its most successful piece escaped entirely. The alphabet that runs _Alpha Bravo Charlie_ was worked out for civil aviation and adopted by the alliance, and it is now spoken every day by pilots, coastguards, police and call-centre staff on every continent - most of whom have never thought about where it came from, and would be surprised.

## Orders

That the standard beats the preference, every time and without appeal. A member may have a better nozzle; it fits the agreed one anyway. Everything the office orders comes from that single submission, and so does the resentment it generates, because the standard is usually somebody else's and everybody knows whose.

## Loses

Ground to every reason not to bother. It loses to national procurement, which is where the money and the jobs are; to the argument that this war will not be that war; and to the long peace, in which a fitting that has never had to fit is indistinguishable from an expense. It is cheapest to abandon on exactly the day it would have mattered.

## Drives

It drives the people who would be holding the hose. Nobody signs a standardisation agreement out of conviction, and nobody who has ever refuelled a stranger's aircraft in the dark needs to be persuaded of one.
