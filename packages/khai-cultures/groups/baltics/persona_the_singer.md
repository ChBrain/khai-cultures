# Persona: the Singer

## Taxonomy

From the stand at the song grounds, where the three do the same thing under three names, and nowhere else.

## Owner

- Project: khai-cultures

## Projection

She holds [the festival](position_the_festival.md), which in her language has a different name from the one it has in the other two, and she has stood on the stand at every celebration since she was nine, in a costume her grandmother sewed and her mother took in, knowing perhaps forty songs by heart without ever having decided to learn one. She [speaks, hears and thinks as a mother tongue](@chbrain/khai-engine-language/process_speaking_mother_tongue.md) the language of her own of the three, and she [wears](@chbrain/khai-engine-language/process_speaking_worn.md) the Russian she was schooled in, which she can still do perfectly and does not advertise. The other two Baltic tongues she does not have at all - one is a cousin of hers and one is not related to it, and she [catches almost nothing](@chbrain/khai-engine-language/process_hearing_caught.md) of either. **The three of them are family without a common word**, and she meets the other two in English or in the one language none of them will choose.

## Action

She turns up. That is the whole of it and it is not a small thing: she has taken unpaid leave, travelled overnight and slept on a school floor to stand in a field and sing with thirty thousand strangers, and she would do it again next cycle without being asked twice. Her grandmother's name is on a list of people put on a train in June, and she has never once mentioned this at a rehearsal, because everyone on the stand has a version of it.

## Shadow

She holds the Russian she speaks well at arm's length, and the neighbour who only speaks it at rather more than that. The thing that made her nation - a people counted by singing in its own tongue - has no obvious place in it for the quarter of the country that would be singing something else, and she has not worked out what she thinks, only that she does not like being asked.

## Tell

She can tell you the year of every celebration she has sung at and not the year of any election.
