# Plot: The Last Cord, February 2025

## Taxonomy

[play_baltics](play_baltics.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-cultures

## Cue

A grid engineer watches a number on a screen that has to stay at fifty. For one weekend in February 2025 the three run as an island - connected to nobody, holding their own frequency by themselves - and then re-connect westward. Nothing is announced by anyone singing. It is the most consequential thing the three have done together since the chain, and it is a wiring job.

## Action

The three had been independent for thirty-four years and had spent all of them on Moscow's electricity clock. The physical synchronisation was the last Soviet-era system still running: whatever the treaties said, the alternating current in a Vilnius kitchen was being kept in step from a control room in Russia. Undoing it took years of substations, converters and rehearsal, and it was done by the three simultaneously because there is no way to do it one at a time.

That is what the group looks like now. It has become an engineering relationship - a rail gauge being changed, a grid being reclocked, a border being fenced - conducted by people in high-visibility jackets rather than by people in a field. It is less moving and it is a great deal harder to reverse.

The other half of the present is not shared and does not resolve. Roughly a fifth to a quarter of the people in the two northern countries speak Russian at home, a legacy of the occupation that ended and the population movement that did not, and tens of thousands still hold the grey passports of a person who is a citizen of nowhere. In the southern country it is a few per cent and barely an issue. [The Singer](persona_the_singer.md) speaks the language perfectly, learned at school, and does not advertise it; she has not worked out what she thinks about the neighbour who speaks only that, and does not like being asked.

And [the festival](position_the_festival.md) still fills the grounds. It is on a heritage list now, which is a compliment with a hook in it: an institution the world has come to admire is one step from being a thing visitors watch rather than a thing a people does.

## Stage

- [the festival](position_the_festival.md): still on its cycle, still filling the stand, now also a heritage listing and a tourist season.
- [the Singer](persona_the_singer.md): who can name the year of every celebration she has sung at and not the year of any election.
- [Estonia](../../cultures/estonia/play_estonia.md), [Latvia](../../cultures/latvia/play_latvia.md) and [Lithuania](../../cultures/lithuania/play_lithuania.md): three grids cut loose on the same weekend, because there was no way to do it one at a time.

## Tension

The bond was made by a shared object - the same hand, the same dates, the same hour to pack - and the Stakes have always been whether it survives the object going away. The answer so far is that it did not have to find out, because the object came back in a different shape, and the three responded by wiring themselves together rather than by singing. **What is genuinely unresolved is the other thing.** The instrument that made these nations counts a people singing in its own tongue, and a fifth of two of these countries would be singing something else. Nobody has an answer to that which is both honest and comfortable, and the play does not pretend to have one.
