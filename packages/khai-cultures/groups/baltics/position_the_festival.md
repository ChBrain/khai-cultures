# Position: laulupidu, dziesmu svētki, dainų šventė

## Taxonomy

Parent group: positions

## Owner

- Project: khai-cultures

## Has

One institution under three names, in three languages, two of which are not related to the third. Every few years each of the three nations empties a proportion of itself into a field and sings in unison for two days: tens of thousands of singers on the stand, a crowd larger than most of their cities in front of it, and repertoire everyone present has known since school.

The scale is the part outsiders do not believe. An Estonian song celebration puts something like twenty-five to thirty thousand people on the stand at once, in a country of one and a third million. There is no equivalent anywhere: a measurable fraction of a whole nation singing the same words at the same moment, by arrangement, on a regular cycle, for over a century and a half.

It was permitted, and that is why it exists. German barons and Russian governors licensed choral singing because choral singing is not politics, and by the time anybody understood what had been licensed there were a hundred thousand people who knew the words. **What the three have instead of a shared language is a shared method**: each was constituted as a nation by people standing in a field singing in a tongue their rulers considered a peasant dialect.

## Orders

That the count is the argument. The festival does not petition, resolve or demand; it assembles, and being assembled is the whole statement. It orders participation over performance - the stand is filled by amateurs out of villages, not by professionals - because a professional choir would prove nothing and a hundred village choirs prove exactly the thing in question.

## Loses

Ground to being made official. It survived censors by being cultural and would not survive being made a ministry's, because an assembly convened by the state counts nothing. It loses to success too: an institution on a heritage list is one step from being a thing tourists watch rather than a thing a people does.

## Drives

It drives everybody who has ever stood on that stand, and it is the reason the freedom, when it came, arrived as singing rather than as a demonstration. Nobody in the three had to be taught how to gather a hundred thousand people who already knew the words.
