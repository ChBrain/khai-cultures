# Play: the Baltics

## Estate

[khai-cultures](https://github.com/ChBrain/khai-cultures): the house that holds this production and answers for it.

## Name

The Baltics, the three states on the eastern shore of the Baltic Sea: Estonia (EE), Latvia (LV), and Lithuania (LT), two Baltic tongues and one Finnic, read by the world as one.

## Arc

Three small nations on the same exposed shore that share one fate almost to the year. All three won independence around 1918, all three were swallowed by the Soviet Union in 1940, all three endured the deportations and the long occupation, and all three sang themselves free again in 1989-91, then walked together into the European Union, NATO, and the euro. What binds them is that shared arc of independence, annexation, and deliverance, and the insistence that the occupation was never lawful; what divides them is almost everything else: Latvian and Lithuanian are kin while Estonian is Finnic and looks north to Finland, Lithuania is Catholic and once a great power while the north is Lutheran and was long ruled by German barons. A group that shows a common catastrophe and a common deliverance can bind three peoples a shared language never did.

## Triggers

**[Plot 0: The Song Grounds, from 1869](plot_00_the_song_grounds.md)**

A farmhand walks two days to sing in a language his rulers call a peasant dialect; eight hundred and fifty singers and fifteen thousand listeners assemble, because a choir is culture and culture is permitted - and [the festival](position_the_festival.md) turns out to be an instrument for counting a people.

**[Plot 1: An Hour to Pack, 1941 and 1949](plot_01_the_hour_to_pack.md)**

Somebody knocks before dawn and a family has an hour; forty thousand are taken from the three in one week in 1941 and some ninety thousand in 1949, by the same hand on the same dates - which is what welds the three together, since they are not alike and never arranged it.

**[Plot 2: The Night Song Festivals, 1987 to 1988](plot_02_the_night_song_festivals_1988.md)**

People drive to the song grounds after work for no announced reason and sing until it is light, until a quarter of a nation is in one field; a column can clear a square and can do nothing at all with a hundred thousand people who are only singing.

**[Plot 3: Baltijos kelias 1989](plot_03_baltijos_kelias_1989.md)**

Two million people join hands in an unbroken chain of some six hundred and seventy-five kilometres from Tallinn through Riga to Vilnius, on the fiftieth anniversary of the pact that erased the three from the map.

**[Plot 99: The Last Cord, February 2025](plot_99_the_last_cord.md)**

The three cut the last Soviet-era system still running - the electricity clock they had stayed on for thirty-four years of independence - in one weekend, together, because there is no way to do it one at a time; the bond is now an engineering relationship, and the question of who is inside the nation is not resolved.

## Stakes

Whether three nations bound by one occupation and one deliverance stay a family now that the threat which united them wears a new face, or whether Estonia's gaze north and Lithuania's memory of greatness pull the three apart once the shared danger recedes.
