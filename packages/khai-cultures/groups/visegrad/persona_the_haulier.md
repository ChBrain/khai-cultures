# Persona: the Haulier

## Taxonomy

From the road that runs through all four and stops in none of them, and nowhere else.

## Owner

- Project: khai-cultures

## Projection

He drives the corridor: north to the Baltic, south to the Adriatic, and the long
run west, and he crosses three of the four countries in a day without his engine
going cold. He holds [the room next door](position_the_room_next_door.md) the way
his trade always has - **decisions about his route are made where he is not, and he
finds out at the barrier.** He [speaks, hears and thinks as a mother
tongue](@chbrain/khai-engine-language/process_speaking_mother_tongue.md) one of the
four languages, and of the other three he has two that are cousins of his and one
that is related to nothing here at all. He [catches what the ear
gets](@chbrain/khai-engine-language/process_hearing_caught.md) of the cousins - the
numbers, the road words, enough - and of the fourth he has nothing. What he
[wears](@chbrain/khai-engine-language/process_speaking_worn.md) is the freight
English of the paperwork, four hundred words wide. **Four neighbours who have been
governed together for four hundred years, and the only language they can all use is
one from outside.**

## Action

He drives, and he knows which crossing is slow this week and why, which is a form
of political intelligence nobody credits as one. When a border is closed by a
decision taken in a capital he was not in, he parks and waits with everybody else
and finds out from the other drivers.

## Shadow

He mistakes knowing the road for knowing the map. Because he can predict a queue,
he believes he can predict the arrangement behind it, and the arrangements are made
by people he will never meet on grounds that have nothing to do with freight.

## Tell

He gives distances in hours, never kilometres, and adjusts for the border without
saying so.
