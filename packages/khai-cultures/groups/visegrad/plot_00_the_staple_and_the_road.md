# Plot: The Staple and the Road, 1335

## Taxonomy

[play_visegrad](play_visegrad.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-cultures

## Cue

A carter hauling copper north out of Hungary reaches Vienna and is stopped. Not robbed, not taxed exactly - stopped. The city holds a staple right, which means his load must be unpacked and offered for sale there before it may go on. He will get a price. He will not get _his_ price, and the delay is the point.

## Action

Every merchant on this corridor has the same problem, and the corridor is the whole economy of the region: Baltic amber and Polish salt going south, Hungarian copper and cattle going north, cloth going east. There is no natural frontier anywhere along it - no sea, no mountain wall, no river anybody could hold - so the road is the country, and whoever sits astride the road takes the profit.

In November 1335 three kings meet at Visegrád, on the Danube bend, and among other business they settle this: a new route north through Brno and Kraków that goes round the staple entirely. **The point is not friendship. Three courts that will spend the next centuries variously at odds with each other agree on one thing, because on that one thing they were all standing outside the same door.**

That is the shape, and it is set here. There is no natural border between the German lands and Russia, and so this ground has always been something other people cross, garrison and settle between themselves. What the four learn is not how to hold a line - no line exists - but how to notice which room is sitting, and how to get a seat in it. [The room next door](position_the_room_next_door.md) is the office, and 1335 is the first time they are on the right side of the door.

[The Haulier](persona_the_haulier.md) has been on this road ever since, and still gives distances in hours.

## Tension

Six hundred and fifty-six years later the same four will meet in the same town and say so out loud, which tells you the problem was never solved and was never going to be: a corridor cannot be defended, only crossed. Everything this group is comes from that. It is an alliance of attendance rather than of agreement - and the question the whole play carries is what such an alliance is _for_ on the day it finally gets inside the room.
