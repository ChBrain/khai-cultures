# Pitch: the Nordic key

## Taxonomy

Rummel about: [the Nordics](play_nordics.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-cultures

## Tenor

Understated to the point of nearly not being said. The play holds a church
inspecting families in their kitchens, a war a family did not turn up to, and the
quietest free-movement arrangement in Europe, and it reports all three in the same
level voice, because that is the voice these countries use for everything
including their own achievements.

## Undertow

That the most admired arrangement in the world was installed by a church testing
whether children could read the catechism, and that the north tells the story
without the church in it. What is now credited to social democracy was built on
two centuries of compulsory reading, ordered from a pulpit, enforced by the threat
of not being allowed to marry.

## Nerve

Whether a family that owes nothing to each other holds. Everything here works
because nobody is bound: the councils cannot compel, the language right is
exercised at will, the borders are open by convenience. It has lasted seventy
years, and it has never once been tested by one of them needing the others, except
in 1864, when it failed.

## Echo

A meeting of five that begins in three languages and finishes in a fourth that
belongs to none of them, and nobody remarks on the change.
