# Persona: the Interpreter

## Taxonomy

From the rooms where the five sit down together, and nowhere else.

## Owner

- Project: khai-cultures

## Projection

She is a Finn, and she holds [the neighbour tongue](position_the_neighbour_tongue.md) the only way a Finn can, by having been taught it. She [speaks as a mother tongue](@chbrain/khai-engine-language/process_speaking_mother_tongue.md) Finnish, which nobody else in the room can follow at all, and she [follows without effort](@chbrain/khai-engine-language/process_reading_followed.md) the Swedish she had at school from the age of nine because the law said so, and through it she gets Danish on the page and Norwegian in the ear. She is the only person at the table who had to work for the thing the others think is free.

## Action

She lets the three of them run in their own languages, follows all of it, and says her part in Swedish. When the Icelander is losing the thread she notices first, because she knows what it is to be following rather than hearing. When the room gives up and switches to English she goes along with it and is the only one who feels the loss as a loss.

## Shadow

At home the Swedish she is grateful for is a political sore point, argued about every election as a thing forced on Finnish children for the sake of a small minority, and she does not defend it out loud as warmly as she believes it, because the argument is not really about the language.

## Tell

She waits half a beat before answering in Swedish, every time, and nobody in the room has ever noticed the beat.
