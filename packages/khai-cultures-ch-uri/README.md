# Uri: a khai culture

Uri staged as a full khai play, ISO CH-UR (a Swiss canton; the website derives its parent, `CH`, from the subdivision code), language de. A valley that became a road: its history as the plot line, its people as the cast, the office of being the way through, its places, its practices, and its defining objects, all tuned by a single pitch.

## Estate

[khai-cultures](https://github.com/ChBrain/khai-cultures): the Cultures house that holds this culture and answers for it. This culture logs the house as its Estate; a culture with no Estate is not yet a production.

## The production

- **Anchor:** [play_uri.md](play_uri.md), the culture itself.
- **Pitch:** [pitch_uri.md](pitch_uri.md), written from Switzerland's profile inflected by an alpine transit canton, and naming no dimension.
- **Plots (history):** the bridging of the Schöllenen around 1200 that created the country, the imperial immediacy of 1231 that was bought with the pass, the oath of the three Waldstätte in 1291, Suvorov's crossing in 1799, the Gotthard railway of 1882, the Tell monument and play of 1895 and 1899, and the base tunnel of 2016 that passes underneath.
- **Personas:** Ruedi (the mountain farmer who stays because staying is the work) and Nadia (the young Urnerin who commutes out through the mountain she grew up under).
- **Positions:** die Urner Kultur (a way of living Swiss culture, nested on the position and not on every persona) and der Durchgang (the office of being the way through).
- **Places:** Altdorf (the capital), die Schöllenen (the gorge), der Urnersee (the lake).
- **Processes:** das Tellspiel (the town staging its own legend).
- **Pieces:** die Teufelsbrücke (the bridge that made the country), das Telldenkmal (the assertion in bronze).
