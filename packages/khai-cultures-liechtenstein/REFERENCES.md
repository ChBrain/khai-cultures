## The defining question

Per the house order on the defining question
(`management/orders/order_the_defining_question.md`), this play had three plots
and all three were acts of a state: a **land purchase** in 1699 and 1712, an
**imperial elevation** in 1719, and a **tax and company-law regime** after 1945.
It is the clearest case the house has found of what that order exists to catch. A
Viennese family bought two lordships in order to qualify for a seat in the
Imperial Diet, and did not live in them for two hundred and thirty-nine years;
the people in the valley had been there for four hundred and the people on the
terrace above for a thousand, and the play knew about none of them. Run the tell
on its own Arc and it failed on the alpine dialect, on the Alpabtrieb and on the
loyalty to the princely house: each named as what defines this culture, and none
of them in a plot.

**`plot_00`** answers the origin with the two settlements rather than with the
purchase: the Alemanni take the valley floor after Rome, the Walser come over
the passes around 1300 and take the terrace, and seven hundred years later the
two are still audible in one sentence at five kilometres' distance. That is what
the country is made of, and it is older than the state by four centuries and
independent of it.

**`plot_03`** answers the loyalty the Arc asserts. It could not begin with a
purchase, because nobody is loyal to an absentee landlord: it begins in 1938,
when Franz Josef II moved in and the principality acquired an address inside
itself, in the same span of years in which the country cancelled its customs
union with Vienna and chose Bern instead. The sovereignty of 1719 was a title;
here it is a decision, taken in the moment the older neighbour became dangerous.

**`plot_99`** records the decade still resolving: a financial centre that gave up
the secrecy that made it and survived, a tercentenary celebrating an elevation
that followed a purchase, and a working population that crosses a border twice a
day. Where that leads belongs in the Stakes.

The numbering is closed rather than gapped, as in the United Kingdom: inserting
1919-1938 renumbered the economic miracle from 03 to 04. `plot_00` and `plot_99`
sit outside the sequence because they answer different questions; the sequence
between them is a reading order.
