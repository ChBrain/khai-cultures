# Arizona: a khai culture

Arizona staged as a full khai play, ISO US-AZ, language en. The Grand Canyon State: its history as the plot line, its people as the cast, the roles its park protections, water allocations, and native military code contributions encodes, its places, its practices, and its defining objects, all tuned by a single pitch.

## Estate

[khai-cultures](https://github.com/ChBrain/khai-cultures): the Cultures house that holds this culture and answers for it.

## The production

- **Anchor:** [Arizona](play_arizona.md), the culture itself.
- **Pitch:** [the Arizona key](pitch_arizona.md), the tone this culture is played in.
- **Plots (history):** the Grand Canyon National Park Act of 1919 and the Central Arizona Project authorisation in 1968.
- **Personas:** Ramon (Bisbee mine geologist) and Yazhi (a Diné weaver and guide who lives here), written in English.
- **Positions:** desert resilience (conservation/native wisdom), southwest frontier (mining/self-reliance).
- **Places:** Grand Canyon, Bisbee Mine.
- **Processes:** copper smelting (mineral extraction).
- **Pieces:** copper ore.
- **Plan:** the groundwater management plan.

## What this package deliberately does not hold

Navajo weaving, the rug and the code talkers were staged here until
`us_navajo` existed. They are Diné, not Arizonan, and they now live in
`@chbrain/khai-cultures-us-navajo`, which this package depends on and links.
`management/orders/order_a_culture_without_a_map.md` names the older shape by
filename: a culture must not be staged as a chapter of its host. Yazhi stays,
because a third of this state is the Navajo Nation and an Arizona with no Diné
person in it would be false the other way, but she belongs to the Diné way and
lives here, rather than belonging here.
