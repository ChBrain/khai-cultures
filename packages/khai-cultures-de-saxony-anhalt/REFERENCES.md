## The defining question

Asked of this restaging, per the house's `order_the_defining_question.md`: what defines Saxony-Anhalt, and does the play stage it? The play already carried a claim, in its Name and Arc, that this restaging did not rewrite: a land of the Reformation and the Bauhaus, once neu gefügt out of Halle and Magdeburg. The gap the previous staging left was that "Reformation" was staged as theology Germany's own play already carries (Luther's conscience, his translation), never as what it specifically meant to this place: Wittenberg became, in Cranach's workshop, the first place where a religious conviction was mass-produced, branded and sold. `plot_02` now stages that. The Arc's claim to statehood ("1990 als Land neu gefügt aus Halle und Magdeburg") was staged only as an event, never as what it actually is: the third and unrelated pairing of a name that has never once described a single continuous political community, which `plot_08` now stages in full, back to 1945. And the play carried no answer at all to where this Land comes from before any of that, which `plot_00` now supplies: not a founding, but a thousand years of imperial and ecclesiastical weight this territory carried without ever again being a country's centre, the exact tension the 1990s state (and the 1999 disc) inherit unresolved.

Two self-descriptions in the existing Arc are, on the evidence, not accurate, and are recorded here rather than silently edited, per this house's practice of leaving a claim in place and writing the disagreement down:

- The Arc calls Bauhaus modernism "klar wie die karge Erde" (clear like the barren earth). The Magdeburger Börde is not barren; it is among the most fertile black-earth (chernozem) farmland in Germany, which is precisely why the Bodenreform of 1945/46 and the siting of the chemical industry both mattered here. The image the Arc reaches for is exactly backwards.
- The Arc says the Land was "1990 als Land neu gefügt aus Halle und Magdeburg" (refashioned from Halle and Magdeburg), naming only the two Prussian provinces and passing over Anhalt, even though the Land carries Anhalt in its own name and the 1990 reconstitution is legally the same Halle-and-Magdeburg-plus-Anhalt-derived territory first assembled in 1945, not a new pairing invented in 1990.

Neither claim was edited for this restaging; both are left as the Playwright wrote them, with the disagreement on record here instead.

### What this pass changed, and why

`npm run next` had this culture at the head of the queue on the "wrong" rung, for two
reasons: the line ran backwards, and it carried 473 years of span with a 408-year hole in
it between 1517 and 1925.

The backwards edge was the chemical triangle. It opens in 1916 and was numbered after the
1945 Land, because its Cue was written from the Land's point of view - the Land inherits a
landscape. It now opens on its own date, with the works going up on the Börde, and sits
where 1916 belongs.

The hole was not the record being silent. The house was already pointing into it: the
Magdeburg Rider's own yearbook names "der fast vollständigen Zerstörung Magdeburgs im
Dreißigjährigen Krieg 1631", and no plot staged it. Four plots now fill the gap - the
Halle salters in 1491, Magdeburg in 1631, Guericke in 1654, the Garden Realm from 1765 -
and the largest remaining gap is 146 years against a house median of 145.

Halle had no place file at all, in a Land whose Arc names it and whose `plot_99` turns on
a door in that city. It has one now, as does Wörlitz.
