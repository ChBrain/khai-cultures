# Ceuta: a khai culture

Ceuta staged as a full khai play, ISO ES-CE, language es. Ceuta: its history as the plot line, its people as the cast, the roles its Portuguese conquest, thirty-year siege, and statute of autonomy encode, its places, its practices, and its defining objects, all tuned by a single pitch.

## Estate

[khai-cultures](https://github.com/ChBrain/khai-cultures): the Cultures house that holds this culture and answers for it.

## The production

- **Anchor:** [play_ceuta.md](play_ceuta.md), the culture itself.
- **Pitch:** [pitch_ceuta.md](pitch_ceuta.md), the Ceutan key.
- **Plots (history):** the Portuguese conquest of 1415, the thirty-year siege of 1694 to 1727, and the statute of autonomy of 1995.
- **Personas:** Antonio (port stevedore) and Nadia (heritage officer), written in Spanish.
- **Positions:** the Strait (the crossing, the port, the wind), the four cultures (Christian, Muslim, Jewish, Hindu), Ceutan culture, and Ceutan Spanish.
- **Places:** Monte Hacho, the Royal Walls.
- **Processes:** the atypical border trade (the porteo through the Tarajal), the round of the four festive calendars.
- **Pieces:** the Caliphal Gate, the border fence.
