# Plot: The Border That Is Not There

## Taxonomy

[play_benelux](play_benelux.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-cultures

## Cue

A woman drives north out of Antwerp and notices, a few minutes later, that the road markings have changed colour. That is the only thing that happened. She has crossed a frontier her grandparents were shot at, and the car did not slow down.

## Action

The customs union worked so completely that it disappeared into its own result: the thing these three prototyped in London became the single market, and the three of them now host Brussels, Luxembourg and The Hague between them. The Benelux Union was quietly renewed in 2008, indefinitely, and almost nobody in the three countries could say what it currently does. Meanwhile, in the same decade, each of the three has found the same question at home. In [Belgium](@chbrain/khai-cultures-belgium/play_belgium.md) the two communities live complete separate lives and greet each other in English. In [the Netherlands](@chbrain/khai-cultures-netherlands/play_netherlands.md) the country that was proud of speaking everyone's language finds its own has become optional. In [Luxembourg](@chbrain/khai-cultures-luxembourg/play_luxembourg.md) nearly one resident in two has no vote and nearly one worker in two sleeps abroad. Three of the most integrated countries in Europe, each of them unsure at the same moment who "we" is.

## Stage

- [Belgium](@chbrain/khai-cultures-belgium/play_belgium.md): the state that holds without a government, and cannot say whether that is compromise or indifference.
- [the Netherlands](@chbrain/khai-cultures-netherlands/play_netherlands.md): the flat country whose equality is now conducted in a language nobody at the table learned first.
- [Luxembourg](@chbrain/khai-cultures-luxembourg/play_luxembourg.md): the small state whose motto was written against occupiers and now asks who counts as being from here.

## Tension

This group solved the problem between its members and has no vocabulary for the problem inside them. Benelux was built so that the delta could never be played off, one against another, and on that it succeeded past the point of being noticed. It has nothing to say about a country whose halves have stopped needing each other, or one whose workforce goes home over a border every night. That is not a failure of the union; it is the shape of what a customs union is for, and it is worth naming rather than papering over. The decade does not ask whether the arrangement holds - it holds so well it is invisible. It asks whether "small states that negotiate" is still an identity once the negotiating is finished, and what is left when the answer to _who belongs_ has quietly become three different answers.
