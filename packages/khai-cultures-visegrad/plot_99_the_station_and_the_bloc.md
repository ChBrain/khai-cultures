# Plot: The Station and the Bloc, since 2022

## Taxonomy

[play_visegrad](play_visegrad.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-cultures

## Cue

A woman stands on a platform in a border town holding a piece of cardboard with a number written on it: how many people she can put up, and for how long. Nobody organised her. There are hundreds like her along that frontier within about forty-eight hours.

## Action

What happens next is the largest movement of people in Europe since the war, and the four countries on this corridor take a share of it that no one had planned for. [Poland](@chbrain/khai-cultures-poland/play_poland.md) receives the most by a long way; [Czechia](@chbrain/khai-cultures-czechia/play_czechia.md) takes among the most per head of anywhere. It is done at first almost entirely by people with cars and spare rooms, which is to say by the same means as everything else that has ever worked on this ground.

And in the same months, the bloc stops working. The four had spent thirty years acting as one body - into NATO, into the Union, at every negotiation where four votes were worth more than one. On the question of the war they split, and they split hard: two of them among the most forward-leaning states in Europe, and the others taking a position their partners regard as the opposite. Summits are cancelled. The format goes quiet.

**Both halves of that are the same country, and that is the finding.** The people on the platforms and the governments in the capitals are not two populations; they are the same one, and the split runs through it rather than between the four.

It also completes [the room next door](position_the_room_next_door.md). The office was always attendance - get through the door, be present when it is decided. The four got through the door: they are in both institutions, they have been for twenty years, they are inside. **And an office defined by being outside the room has nothing to say about what to do once you are in it.** They agreed all the way to the threshold and not one step past it.

[The Haulier](persona_the_haulier.md) still drives the corridor, still finds out at the barrier, and still cannot talk to one of his four neighbours in anything but freight English.

## Stage

- [the room next door](position_the_room_next_door.md): an office that ends at the door it was built to get through.
- [the Haulier](persona_the_haulier.md): the corridor's constant, unchanged by any of it.
- [Poland](@chbrain/khai-cultures-poland/play_poland.md) and [Czechia](@chbrain/khai-cultures-czechia/play_czechia.md): the two that took the most and lean the furthest forward.

## Tension

A grouping made of a shared road out of empire has arrived, and arrival is what it has no answer for. The bond was never a common mind - the play said so from the start - but it was a common destination, and the destination has been reached. What is genuinely open is whether four countries who still share a corridor, a century of being decided about, and an instinct to meet the train, have anything left to be together _for_ - or whether the honest reading is that the alliance did its work, worked completely, and is now a habit that outlived its errand.
