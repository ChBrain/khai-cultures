# Position: the room next door

## Taxonomy

Parent group: positions

## Owner

- Project: khai-cultures

## Has

The experience of being decided about. Not conquered, not consulted - decided
about, by people meeting somewhere else, whose meeting you learn the outcome of
afterwards. It is the one thing all four of these countries have that their
western neighbours do not, and they have it in quantities: a partition agreed
between three courts, a conference that settled a border without inviting the
country whose border it was, a demarcation drawn at a seaside resort, a bloc
membership nobody was asked about.

It is not the same as weakness. A weak country loses a war it fought. **This is
the condition of being an item on someone else's agenda**, and its distinctive
mark is that there is no moment at which you could have done anything differently,
because you were not in the room and did not know the room was sitting.

What it produces is not resentment exactly. It is a permanent, practised
scepticism about arrangements arrived at elsewhere and announced as settled - and
a corresponding attention to whichever room is currently sitting.

## Orders

That you find out who is meeting, and that you get into the room if there is any
way to get into it. Everything the four have done together follows from this and
is best read as one long attempt at it: the 1335 congress, the 1991 declaration,
the coordinated push into two western institutions. **The point of a bloc, for
these four, was never unity of opinion. It was attendance.**

## Loses

Ground the moment attendance is achieved. An office defined by being outside the
room has nothing to say about what to do once you are inside it, which is why the
four could act as one body all the way to the door and not agree about anything
afterwards.

## Drives

It drives everyone here who has ever been told what was decided. It is why a
Central European reads an announcement of a settlement the way other people read
a weather forecast: as a thing that has happened to them, from a direction they
were watching.
