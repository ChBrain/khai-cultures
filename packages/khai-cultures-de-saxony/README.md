# Saxony (Sachsen): a khai culture

Saxony staged as a full khai play, ISO DE-SN, language de. The eastern state of Germany: its history as the plot line, its people as the cast, the roles its artistic, industrial, and democratic life encodes, its places, its practices, and its defining objects, all tuned by a single pitch.

## Estate

[khai-cultures](https://github.com/ChBrain/khai-cultures): the Cultures house that holds this culture and answers for it.

## The production

- **Anchor:** [play_saxony.md](play_saxony.md), the culture itself.
- **Pitch:** [pitch_saxony.md](pitch_saxony.md), the Saxony key.
- **Plots (history):** the Margravate of Meissen's borrowed name and Ore Mountains silver (origin), the reign of Augustus the Strong, Saxony's rise as an industrial pioneer, the Peaceful Revolution of 1989, and the decade from Pegida's Monday walks to the ESMC chip fab groundbreaking (present).
- **Personas:** Günter (traditional porcelain craftsman) and Peggy (modern microelectronics engineer), written in German.
- **Positions:** der sächsische Erfindergeist (ingenuity), der Barockstolz (baroque cultural pride), der Bürgerprotest (protest legacy), das sorbische Erbe (the Slavic Sorbian minority the land was settled from).
- **Places:** die Frauenkirche in Dresden, das Silicon Saxony (semiconductor hub), das Erzgebirge (the Ore Mountains, source of the silver that funded the Wettins).
- **Processes:** die Porzellanherstellung (making Meissen porcelain), der Bergbau (the silver mining craft that becomes the Bergakademie Freiberg and, centuries later, the region's engineering identity).
- **Pieces:** das Meissener Porzellan (porcelain), der Leipziger Aufruf (non-violence declaration of Oct 9, 1989).
- **Plan:** der Ausbau von Silicon Saxony (semiconductor expansion plan).
