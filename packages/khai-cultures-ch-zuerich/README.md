# Zürich: a khai culture

Zürich staged as a full khai play, ISO CH-ZH (a Swiss canton; the website derives its parent, `CH`, from the subdivision code), language de. A Roman toll post that never stopped earning on other people's goods: its history as the plot line, its people as the cast, the office of counting, its places, its practices, and its defining objects, all tuned by a single pitch.

## Estate

[khai-cultures](https://github.com/ChBrain/khai-cultures): the Cultures house that holds this culture and answers for it. This culture logs the house as its Estate; a culture with no Estate is not yet a production.

## The production

- **Anchor:** [play_zuerich.md](play_zuerich.md), the culture itself.
- **Pitch:** [pitch_zuerich.md](pitch_zuerich.md), written from Switzerland's profile inflected by a place whose business has always been a percentage of what passes through, and naming no dimension.
- **Plots (history):** the Roman toll station at the outflow of the lake, the guild revolution of 1336, the sausages of 1522 and the Reformation that followed, Kappel in 1531, the Stäfa affair of 1795, Escher's railway, bank and polytechnic from 1853, Züri brännt in 1980, and the weekend in March 2023 when Escher's bank was handed to its neighbour.
- **Personas:** Felix (who belongs to a list he did not make) and Sara (who has to do the arithmetic on her own city).
- **Positions:** die Zürcher Kultur (a way of living Swiss culture, nested on the position and not on every persona) and das Zählen (the office of earning on goods that are not yours).
- **Places:** die Limmat (the narrows the city is built on), die Langstrasse (where what does not pay went), der Paradeplatz (fifty metres of Swiss economic history).
- **Processes:** das Sechseläuten (the guilds' spring parade, which everyone watches and few join).
- **Pieces:** das Grossmünster (an argument in stone, emptied in the 1520s), der Böögg (a custom run with a stopwatch).
