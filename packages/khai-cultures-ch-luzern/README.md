# Luzern: a khai culture

Lucerne staged as a full khai play, ISO CH-LU (a Swiss canton; the website derives its parent, `CH`, from the subdivision code), language de. A place that began as a crossing and has been looked at ever since: its history as the plot line, its people as the cast, the office of being looked at, its places, its practices, and its defining objects, all tuned by a single pitch.

## Estate

[khai-cultures](https://github.com/ChBrain/khai-cultures): the Cultures house that holds this culture and answers for it. This culture logs the house as its Estate; a culture with no Estate is not yet a production.

## The production

- **Anchor:** [play_luzern.md](play_luzern.md), the culture itself.
- **Pitch:** [pitch_luzern.md](pitch_luzern.md), written from Switzerland's profile inflected by a place whose income depends on being looked at, and naming no dimension.
- **Plots (history):** the bridge at the point where boat becomes road, Sempach in 1386, the Jesuit college of 1574 and two centuries as the Catholic capital, the Sonderbund defeat of 1847, the first mountain railway in Europe in 1871, and the ninety-minute visit at the Schwanenplatz today.
- **Personas:** Vreni (who lives in a city centre not arranged for her) and Seppi (for whom the guests are the work and not the problem).
- **Positions:** die Luzerner Kultur (a way of living Swiss culture, nested on the position and not on every persona) and der Blick (the office of earning by being looked at).
- **Places:** der Vierwaldstättersee (a lake whose founding story belongs to the neighbours), der Pilatus (a mountain with a timetable), der Schwanenplatz (ninety minutes of what this city lives on).
- **Processes:** die Fasnacht (three days a year when no allowance is made for anyone).
- **Pieces:** die Kapellbrücke (a crossing and a narrative at once, three centuries before the first coach).
