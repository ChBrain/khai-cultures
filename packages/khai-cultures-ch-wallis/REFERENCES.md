# References: Wallis

## The pitch layer

The pitch is written from Switzerland's national Hofstede profile, inflected by what six hundred years of irrigation cooperatives do to it: the same low power distance and high individualism, read through a community whose reliability rests on a written order of turns rather than on trust. No dimension is named in the prose.

Source: Geert Hofstede, Hofstede Insights (the Country Comparison tool and the six-dimensions model of national culture), the national profile for Switzerland. No separate sub-scores for the cantons are claimed.

## Three decisions, recorded

**Walliserdeutsch gets no tongue file, yet.** It is the strongest candidate in this house for a `gsw` variety of its own: it is generally described as among the most archaic forms of Alemannic, and its features are nameable. This house does not ship a variety until someone can give the rule, the minimal pair on both sides, and say whether it survives in writing, and that is a competence question. Gaby holds `gsw`, and the gap is recorded here rather than filled badly.

**Céline is cast by consequence.** The Rhône correction is a nineteenth-century plot and she is alive now, so she appears in its Tension as what it produced: terraces on ground that would not exist without the dam in the valley and the channel on the slope. That is the pattern the house order on casting by consequence describes, and this culture appears in the `--consequences` queue accordingly.

**Blatten is a record and not a lesson.** `plot_99` says what happened and says that the evacuation worked. What a warming mountain means for a canton that lives under it is a judgement and by design likely wrong, so it stays in that plot's Tension and in the play's Stakes, which are a question and not a claim.
