# Catalonia: References

The provenance behind this culture: the Hofstede source data that tunes the pitch (kept here, never in the prose) and the public-domain historical sources behind the plots, personas, places, and pieces.

## Hofstede source data

The pitch [pitch_catalonia.md](pitch_catalonia.md) is written from Catalonia's profile inflected by its Mediterranean trade history, modernist art, and textile industries, naming no dimension and carrying no number.

| Dimension                     | Catalonia base score (estimated / Spain comparison) |

Source: Geert Hofstede, Hofstede Insights (Spain national base adjusted for Catalonia's flatter industrial organization, mercantile individualism, and long-term business orientation).
