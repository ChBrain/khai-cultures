# Bern: a khai culture

Bern staged as a full khai play, ISO CH-BE (a Swiss canton; the website derives its parent, `CH`, from the subdivision code), languages de and fr. A city built to a plan, which lost an empire and was handed a country's files: its history as the plot line, its people as the cast, the office of slowness, its places, its practices, and its defining objects, all tuned by a single pitch.

## Estate

[khai-cultures](https://github.com/ChBrain/khai-cultures): the Cultures house that holds this culture and answers for it. This culture logs the house as its Estate; a culture with no Estate is not yet a production.

## The production

- **Anchor:** [play_bern.md](play_bern.md), the culture itself.
- **Pitch:** [pitch_bern.md](pitch_bern.md), written from Switzerland's profile inflected by a place that decides slowly and then completely, and naming no dimension.
- **Plots (history):** the founding on the Aare peninsula in 1191, the Burgundian spoils of Murten in 1476, the taking of the Vaud in 1536, the peasants' war of 1653 and the broken treaty, the Grauholz in 1798, the choice as federal city in 1848, and the thirty-one-year settlement of the Jura question to 1979.
- **Personas:** Ueli (whose income depends on forms) and Chantal (for whom two languages are a working tool).
- **Positions:** die Berner Kultur (a way of living Swiss culture, nested on the position and not on every persona) and die Behäbigkeit (the office of deciding slowly and completely).
- **Places:** die Aareschlaufe (the peninsula that dictated the plan), das Bundeshaus (where a city stopped deciding for itself), das Emmental (the picture the world holds of Switzerland, and where its largest revolt came from).
- **Processes:** der Zibelemärit (a market that starts at four in the morning and casts nobody).
- **Pieces:** die Zytglogge (a city gate that stayed put while the plan moved west).
