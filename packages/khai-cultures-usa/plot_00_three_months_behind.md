# Plot: three months behind

## Taxonomy

[play_usa](play_usa.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-cultures

## Cue

A farmer stands in a doorway with a letter in his hand. It carries an instruction from three thousand miles away about a matter that has to be settled this week. The letter left in the spring. Whoever wrote it will not learn what was decided until the autumn, and by then it will not matter. He folds it, puts it in his coat, and decides.

## Action

That is the whole of it, for a hundred and fifty years. Under sail the crossing runs six to ten weeks each way, so an order is a season old on arrival and a question takes half a year to come back answered. A settlement that has to decide today cannot wait, so it decides: the town meeting that sets the rates, the congregation that picks its own minister, the assembly that votes the governor's money and thereby owns him. None of that begins as a philosophy. It is what distance does to administration, practised until it is ordinary. The theory arrives later to explain a habit that is already older than anyone alive - which is why, when an empire finally tries to govern closely, it is not introducing a rule but breaking one.

And behind the coast lies the reason anyone stays. Beyond the first ridges is the largest body of first-rate farmland on earth, and it is drained by the largest system of navigable inland water in the world, so grain grown a thousand miles inland can float to the sea for almost nothing. That is the hard fact under every promise of a fresh start: [the frontier](position_the_frontier.md) is not an idea about character, it is a river system with soil on top of it. And where the water reaches the ocean, a harbour like [New York](place_new_york.md) becomes the hinge of a continent.

But the land was not empty, and the reason it looked empty is the darkest thing in this origin. Hundreds of nations had lived here for thousands of years, and Old World disease travelled inland faster than any European did - carried by trade, ahead of settlement, catastrophic and uncounted. Much of what newcomers walked into had been emptied within the living memory of the people who survived it: fields already cleared, orchards still bearing, villages gone. What got written down as wilderness was in many places a graveyard, and reading it as unclaimed was the founding error that the rest of the story keeps paying for.

The first thing this culture made of itself was a story about that meeting. [A harvest meal](process_thanksgiving.md) between a colony that would not have survived its first winter and the people who fed it was remembered afterwards as a beginning of friendship - a true event, kept and retold with almost everything difficult taken out. Before there is a country there is already an American habit of telling itself a simpler version and believing it.

## Stage

- [New York](place_new_york.md): the harbour where that continent meets the ocean, and the hinge everything arrives and leaves through.
- [Thanksgiving](process_thanksgiving.md): the first story this culture told about itself, true in its event and simplified in its keeping.
- [the American way](position_culture_american_way.md): the belonging of a people who governed themselves because nobody could reach them in time.

## Tension

Two habits came out of the same distance and went into the same founding. A people left to decide for themselves learned self-government by practising it, which is the thing this culture is proudest of and has the best claim to. The same people, arriving in a country emptied by something they did not see and did not understand, learned to treat what they found as unclaimed. Neither habit was chosen and both were inherited, and every later argument about who the creed covers is an argument between them.
