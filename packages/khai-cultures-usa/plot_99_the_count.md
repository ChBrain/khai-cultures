# Plot: the count

## Taxonomy

[play_usa](play_usa.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-cultures

## Cue

[Maya](persona_maya.md) is twenty-six, born here to parents who were not, and she is looking at two numbers on a screen that do not agree. One is the official count. The other is what a great many people she knows believe instead. Nobody in the argument is lying, exactly. They are working from different counts, and neither side can any longer say what would settle it.

## Action

This decade the country lost its agreement about arithmetic, and the arithmetic it lost was about the plainest things a nation keeps.

It counted more than a million of its own dead from one disease, the highest recorded national toll anywhere. Life expectancy fell two years running - the sharpest fall in a century, and steepest among the people who could least afford it - and has since come partly back. Overdose deaths passed a hundred thousand in a single year, most of them from a drug measured in micrograms, and then in 2024 fell sharply for the first time. Each of those numbers was disputed while it was being taken.

In 2020, after a man was killed under a knee in Minneapolis and the whole thing was filmed, the largest wave of protest in the country's history moved through several thousand towns and cities at once, most of it peaceful and some of it not. In January 2021 the counting of an election was interrupted by a crowd that came into the building where the count was being certified. In June 2022 the court that had recognised a constitutional right for half a century removed it, so that something lawful in one state became a felony across a line on a map.

That last one is the oldest American mechanism doing exactly what it was built to do. A country that learned to decide locally because no instruction could reach it in time is designed to give different answers in different places. It has always done that about policy and mostly held. What is new is the number of questions now answered differently that are not policy at all: what happened, how many, who won.

And the ordinary things carry on, which is the part a bare chronicle misses. [The flag](piece_the_flag.md) flies from both sides of every argument, claimed with equal sincerity by people who agree on nothing else - a symbol so widely held it can no longer tell you anything about the person holding it. [The jeans](piece_blue_jeans.md) invented for miners are worn by more of the world than any other garment ever made. [The road](process_the_road_trip.md) is still there and people still take it, four dollars a gallon and eight hundred miles of it, because the country is laid out so that leaving is always available. And [New York](place_new_york.md) emptied out in 2020, was written off, and filled up again.

Maya's own position is the one the play keeps returning to. She was raised on [the creed](position_the_creed.md), holds it more literally than most people born to it, and is old enough now to have watched the country argue about whether the words include her. She has not concluded that they do not.

## Tension

This country was built to give different answers in different places, and that was never the flaw - it is the mechanism that let it hold a continent without one capital deciding everything. What has changed is what is being answered differently. A federation can carry fifty policies. Whether it can carry fifty accounts of the same week is the open question, and nothing in the design was written with that in mind.
