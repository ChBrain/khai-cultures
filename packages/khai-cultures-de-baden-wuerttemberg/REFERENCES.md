The defining personas [Karl](persona_karl.md) and [Frieda](persona_frieda.md) are archetypes representing the traditional medium-sized company (Mittelstand) engineer and the modern tech innovator/start-up founder. No new persona files were added in the 2026-08 staging pass: the Alamanni, the Staufer, the Tübinger Stiftler, Carl and Bertha Benz, and Winfried Kretschmann are all carried in prose only, matching the convention this culture already used for Napoleon, Carl Benz and Gottlieb Daimler.

## The defining question (2026-08 staging pass)

Read against `management/orders/order_the_defining_question.md`: the play's Arc named `Dialekt`, `Kehrwoche` and `Häusle`, and its Stakes named `Tüftlergeist`, against a plot line that opened at the 1806 elevation to kingdom/duchy and the 1951 referendum, both constitutional facts about the state rather than the culture. `Kehrwoche` was already staged (in the 1806 plot's Tension); `Dialekt` and `Häusle` were not staged anywhere outside the Arc and the personas' own speech.

This pass added a `plot_00` (the Alamanni settlement and the Alemannic/Swabian dialect boundary, staging `Dialekt` as an inherited fact older than either 1806 or 1951, not a constitutional one), a Staufer plot (1079-1268, the region's one experience of ruling rather than being ruled, and the source of the state's own coat of arms), a Tübinger Stift plot (naming the Pietist root of the quiet, unshowy intelligence the pitch already dramatizes as `stille Klugheit`, and staging `Tüftlergeist`'s intellectual ancestor rather than only its engineering one), a Wüstenrot plot (staging `Häusle` as an institution, the Bausparkasse, rather than only a word), and a `plot_99` recording the decade in which the state's automotive base, Green-led government and Stuttgart 21 tunnel are all still unresolved. The 1806 and 1951 plots stand as they were: they remain real events worth staging, they are simply no longer asked to carry the culture's origin and present on their own.
