# Graubünden: a khai culture

Graubünden staged as a full khai play, ISO CH-GR (a Swiss canton; the website derives its parent, `CH`, from the subdivision code), languages de, rm and it. A hundred and fifty valleys that were never one country: its history as the plot line, its people as the cast, the office of living off guests, its places, its practices, and its defining objects, all tuned by a single pitch.

## Estate

[khai-cultures](https://github.com/ChBrain/khai-cultures): the Cultures house that holds this culture and answers for it. This culture logs the house as its Estate; a culture with no Estate is not yet a production.

## The production

- **Anchor:** [play_graubuenden.md](play_graubuenden.md), the culture itself.
- **Pitch:** [pitch_graubuenden.md](pitch_graubuenden.md), written from Switzerland's profile inflected by a canton whose form is plurality and whose cost is plurality, and naming no dimension.
- **Plots (history):** the valleys that could not reach each other, the three leagues of 1367 to 1524, the Valtellina murders of 1620, the loss of the subject lands in 1797, the invention of winter tourism at St. Moritz in 1864, the national park of 1914, Rumantsch Grischun in 1982 and the return to the idioms from 2011, and the second-home vote of 2012.
- **Personas:** Gian (who passes on a language with no economic print run) and Luisa (whose canton begins on the other side of a pass).
- **Positions:** die Bündner Kultur (a way of living Swiss culture, nested on the position and not on every persona) and der Kurgast (the office of a country whose business is other people's leisure).
- **Places:** Chur (the oldest town in the country), das Oberengadin (where the winter season was invented), der Nationalpark (the land taken out of use).
- **Processes:** der Chalandamarz (the first of March, which lasts as long as there are children).
- **Pieces:** Rumantsch Grischun (a language built because five were too expensive), die Rhätische Bahn (the line that made the season possible).
