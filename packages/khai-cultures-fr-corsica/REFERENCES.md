# Corsica: References

The provenance behind this culture: the source data that tunes the pitch (kept
here, never in the prose), the public historical record behind the plots,
personas, places, pieces and processes, and the note on the language the package
is written in.

## The language this package is written in

Every file here is written in Corsican, against
[`position_language_co.md`](@chbrain/khai-cultures-tongues/co/position_language_co.md).
That is the house rule and it is the right rule: a culture whose central open
question is the legal standing of its tongue cannot be staged in the tongue that
displaced it.

**The Corsican in this package has not been read by a speaker.** It was written
by the agent that staged the culture, from the tongue position and from written
Corsican, and it should be treated as staged prose awaiting native review, not
as attested usage. The likely faults are the ones a non-speaker makes: the
initial-consonant lenition the tongue position names as its Orders is a
pronunciation rule and does not surface in spelling, so nothing here proves it
was respected; the polynomic question — which variety a given form belongs to —
is answered here by defaulting to the northern written norm rather than by
choice; and the French administrative vocabulary the tongue position lists under
Loses is worked around in prose (`statutu particulare`, `Cullettività`) where a
speaker might simply use the French. A native reader should expect to change
words, not the content.

## The defining question, asked of Corsica

Checked against the house order on the defining question
(`management/orders/order_the_defining_question.md`): the Arc names `statu`,
`Ponte Novu`, `partenza`, `Riacquistu`, `Aleria`, `u populu corsu`, the 2003
referendum and the 2021 annulment; the Name names `ISO FR-20R`, `lingua co` and
`u modu d'appartene`; the Stakes name `una manera d'appartene chì a Republica ùn
hà ancu imparatu à scrive`; the pitch names `u lamentu`, `l'orgogliu` and
`l'appartenenza rifiutata`.

Every word of the Arc lands in a plot, which is the whole reason the plot line
runs to six rather than the house floor of three: the claim is made and struck
four separate times (1769, 1991, 2003, 2021), and collapsing any pair of them
would turn a pattern into an incident. `u modu d'appartene` from the Name is the
Stakes restated, and it is staged as the shape of the sequence rather than as
one scene, which is correct — no single plot can carry a question that is only
visible across four erasures.

Three things are deliberately not staged, and the reasoning is recorded here so
the next hand argues with a reason instead of asking again:

- **Napoleon.** He is born three months after Ponte Novu and appears in
  [plot_01](plot_01_ponte_novu_1769.md) as exactly that: the timing. He belongs
  to France's play, not to Corsica's — he is the Corsican who completed the
  absorption, and staging him here as a Corsican plot would make the culture's
  most famous son the answer to a question he spent his life on the other side
  of. The house rule that a persona belongs to one culture settles it.
- **The armed campaign as its own plot.** The FLNC's bombing years, the 1998
  assassination of prefect Claude Érignac and the long Colonna affair are the
  best-documented Corsican material there is, and they are held inside
  [plot_03](plot_03_u_riacquistu.md) and
  [plot_04](plot_04_u_populu_corsu_1991.md) rather than given a scene. The
  reason is that this culture's arc is a legal argument, not a security file:
  every erasure in the plot line is a court or a legislature, and a plot about
  explosives would answer a question about violence that the Arc, the Name and
  the Stakes never ask. The violence is present where it changed the legal
  argument — Aleria, because it is the moment cultural reclamation became a
  question of public order — and nowhere else.
- **Bonifaziu and Calvi.** Bonifacio speaks a Ligurian, Calvi a Genoese
  remnant; neither is the `co` this package is written in, and both sit inside
  the same ISO code. They are a tongues-package question (a variety needs its
  own position, written in itself) and not a culture-package one, and inventing
  a Corsican-language scene about a non-Corsican-speaking town would be the
  contrived casting the coverage rule warns against. Gallurese, a Corsican
  variety spoken in Sardinia, is the same question with a border through it and
  is parked for the same reason: culture drives this house, but the tongue is
  where a variety gets a file.
