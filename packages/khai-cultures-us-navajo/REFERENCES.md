# References

This is a creative staging; sources are generic historical encyclopedias for the
Long Walk and the treaty of 1868, the stock reduction of the nineteen-thirties,
the code talkers, the Church Rock tailings spill of 1979, and Arizona v. Navajo
Nation (2023).

## The defining question

**What defines the Diné, and does the play stage it?**

What defines them is a people who took up everything offered and stayed exactly
themselves, who were removed and came back, and who are now losing by paperwork
what cavalry could not take. The Arc says: came from the north and stopped; took
sheep, horses and silver without becoming anything else; starved out of a canyon
and walked back; then the herds by a quota, the language by a school, the water
by a ruling. Every clause is a plot. The stopping is `plot_00`, the taking up is
`plot_01`, the canyon and the return are `plot_02` and `plot_03`, and the three
paper losses are `plot_05`, `plot_04` and `plot_99`.

The Name chapter says both names are in daily use by the same people and neither
is a grievance. That is deliberate and it is why no plot is built on the name.

The Stakes ask whether what is handed down is the language or only the
membership. `plot_04`, `plot_06` and `plot_99` are that question at three dates,
and it is not answered.

| 99   | a tap that has never run                   | no                                                                           |

**Three of nine are partly the state, and each is staged from the material
thing**: an orchard, a child, a carcass. That sits exactly on the house habit of
about three in six to nine.

It is worth recording the opposite worry for this culture. For the Diné the
state is not an occasional actor, it is the medium: the treaty, the quota, the
school, the lease and the ruling are what the last hundred and sixty years were
made of. A line that staged none of it would be false. Three, each entered
through a physical thing rather than through the order that produced it, is the
judgement made here, and a later hand may reasonably argue it should be four.

## Not staged

- **Weaving, the rug, and the code talkers as this package's own files.**
  `us_arizona` already holds `process_navajo_weaving.md`, `piece_navajo_rug.md`,
  `plot_02_code_talkers.md` and `persona_yazhi.md`. This package stages the code
  talkers as `plot_06`, from the language rather than from the Corps, and leaves
  weaving alone for now. That is a deliberate hold, not an oversight: the
  duplication is real and the fix is to thin Arizona, which is its own change.
  `order_a_culture_without_a_map.md` names `persona_yazhi.md` as the fault this
  shape exists to prevent, and this package is the first half of paying it.
- **The emergence.** `plot_00` names that the Diné hold an emergence account and
  that linguists trace a migration, and stages neither as the thing that moves.
  Staging a people's sacred origin as a plot beat is not this house's to do.
- **Ceremony in detail.** `position_hozho.md` says what the premise is and what
  it costs. The content of a sing is not written here and should not be.
- **The casino, the film industry and Monument Valley.** Left out as the
  postcard: they are how this nation is looked at rather than how it lives, and
  `plot_99` stages a classroom and a barrel instead.

## What this package does not have, and what it owes

No `geo.json` and no ISO code, which is the mapless shape working as intended on
a nation that genuinely holds ground.

**The debt: this package should be written in Diné bizaad and is not.** The
house rule is that English is the last resort, taken only where nobody could
write the tongue. Something on the order of a hundred and seventy thousand
people can write this one, so English here is a fallback, and a fallback in a
culture whose central tension is its own language is the worst place in the
house to take one.

It is taken anyway, for a stated reason. The tongue file `nv` is already one of
the three in `khai-cultures-tongues` that describe rather than perform, and is
already booked as a debt; this package extends that debt rather than opening a
new kind. The alternative was to leave the Diné appearing in this house only as
`persona_yazhi.md`, a weaver who belongs to Arizona culture through the
desert-resilient half of its character, which is the fault the order indicts by
name. Shipping in English pays part of that and owes the rest.

What is owed is a rewrite in Diné bizaad by somebody who can write it, reviewed
by somebody who speaks it. That is a person, not a wall, and there is no reader
wired in this house to stand in for one.
