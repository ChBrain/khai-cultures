# Process: weaving at the upright loom

## Taxonomy

Parent group: process of [the Diné](play_navajo.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-cultures

## Initiated by

There is wool from the spring shearing in a sack in the corner, and a loom against the wall with the warp still on it from the last one.

## Direction

The fleece is washed, carded and spun by hand, dyed, and then built upward on a vertical loom that is strung between two beams and cannot be taken apart and carried. The design is not drawn first. It is held and worked out as the rows go up, which is why two weavers given the same wool produce work nobody would mistake, and why a piece cannot be finished by a second pair of hands.

## Lever

It converts a flock into money without selling the flock. That was the whole economy of a great many households for a century, and it is the reason a woman with no wage and no English could put children through school.

## Echo

The trading posts shaped it as much as the weavers did: the regional patterns buyers now treat as ancient tradition were in many cases worked out between a weaver and a trader who knew what would sell in Chicago. That is not a scandal and the weavers were not duped. It is what happens when a craft has to eat.
