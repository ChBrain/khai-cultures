# Persona: Delvina Yellowhair

## Taxonomy

The language programme at the chapter house, a classroom, a projector and a grant that runs two years at a time.

## Owner

- Project: khai-cultures

## Projection

Twenty-nine, back from Flagstaff with a degree and a car that is wrong for these roads. [American English](@chbrain/khai-cultures-tongues/en/position_language_en_us.md) is [what she was raised in](@chbrain/khai-engine-language/process_speaking_mother_tongue.md), which is the fact of her life she is least willing to say out loud. [Diné bizaad](@chbrain/khai-cultures-tongues/nv/position_language_nv.md) she [works out word by word](@chbrain/khai-engine-language/process_reading_deciphered.md) from a grammar and a recording, and speaks [as far as a room needs and not one sentence further](@chbrain/khai-engine-language/process_speaking_borrowed.md). She belongs to [the Diné way](position_culture_dine.md) by choosing to come back, which older people here count and younger people find slightly embarrassing.

## Action

She teaches the language she is learning, she [hauls the water](process_hauling_water.md) for her grandmother's house on Saturdays, and she records [Asdzáá Tsosie](persona_asdzaa_tsosie.md) on a phone whenever she can get her talking.

## Shadow

She is building a version of the language out of books and elders' recordings and is starting to hear her own reconstruction as correct, because in a classroom of eleven children she is the only authority present. Nobody in the room can tell her she is wrong.

## Tell

When an elder switches to English to help her, she keeps answering in Diné bizaad for one sentence too long.
