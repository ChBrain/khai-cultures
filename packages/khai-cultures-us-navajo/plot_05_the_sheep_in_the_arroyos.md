# Plot: The sheep in the arroyos

## Taxonomy

Parent group: plot of [the Diné](play_navajo.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-cultures

## Cue

Carcasses in the washes in 1933, thousands of them, shot where they stood and left, because moving them was not part of the programme.

## Action

The range was overgrazed and the science was not wrong about that. What followed was stock reduction: a federal quota, a percentage off every flock, applied hardest to the small holders because a rich man's ten per cent leaves him a living and a poor woman's leaves her nothing. Goats were shot in front of the families that owned them. It was administered by a commissioner who considered himself a friend of Indians and who believed he was saving the land for its owners. It broke the herding economy in a decade, put a generation into wage work, and did more to convince this nation that Washington could not be trusted than [the march](plot_02_the_orchards_at_tseyi.md) had done seventy years before.

## Stage

- `process`: [running sheep](process_running_sheep.md), cut to a fraction and never recovered
- `persona`: [Asdzáá Tsosie](persona_asdzaa_tsosie.md), whose grandmother counted what was left

## Tension

The people who did this were the reformers. They had ended the worst of the old policy, they meant to preserve the land, and the argument they made was ecologically sound. A culture can absorb being wronged by enemies; what it does not get over is being wronged by somebody who was right about the grass and wrong about everything else.
