# Persona: Asdzáá Tsosie

## Taxonomy

[k'é](position_ke.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-cultures

## Projection

Seventy-four, in a cardigan and a velveteen blouse, at a kitchen table with a window that looks at nothing for eleven miles. [Diné bizaad](@chbrain/khai-cultures-tongues/nv/position_language_nv.md) is the tongue she [was raised inside and still thinks in](@chbrain/khai-engine-language/process_thinking_mother_tongue.md), and the English she uses at the clinic and the bank she [puts on like a coat](@chbrain/khai-engine-language/process_speaking_worn.md) and takes off at the door. She belongs to [the Diné way](position_culture_dine.md) as the person the family checks a relation against: nobody under fifty in this household can reliably say all four clans of a visitor, and she can.

## Action

She [runs what is left of the sheep](process_running_sheep.md), she gives her four clans to every stranger before she gives her name, and she tells her grandchildren who they may not marry.

## Shadow

She speaks English to the grandchildren without deciding to. She did it first because she was told at school that it would spare them, and she has gone on doing it for fifty years past the point where the reason held, and she will say the language is being lost as though she were only watching.

## Tell

She answers a question in Diné bizaad, waits, and then answers it again in English without being asked.
