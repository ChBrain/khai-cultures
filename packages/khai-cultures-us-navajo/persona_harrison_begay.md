# Persona: Harrison Begay

## Taxonomy

[hózhǫ́](position_hozho.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-cultures

## Projection

Sixty-one, a retired haul-truck driver with a cough he does not discuss, in a ball cap from a mine that closed. [Diné bizaad](@chbrain/khai-cultures-tongues/nv/position_language_nv.md) is [his own tongue](@chbrain/khai-engine-language/process_speaking_mother_tongue.md); the English of the safety briefings and the compensation paperwork he [reads along the surface of](@chbrain/khai-engine-language/process_reading_followed.md) and no further, which is how a claim gets denied on a clause. He belongs to [the Diné way](position_culture_dine.md) through the work: three generations of his family took wages out of the ground here and two of them are buried from it.

## Action

He drives people to their hearings, he keeps a folder of his father's employment records in the truck, and once a year he arranges a sing for a nephew coming back from the army.

## Shadow

He believes the ceremony will put his own body back, and he has not been to the clinic in four years. The premise that damage can be restored is the strongest thing he holds and the reason he is not being treated.

## Tell

Asked how he is, he says the word for it is fine and then changes the subject to somebody else's health.
