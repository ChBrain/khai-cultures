# Place: Tséyi'

## Taxonomy

Parent group: place of [the Diné](play_navajo.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-cultures

## Shown

A canyon that is not visible until you are at the rim, and then drops a thousand feet. Red walls, a flat sandy floor with a wash down the middle, cottonwoods, and dwellings built into the alcoves by people who were here before the Diné were.

## Holds

Farmland on the floor, the best there is in this country: peach orchards, corn, and summer grazing that people still move down to. Families hold plots there by inheritance rather than by deed.

## Offers

It offers shelter that has worked for a thousand years. You can see who is coming from a long way off, and the walls do the rest.

## Withheld

It withholds the last resort it looks like. In 1864 the shelter became a trap: the soldiers did not need to come down, they only needed to stay up top and cut what was growing. A place that feeds you can be starved from above, and this one was.
