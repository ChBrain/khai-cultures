# Piece: the rug

## Taxonomy

Parent group: piece of [the Diné](play_navajo.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-cultures

## Place

On a floor in Phoenix or Santa Fe, on a wall in a museum, at a roadside stand under a tarp, and occasionally still in the house it was made in.

## Load Bearing

It is the one thing this culture makes that the outside world has always been willing to pay real money for, and that fact has carried households through every decade in which nothing else did. Remove it and the hundred years between the treaty and the wage economy has no visible means of support.

## Apparent

Wool, geometric, hard-edged, usually in the undyed blacks, greys and whites of the sheep themselves with one plant or aniline red. It lies flat and it does not wear out.

## Yearbook

It began as clothing and blankets for the people who made it, and became a rug because that is what buyers wanted on a floor. It was collected, catalogued and named by region by people who were not Diné. It has been faked in factories overseas and sold under the name. And it is still made the slow way by people who could not replace the income any other way, which is the part of its history that has not changed once.
