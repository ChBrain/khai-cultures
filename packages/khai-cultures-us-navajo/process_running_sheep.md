# Process: running sheep

## Taxonomy

Parent group: process of [the Diné](play_navajo.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-cultures

## Initiated by

Somebody opens the corral at first light, usually the person in the household with the least standing and the most time, which for a century meant a child.

## Direction

Out and back, every day, over ground that carries a few animals per section. The herd is moved rather than fenced, the route changes with the season and the rain, and where it goes is a judgement made fresh each morning by whoever is walking. The animals are counted out and counted in. A herd is not a crop that waits: it must be gone with.

## Lever

It converts land nobody could farm into wool, meat and standing. It also converts a child's day into an education, because the person walking learns the country, the weather and the plants in the only way anybody ever has.

## Echo

The wealth it made was legible to the people who later decided there was too much of it, and it was counted and cut by men with clipboards. What is left runs at a fraction of the old numbers and is kept by people who mostly lose money on it, for reasons they will explain patiently if asked.
