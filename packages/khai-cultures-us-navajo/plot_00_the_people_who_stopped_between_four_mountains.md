# Plot: The people who stopped between four mountains

## Taxonomy

Parent group: plot of [the Diné](play_navajo.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-cultures

## Cue

A people speaking a northern language, a long way south of everyone who speaks anything like it, on high desert with a mountain standing at each of the four directions.

## Action

They stopped and did not move again. The tongue they carried has its nearest relatives two and three thousand miles north, in Alaska and northwestern Canada, which is the one fact about this people that no account disputes. What they did after stopping was take things up: farming from the Pueblo towns already here, and later sheep and horses and silver from people who arrived after them, and none of it made them less themselves. [The four mountains](place_dinetah.md) were set as the corners of where a person may properly live, and they are not a border anyone patrols. They are the edge past which the world stops being arranged.

## Stage

- `place`: [Dinétah](place_dinetah.md), the ground the four mountains fix
- `position`: [k'é](position_ke.md), the arithmetic that makes strangers into relatives

## Tension

There are two accounts of how this people got here and they do not agree. The linguists trace a migration down the spine of the continent; the Diné hold an emergence, upward through worlds into this one, at a place that can be pointed to. The nation lives by the second and is perfectly aware of the first, and the friction is not resolved by anybody here. A culture that takes up everything it is offered and stays exactly what it was is difficult to explain from outside, and this is where the difficulty starts.
