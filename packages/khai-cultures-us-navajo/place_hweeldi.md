# Place: Hwééldi

## Taxonomy

Parent group: place of [the Diné](play_navajo.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-cultures

## Shown

Flat ground on the Pecos in eastern New Mexico, four hundred miles from anywhere this nation had been, with a fort on it and no wood and bad water.

## Holds

It held between eight and nine thousand people at once, and it held the Mescalero at the same time, who were not kin and not consulted. It held a plan: that a people moved far enough from their land would become farmers, Christians and English speakers within a few years.

## Offers

It offers the clearest proof this culture has that the plan does not work. Four years, crops that failed, disease, and a bill so large that the government preferred to send the survivors home.

## Withheld

It withheld the return for four years, and it withheld it permanently from the quarter who did not survive to take it. The name is not a place name in this language, it is the word for the event, and people use it as a date.
