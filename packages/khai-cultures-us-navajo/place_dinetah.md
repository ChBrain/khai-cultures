# Place: Dinétah

## Taxonomy

Parent group: place of [the Diné](play_navajo.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-cultures

## Shown

High desert that runs to the horizon in every direction and is not empty in any of them. Four mountains stand at the corners, one to each direction, and they are visible from a great deal of the ground between, which means a person out here can nearly always tell where they are without asking.

## Holds

Grazing that is thin and enormous. Coal under the northern edge and uranium under a good deal of the rest. Water that is present as an idea, in the river along the border, and absent as a tap in something like a third of the houses.

## Offers

It offers orientation as a free good. A direction here is not a compass bearing, it is a mountain, and a child learns the four before learning to read.

## Withheld

It withholds an easy living. The rain is unreliable, the distances are long, the jobs are in Phoenix and Albuquerque and Gallup, and staying here costs a person the career they could have had somewhere else. Almost everyone who stays has decided to.
