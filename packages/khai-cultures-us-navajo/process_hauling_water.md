# Process: hauling water

## Taxonomy

Parent group: process of [the Diné](play_navajo.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-cultures

## Initiated by

The barrel in the truck bed is light when you tap it, so today is a water day whatever else was planned.

## Direction

To the windmill, the chapter house or the filling point, which may be twenty or forty miles, and back with as much as the vehicle will carry. Then the barrel is pumped or siphoned into the house tank and the water is spent in a strict order: drinking, cooking, washing people, washing dishes, the animals, last of all the floor.

## Lever

It is the only way the house runs. Nothing about it is symbolic and nobody doing it finds it picturesque.

## Echo

It costs a day and a tank of fuel every week or two, in a country where the river that bounds this nation is the one everybody else has already drawn from. The people who do it can tell you exactly how many gallons a family of five uses, because they have carried all of them.
