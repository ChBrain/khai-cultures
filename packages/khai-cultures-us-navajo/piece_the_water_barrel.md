# Piece: the water barrel

## Taxonomy

Parent group: piece of [the Diné](play_navajo.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-cultures

## Place

In the bed of the pickup, strapped against the cab, and at rest beside the house door.

## Load Bearing

Without it the house is not habitable. It is the difference between a home and a place you visit, on ground where the pipe never came, and every plan a household makes is shaped around when it next needs filling.

## Apparent

A blue plastic drum, food grade, sun-faded on the side that faces out, with a screw cap and a length of hose.

## Yearbook

It is the newest thing in this culture and the one that dates it most precisely: a nation with a supreme court, a college and a coal-fired power station that exported electricity to Phoenix, in which a third of houses keep a barrel by the door. In 2020 it became the reason the pandemic ran through here faster than almost anywhere in the country, because the instruction everyone else was given was to wash their hands.
