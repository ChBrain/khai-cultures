# Position: the Diné way

## Taxonomy

Parent group: positions. It stands inside [the American way](@chbrain/khai-cultures-usa/position_culture_american_way.md), because the country grew around this land and the people on it did not move off it, except once and under escort, and they came back.

## Owner

- Project: khai-cultures

## Has

It has the largest reservation in the country, a government with three branches, a college, a supreme court and a police force, and it has that on land where a third of homes still haul their water. It has the most widely spoken Indigenous language north of Mexico, and a fluency curve that bends the wrong way in every measured decade. It has four mountains that fix where the world is, and most of its young people living outside them.

## Orders

It orders that you say who you are by saying who you belong to. A person introduces themselves by four clans before they give a name, and the order of them is not decoration: it places you against everyone in the room and tells both of you what you owe each other before a word of business is spoken.

## Loses

It loses its children to the cities and its language to the schools, and the second loss was done on purpose by people who wrote down that they were doing it. It loses water, which the highest court in the country has now said in writing it is owed no help to get.

## Drives

It drives a long patience with paper. This nation has been promised, moved, counted, reduced, surveyed and litigated for a hundred and sixty years, and it has learned to read a document the way a rancher reads weather. Nobody here is surprised by a bad one and nobody stops filing.
