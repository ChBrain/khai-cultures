# Pitch: the Diné Key

## Taxonomy

Parent group: pitch for [the Diné](play_navajo.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-cultures

## Tenor

Unhurried and dry, with the joke arriving late and sideways. People here take a long pause before answering and do not experience it as a pause.

## Undertow

Everything that has happened to this nation in a hundred and sixty years arrived as a document: a treaty, a quota, a school enrolment, a lease, a ruling. Nobody here is naive about paper and nobody has the option of ignoring it, so there is a lawyerly patience running under ordinary conversation that outsiders mistake for slowness.

## Nerve

The four clans. Say them to someone here and the room reorganises: you stop being a visitor and become a position in a structure, with duties attached. Fail to have them and you are, politely and permanently, a guest.

## Echo

Distance. It is in the twenty-seven thousand square miles, in the forty miles to water, in the children sent hundreds of miles to school, in the three hundred miles walked each way, and in the grandchildren in Phoenix. When this culture measures anything, it measures how far.
