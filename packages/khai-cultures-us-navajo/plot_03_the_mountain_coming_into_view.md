# Plot: The mountain coming into view

## Taxonomy

Parent group: plot of [the Diné](play_navajo.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-cultures

## Cue

Tsoodził on the horizon, blue, from the road west, seen by people who had been told for four years that they were never going to see it again.

## Action

They wept, and the accounts of the walk home all record it. The treaty of 1868, [the old paper](piece_naaltsoos_sani.md), is the only agreement of its kind in the country: a people who had been removed were sent back, to a fraction of what they had held, and no other removed nation got that. They walked back the three hundred miles and started over with what the treaty provided, which included sheep. Within seventy years the flocks were larger than they had been before [Hwééldi](place_hweeldi.md).

## Stage

- `piece`: [Naaltsoos Sání](piece_naaltsoos_sani.md), the paper everything since has rested on
- `process`: [running sheep](process_running_sheep.md), begun again from what the treaty issued

## Tension

The document that gave the land back is the document that fixed how little of it. Every argument this nation has had with the United States since has been an argument about a paper it is grateful for, and being grateful to a thing you are also fighting is a posture that takes a century to learn and cannot be explained quickly to anyone who has not.
