# Position: hózhǫ́

## Taxonomy

Parent group: positions

## Owner

- Project: khai-cultures

## Has

It has a word that the translations all get slightly wrong. Beauty, harmony, order, health, the right relation of a thing to everything around it: it is all of those at once and it is a condition of the world rather than a feeling about it.

## Orders

It orders that what has gone out of order is put back, not endured and not explained. A person who is sick, or who has come home from a war, or who has been somewhere they should not have been, is not counselled; a ceremony is held and the condition is restored. The work is to walk back into it, and the phrase for the end of the ceremony says exactly that.

## Loses

It loses to cost and calendar. A sing takes days and a practitioner who has served a long apprenticeship, and both of those are scarcer every decade, so the ceremony that would put a thing back is increasingly a thing people cannot take the week off for.

## Drives

It drives a refusal to treat damage as permanent. Something knocked out of order can be restored, which is a different premise from forgiveness and a different premise from justice, and it is why this culture will spend on a ceremony what another would spend on a lawyer.
