# Plot: A tap that has never run

## Taxonomy

Parent group: plot of [the Diné](play_navajo.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-cultures

## Cue

A kitchen tap in a house with a stove, a television and a phone signal, which has never had water in it and is not connected to anything.

## Action

Roughly a third of homes here [haul](process_hauling_water.md) what they use, on a reservation bounded on one side by a river this nation has claimed a share of for a century. In 2020 that arithmetic became fatal: the instruction the whole country was given was to wash its hands, and the infection rate here ran higher than almost anywhere in the United States. In 2023 the Supreme Court read [the old paper](piece_naaltsoos_sani.md) and held that it obliges the United States to build nothing and secure nothing; the promise of a permanent homeland does not imply the water to live on it. Meanwhile the coal station that sent power to Phoenix has shut, the jobs with it, and [Delvina Yellowhair](persona_delvina_yellowhair.md) teaches eleven children a language she is learning at the same speed they are.

## Stage

- `piece`: [the water barrel](piece_the_water_barrel.md), the newest thing in this culture
- `process`: [hauling water](process_hauling_water.md), a day a week, every week
- `persona`: [Delvina Yellowhair](persona_delvina_yellowhair.md), who came back

## Tension

This is the largest Indigenous nation in the country, with its own courts, college, police and press, and it is negotiating for tap water. Both of those are true at once and neither cancels the other. The question the decade actually poses is not whether the nation survives, which it plainly does, but whether the thing being handed down is the language or only the membership, and the people teaching in the chapter house on a two-year grant are the ones who will find out.
