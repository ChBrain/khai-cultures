The 1933 to 1945 plot is staged from the public historical record and names no
individual perpetrator beyond the head of the regime; the term Zivilisationsbruch
is Dan Diner's and is standard in German historiography. The Stolpersteine and
the Erinnern process are linked from that plot's Tension as what the country did
later, not as anything present in the scene.

The Rettungsgasse named in [die Autobahn](place_die_autobahn.md) is a
German rule (StVO) but not a German invention: Austria, Switzerland and Czechia
require the same corridor. The file stages the moral weight the practice carries
here, and claims no origin.

The two defining personas, [Elke](persona_elke.md) and [Jonas](persona_jonas.md),
are archetypes, not historical individuals: composites that carry the
generational tension between the East-shaped older generation and the
reunification-native young.

## Not staged, and one debt that is owed

**The blood-brother position is written two-sided on purpose.** `position_blutsbruderschaft.md`
holds both halves: the same posture that funds indigenous legal aid also sews the
costume, and this culture cannot tell them apart in the moment. A version that
staged only the appropriation would be as false as the century of German writing
that staged only the affection.

**No causal line is drawn from Winnetou to 1933.** Klaus Mann's essay of 1940 is a
polemic, not a finding. What is recorded here is what is documented: that the
regime did not ban the books and read them for its own purposes. Ernst Bloch and
Albert Einstein admired May as well, and May's late work is pacifist and mystical.
A position that could only explain the worst reader would not be a position.

**The colonial debt, paid in part.** Until `plot_06_die_rinderpest.md` this play
ran from the Karl May plot straight to 1933, so it staged the German fantasy of an
elsewhere and not the actual elsewhere. The colonial plot now stands between them,
and the order is deliberate: the invented Apache of 1893 and the Herero genocide of
1904 were read and done by the same country in the same decade.

Its Cue is the rinderpest of 1897 and not a decision in Berlin, because that is
what moved first and because historians have mostly overlooked it. The risk is
that a plague reads as the cause, so the plot's Tension says in terms that the
plague explains the land sales and explains nothing about the order to shoot.

**One causal line is drawn here, and it is not the one refused above.** No line runs
from Winnetou to 1933; a book was read by a reader, and that is all. From the
colony one does run, and it is documented rather than rhetorical: the word
Konzentrationslager was official there, the bans on marriage between whites and
Africans were issued there, and Eugen Fischer, who measured people in Rehoboth in
1908, headed the Kaiser Wilhelm Institute for Anthropology from 1927 and was rector
of the Berlin university in 1933. The skulls from the camps went to German
anthropological collections; this file does not claim Fischer studied those
particular ones, because the sources checked here do not establish it.

**Still owed: the First World War.** The line now runs from 1904 to 1933 with the
war and Weimar absent. That is a smaller gap than the one it replaces and it is a
gap, and it stays written down here until a plot fills it.

**The Apache are not staged in this house.** `piece_winnetou.md` stages a German
invention of an Apache while the actual Apache peoples hold, at present, one
subordinate clause in `us_arizona/plot_99` about a copper lawsuit. That is the
fault `order_a_culture_without_a_map.md` names at `persona_yazhi.md`, one turn
worse, and it is stated here rather than left for a reader to notice. The piece is
about exactly that displacement, so it carries the admission rather than waiting
for the cultures that would fix it.
