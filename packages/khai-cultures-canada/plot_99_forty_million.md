# Plot: Forty Million

## Taxonomy

[play_canada](play_canada.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-cultures

## Cue

In June 2023 a clock on a government website ticks past forty million. [Jun](persona_jun.md) sees it on his phone in a queue at the [drive-through](process_tim_hortons.md) and thinks nothing much of it, except that his own family is somewhere in that number twice over: once when they arrived, and once again for the cousins who came last year.

## Action

Canada grows faster in these years than any other country in the G7, and almost all of it is arrival: over a million people added in 2023 alone, forty-one million within a year of forty. A country whose whole answer to the question of who it is has always been "whoever comes and keeps what they brought" gets the largest possible confirmation of itself.

Then it stops. In late 2024 the government cuts the targets sharply and plans for the population to shrink for the first time in modern memory. Nothing had changed about the mosaic. What had changed was that there was nowhere to live. A generation cannot buy a house in the city it grew up in, and the argument that arrives is not about culture at all, it is about arithmetic.

And in the same decade the two things that stood in for "us" both turned out to be something other than they looked. [Hockey](process_hockey.md) - the pond, the backyard rink, the parents hollering from the boards - had a national federation that was found in 2022 to have quietly settled an assault claim from a fund built partly out of minor-hockey registration fees. The sponsors left, the board went, and the game had to be handed back to the people who play it. And [the Timmies run](process_tim_hortons.md), named for a hockey player and running in every town in the country, has been owned since 2014 by a multinational headquartered in another country. The rink and the double-double, and one of them needed rebuilding while the other was already somebody else's asset.

In [le Quebec](place_quebec.md), [Therese](persona_therese.md) watches her province legislate in 2022 to hold the French she never once had to leave, in the same years the country breaks its own record for newcomers, most of whom will arrive in English.

And under all of it, the reckoning went on: in 2021 the ground at the schools was searched and gave up what the survivors had said all along, the country kept a first National Day for Truth and Reconciliation, and in 2022 an apology was made on Canadian soil that had been asked for over decades.

## Stage

- [Jun](persona_jun.md): in the number twice, once as the family that came and once as the cousins who followed.
- [Therese](persona_therese.md): holding a French she never had to leave, in the decade of the country's largest arrival.
- [the Tim Hortons run](process_tim_hortons.md): the most Canadian errand there is, owned in another country since 2014.
- [hockey](process_hockey.md): the game handed back to the people who play it after its own federation failed them.
- [the mosaic](position_the_mosaic.md): confirmed at record scale, and then, for the first time, narrowed.
- [le Quebec](place_quebec.md): legislating to keep its tongue while the country fills up in the other one.
- [Canadian culture](position_culture_canadian_culture.md): an identity made of admitting people, meeting the question of how many.

## Tension

A country built by joining rather than breaking has always answered _who are we_ procedurally - by letting more people in and letting them keep what they brought. This decade it let in more people than ever and then, for the first time, said not so many. The question the mosaic never had to answer was how many, and a country whose identity is its openness has no vocabulary for a limit - so the limit arrives as arithmetic, in housing starts and target numbers, rather than as anything anyone would call a decision about who belongs.

The rhyme with [the beginning](plot_00_kanata.md) is not comfortable. This country started as a working arrangement across difference, and that arrangement held for exactly as long as both sides needed it. The mosaic has never yet been tested by scarcity. The decade does not decide whether it scales. It only asks the question a country defined by taking people in has never had to ask itself: what it is on the day it stops.
