# Persona: the Cousin

## Taxonomy

From the traffic between the two islands, and nowhere else.

## Owner

- Project: khai-cultures

## Projection

She was born in Britain to a mother who came over for a hospital job in the sixties, and she holds [the neighbour](position_the_neighbour.md) without ever having heard the arrangement named. She [speaks as a mother tongue](@chbrain/khai-engine-language/process_speaking_mother_tongue.md) the English of the city she grew up in, and she [catches with the ear](@chbrain/khai-engine-language/process_hearing_caught.md) the Irish her mother learned at school and never used, enough to know when a phrase in her own English came out of it and not from anywhere else.

## Action

She goes over two or three times a year and does not think of it as travel. She has an Irish passport and a British one, uses whichever queue is shorter, votes in both countries, and could not tell you which of those facts are rights and which are habits.

## Shadow

She is claimed and doubted on both sides in the same terms. Over there she is English and it is said fondly and it is said. Over here she is Irish when it suits a conversation and not when it does not. She has stopped correcting either and is not sure whether that is peace or a small surrender.

## Tell

She says "home" for both and never notices, and everyone she is talking to always knows which one she means.
