# Position: Ulster-Scotch

## Taxonomy

Parent group: positions

## Owner

- Project: khai-cultures

## Has

It haes a staunin in law that nae ither Scots haes. It's named in an agreement
atween twa states, it's cairried unner the European chairter, it haes its ain
fundit agency, an it gets owerset in a legislatur an set up on signs. The tongue
haes a budget afore it haes a classroom, an that's a road intae the warld that
Scotland's Scots niver got.

It haes a screivin practice o its ain, biggit on purpose. The revival screivers
o the last twa-three score year didnae jyne in wi Lallans; they pickit spellins
that merk the tongue aff frae Scotland raither nor intae it, sae the same wurd
can be screivit twa weys, an whit wey ye pick says whaur ye stand.

An it haes twa continuums tae slide on, no ane. On the tae side is Ulster
English, that it shades intae wurd by wurd like ony Scots daes; on the tither is
the Gaelic o Ulster, that it haes leeved aside for fower hunner year an taen
soonds an sayins frae. A bodie in Ulster is aye somewhaur on baith at ance, an
maun redd up whit they're daein ilka time they open their mou.

## Orders

It bids the speaker declare afore they say ocht. In this bit the tongue is read
as a badge or iver it's read as speech, sae naebody can lift a wurd o it wioot
the hearer takkin it for a statement aboot whit side they cam aff. There's nae
plain uise o it: ye cannae juist say a thing, ye're aye sayin a thing an sayin
yersel forby.

It bids the screiver choose against Scotland ilka time. Whit's screivit ae wey
in Ayr maun be sattelt again in Antrim, an the sattlin isnae a maitter o soond
but o whilk tradeetion the page is claimin.

An it bids the bodie thole bein luikit at. A tongue that's argued ower in a
chamber gets ilka feature o it fetched oot an hauden up, sae the speaker maun
ken hoo their ain mou soonds tae fowk that dinnae wish it weel.

## Loses

It tines the guid o the doot. Its case is argued in a legislatur insteid o bein
sattelt in a schule, sae ilka feature comes tae the ear as either a richt or a
lauch, an near niver as juist a fact aboot a tongue. Wurds that wad be ordinar
onie ither place canna be said ordinar here.

It tines the bairns while it wins the siller. Recognition cam doun frae the tap
an transmission gaes on frae the mou, an the twa hae come apairt: there's
fundin, signage an offices whaur there's nae hoose that raises weans in it. A
tongue can hae a budget an nae speakers, an that's whaur this ane sits.

An it tines the guid o its ain kin. Scotland's Scots is a muckle warm hoose it
could staun in, but staunin in it wad gie awa the separate name that wan it the
staunin in the first place, sae it maun bide ootby its ain faimily tae haud whit
it haes.

## Drives

It lairns the bodie that hauds it that a tongue can be an entitlement, an that
an entitlement isnae the same thing as a speaker. Naebody that grew up in this
kens recognition an survival for the ae thing, for they hae seen the ane come
wioot the tither.

It lairns them tae hear their ain speech frae the ootside, because it's aye bein
listened tae for whit it means raither nor whit it says.

An it lairns them that a mou can be pit tae uise by fowk that dinnae speak wi
it. Whan a tongue is a coonter in a bargain, them that speak it least can get
the maist guid o it, an them that speak it best can end up quate.
