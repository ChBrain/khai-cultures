# Position: American English

## Taxonomy

Parent group: positions

## Owner

- Project: khai-cultures

## Has

American English has the larger of the two written forms of the language and the unmarked one, and what makes it a variety rather than a default is a spelling system that was regularised on purpose: color and honor and labor without the u, center and theater and meter with the vowels in the order the mouth uses them, traveled and canceled with one l, defense and license with the s, catalog and dialog without the tail. It has gotten alive as a distinction the other form has lost - he has got a car is possession and he has gotten a car is acquisition, two different sentences here and one there - and it turns nouns into verbs on the spot, to google, to text, to parent, to gift, a productivity for coining new verbs that most languages restrict far more tightly. In the mouth it pronounces the r in every position, car, hard, teacher, where the form that gave it its name long ago dropped that same sound at the end of a syllable.

## Orders

It forces one spelling where the other form keeps two: a license and to license, a practice and to practice, so a distinction the other written form makes with a c and an s must be carried here by the sentence around the word. It forces the article onto the institution, in the hospital, at the university, went to the hospital, so the building is named even when the purpose is meant. It forces a collective to take a singular verb, the committee is divided, the band is on tour, so a body of people is one thing grammatically whatever it is doing. And, like all English, it forces strict word order in place of case endings, and collapses every degree of address into the one flat you, so distance and closeness must be built by name and title rather than by the pronoun itself.

## Loses

It loses the noun and the verb as separate spellings, a licence to drive against to license a driver, a practice against to practise, a pair the other written form still keeps and this one folded together; the distinction survives in the reading and never on the page. It loses the bare institution and the meaning that lived in the missing article, where in hospital is a condition and in the hospital is an address, and this form has only the address. It loses regional accent under the pressure of General American, the flattened, rhotic standard the variety holds up as its own unmarked form, treating the non-rhotic drawl of the old South or the dropped r of the Northeast as a deviation rather than a variant just as old. And a noun turned into a verb overnight, to adult, to input, rarely arrives with the settled, agreed usage an older verb carries, so the new coinage can grate on an ear that has not yet adopted it, even while it fills a real gap in the sentence.

## Drives

It trains whoever writes it to expect the spelling to follow the sound and not the history, so the u in colour and the re in centre read as ornament rather than as the older form they are, and the reader who meets them abroad reads a costume. It trains the mind to hold getting and having apart, because gotten and got are two words here and one word there, and the speaker who has both will feel the gap in the form that lost one. It trains whoever speaks it to reach first for the r wherever the spelling puts one, so its absence in another English marks that speaker as a visitor to this tongue rather than a native of it. And it trains the mind to treat any noun as a verb waiting to happen, gifting a friend, parenting a child, incentivizing a team, so the speaker never quite stops listening for the next noun the language can put to work as an action.
