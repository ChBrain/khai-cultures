# Position: de Gullah tongue

## Taxonomy

Parent group: positions

## Owner

- Project: khai-cultures

## Has

De Gullah tongue git mos e wod fom Englishmun mout, bot e hole pon plenty wa cross de wata whole an ain neba change: nyam fa eat, goober fa de groun pea, benne fa de seed wa mek de wafer, cooter fa de turtle, buckra fa de white man, da fa de ooman wa raise annuda ooman chile. E soffen de hard place way English keep um hard, cyar an gyaad an gyirl, an e ain soun de r wa English write, so fadduh an watuh stan de way dey come out de mout.

## Orders

E ain hang no tail pon de verb fa mek time; e set a wod fo um an lef de verb stan still. E da eat fa wa da happen now, e bin eat fa wa done pass, e done eat fa wa finish, e gwine eat fa wa ain come yet. E ain need no is an no are needa: e happy, de wata col, an nottin missin out de sentence. One wod, e, do fa man an ooman an ting alltwo, an oona do fa mo den one, wa come fom Igbo unu an ain fom no English mout. Dem stan behin de noun fa mek um plenty, de chillun dem, an fa tie one verb ta de nex, e come fa see we.

## Loses

Wa e da lose fus is de groun. De wata roun de Sea Islan been de ting wa keep de tongue whole, an now de bridge cross um an de tax rise pon de heir property an de people wa taak um scatta fom de lan dey own people bury een. E da lose een de school, way chillun git laff at an larn fa lay um down an pick up wa dem call proper. An e da lean toward de English wa stan right nex ta um, so de ole wod slip out fus an de gramma hole on de longes: a tongue ain dead all at one time, e thin out.

## Drives

E mek de one wa taak um hab two tongue een one mout an know, widout nobody larn um, wish one fa wish place. E mek a yez fa wa git carry stead o wa git write down: de tale, de shout een de praise house, de song de ole people sing an ain know wa de wod mean no mo. An e mek a way fa name, de basket name wa de fambly gib you an ain neba go pon no paper, side de name de school know you by.
