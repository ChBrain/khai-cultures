# Instructions: Tongues

How a Playwright wires a tongue into a play. You wire by linking the play's own
content to this package's files; the package files stay untouched. The language
engine already owns the grip: a persona's width on each channel, and the gap
meaning crosses. This covers only the other half, which tongue is being gripped.
Authoring guidance, not runtime content, and it does not go on tour.

## Human

- The human decides whether a persona holds the standard or the variety beneath
  it. That is a claim about the person and not about the language, and it is the
  one choice here that cannot be computed.

## Agent

- Beside every grip, link the tongue that grip is on. Where a variety exists for
  what the persona actually speaks, link the variety and not the standard above
  it: a speaker whose mother tongue is the dialect and whose office language is
  the standard carries two links, at two widths, and the difference between them
  is the whole point.

## Collaboration

- What a tongue gives, forces, cannot say and shapes is the tongue's; the grip
  per channel is the language engine's; what only this person does with it is
  the persona's. A fact belongs to exactly one of the three, and the question
  that places it is: is this true of everyone who holds this tongue, or of
  everyone at this width, or only of them?

## Knowledge

- Persona, language and tongue are three forces holding one detail between them,
  and depth added at any of the three reaches the others without being written
  twice. A tongue file deepens every persona that links it; a persona line
  deepens one. So detail is cheapest at the tongue and dearest at the persona,
  and a Projection that describes how a tongue sounds has put the expensive
  vertex to work doing the cheap one's job. When there is no tongue file to
  carry it, the answer is to write the tongue, not to describe it in the person.

## System

- Do link a tongue beside every grip, and link the variety where one exists.
- Do put in the Projection only what is true of this person and not of everyone
  holding the tongue: the dialect words that surface under stress, the register
  they cannot reach, the one word they will not translate.
- Do not write how the tongue sounds or works into the Projection. Those four
  chapters are the tongue's, and written twice they will disagree.
- Do not claim a tongue marked `spoken: false` as anyone's mother tongue; it is
  a written form and nobody grows up in it.
- Do not edit this package's files; wire only from the play's side.
