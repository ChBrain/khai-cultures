# Tongues: References

Each variety carries the provenance of its own prose. What is recorded here is
what travels with the file when it leaves the culture that wrote it.

| Variety                    | Note                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| -------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| `position_language_rif.md` | Tarifit, written in Tarifit in Latin script, the convention of Riffian writing in Morocco and the diaspora; Tifinagh, official in Morocco since 2003, is not used in Melilla. Registered as `rif` in the house's `khai.languages` for assisted verification. **The prose is flagged for native review**: no Riffian speaker has read it. It ships under the flag Faroese shipped under, because the alternative was leaving a tongue two personas speak with no position to hold it. Read against the mnemonic when it moved here: two claims about Melilla, its households and its schools, were cut, because a position says what a tongue does to whoever holds it and not what a city does about it. The Rif stays, as the speech community that holds the tongue. |
