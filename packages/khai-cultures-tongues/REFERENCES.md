# Tongues: References

Each variety carries the provenance of its own prose. What is recorded here is
what travels with the file when it leaves the culture that wrote it: who wrote
it, whether a speaker of the tongue has read it, and what the reading against the
position mnemonic found when it moved.

| `position_language_de_de_bb.md` | Written out after the walk found it thin. It now says what it stands on, the East Low German the Mark spoke before it went High German, and what it kept: the j for initial g that grows with nearness to the city, and the landscape words Fließ, Luch and Kietz, the last Slavic like the -ow and -itz place names. Its Loses is the honest one: the variety loses its own contour to Berlinisch, because the features it carries are heard as Berlin outside the Mark. The sorted drift queue reports it FOREIGN for naming Berlin, and the reading is that it does not: every mention is das Berliner Hochdeutsch, der Berliner Klang, das Berlinische, a neighbouring tongue and not a city's institutions. Naming another variety is what a variety is for, the same allowance Ceutan Spanish gets for the speech of the Campo de Gibraltar. Left standing deliberately; the queue is a review queue, not a verdict. Came here from `cultures/de_brandenburg`, which wrote it. |
| `position_language_de_de_be.md` | Written out after the walk found it thin. | `position_language_nds.md`      | Read against the mnemonic on the way through: nothing was cut, and it is the cleanest position in the package. Written in Low German itself.
