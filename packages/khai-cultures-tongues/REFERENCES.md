# Tongues: References

Each variety carries the provenance of its own prose. What is recorded here is
what travels with the file when it leaves the culture that wrote it: who wrote
it, whether a speaker of the tongue has read it, and what the reading against the
position mnemonic found when it moved.

| `bar/position_language_bar.md`     | Came here as `position_bayerisch.md`: an ordinary position titled "The Bavarian Language Variety", declared das Bairische and tagged `language: de`. A language position in substance wearing another kind of name, which is why the tongues walk never saw it. Its four chapters described the tongue sociologically - identity, Gemuetlichkeit, the Wirtshaus, the generations - and said almost nothing about what it does to whoever holds it. Rewritten to the mnemonic and written in Bavarian, which is the house rule for a variety and also what khai needs: the shibboleth mechanic is blocked on real `bar` prose to calibrate against, and invented sentences would calibrate it against nothing. It now carries what is Bavarian rather than Upper German in general: the -l almost any thing can take, Stoa for Stein, the article before a personal name, and es and enk, the second-person plural no other German dialect kept, down from the old dual. Its sharpest chapter is Loses - with no preterite it cannot put a story at a distance, so in Bavarian the past is always still near. Exempt in `khai.languages` because franc has no `bar` model at all: genuinely undetectable, which is what that list is for. Came here from `cultures/de_bavaria`, which wrote it. |

| `de/position_language_de_de_bb.md` | Written out after the walk found it thin.
