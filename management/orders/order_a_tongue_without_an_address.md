# Order: a Tongue Without an Address

**A persona carries the depth. A tongue is what is wired. When a persona carries
depth at a tongue the house does not hold, the house has named something it
cannot point at.**

Nauru ships this line:

> Nauruan is the miner's mother tongue: they [speak](process_speaking_mother_tongue.md)…

Nauruan is named, in plain prose, as a mother tongue. The depth is wired at three
channels. There is no `position_language_na.md` anywhere in the house, and the
only language position Nauru ships is `position_language_en_nr.md`, which is
English as it is spoken there. The miner's own tongue has no address.

## Dead is not the obstacle

The instinct is to call this a problem of dead tongues and to invent a production
type for them. It is not, and the house already disproves it: `la`, `cu` and
`lzh` are held and wired like any other, and not one of them has a native
speaker.

Nor does a tongue carry a flag saying so. Latin's state is **read off the
personas**, because the persona is where depth lives. Counted across the house:

| channel                    | wirings |
| -------------------------- | ------- |
| reading_followed           | 27      |
| speaking_carried           | 17      |
| writing_drafted            | 7       |
| hearing_caught             | 7       |
| reading_deciphered         | 5       |
| speaking_worn              | 4       |
| speaking_borrowed          | 3       |
| hearing_followed           | 3       |
| writing_mother_tongue      | 1       |
| thinking_mother_tongue     | 1       |
| **speaking_mother_tongue** | **0**   |

Seventy-five wirings and not one native speaker. **That distribution is the
deadness**, and nobody declared it. `cu` and `lzh` read the same way.

So a tongue with a surviving written form is wireable, whatever became of its
speakers. The house is text. It is not making sounds. Punic has a corpus of
thousands of inscriptions and a script read with confidence; what it lacks here
is not life, it is a directory.

## Three tiers, and only the first is a wall

What separates the cases is not how dead the tongue is. It is whether the house
can see the hole.

| tier | shape                                                        | found by     | today |
| ---- | ------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------ | ----- |
| 1    | a persona carries depth and its file wires no tongue at all  | **a rule**   | 2     |
| 2    | a persona wires some tongues and names another in bare prose | **reading**  | Punic |
| 3    | the house never names the tongue at all                      | **a person** | Sámi  |

**Tier 1 is deterministic** and is the only one a ratchet can hold. Both of
today's two are the Nauru personas above.

**Tier 2 cannot be ruled.** `persona_septimius.md` wires Latin to the tongues
house and Arabic to its own culture, then carries Punic at
`process_speaking_carried.md` with the tongue named only in Arabic prose. A
file-level rule passes it, because the file does wire tongues. Catching it means
knowing that الفينيقية البونية is the name of a language, which is reading. It
was found the same way `these_islands` was found in
`order_an_address_resolves.md`: by a person, and reading is not a wall.

**Tier 3 cannot be found at all.** Sápmi appears nowhere in this house - not as a
culture, not as a group, not as a tongue, and not in the prose of Sweden, Norway
or Finland, each of which ships a culture over ground the Sámi live on. A search
of every package returns one mention of Lapland, in Spain's REFERENCES, comparing
population density. **The house can only see a hole something already points at.**
Tier 3 is a roster, kept by hand, the way `order_the_sunken.md` keeps its
targets.

## What is not a fault

Fifty-seven bare language codes are referenced outside the tongues house and are
not held by it: `be`, `so`, `crs`, `ka`, `ur`, `he`, `tpi` and fifty more. **Every
one resolves locally**, in the culture's own directory, which is the convention:
a culture ships the position for the language as spoken there, and the tongues
house holds the tongue. `position_language_he.md` lives in `israel`. Counting
these would report fifty-seven faults where there are none, and this is recorded
here so the count is not raised a second time.
