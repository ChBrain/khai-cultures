# Ceuta: References

The provenance behind this culture: the Hofstede source data that tunes the pitch (kept here, never in the prose) and the public-domain historical sources behind the plots, personas, places, and pieces.

## Hofstede source data

The pitch [pitch_ceuta.md](pitch_ceuta.md) is written from Spain's profile inflected by Ceuta's garrison and administrative economy, its border dependence, and its dense multi-community coexistence, naming no dimension and carrying no number.

| Dimension                     | Ceuta base score (estimated / Spain comparison) |
| ----------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------- |
| Power Distance                | 60                                              |
| Individualism                 | 45                                              |
| Motivation toward Achievement | 42                                              |
| Uncertainty Avoidance         | 88                                              |
| Long Term Orientation         | 48                                              |
| Indulgence                    | 46                                              |

Source: Geert Hofstede, Hofstede Insights (Spain national base, adjusted for Ceuta's public-sector and military employment, its permit-and-customs administration, and the tight family and community networks of a city of seven square kilometres). The figures are an estimate: Hofstede's instrument was never fielded on the autonomous cities separately.

## Language note

Ceuta owns [el español ceutí](@chbrain/khai-cultures-tongues/position_language_es_es_ce.md), the Spanish as the ceutíes speak it. It no longer lives in this directory: it is held by `@chbrain/khai-cultures-tongues` and cast from here by package specifier, because a tongue belongs to the speech community that speaks it and not to one city. It opened by placing itself on the African shore of the Strait, and where is not one of the four chapters a position answers, so that opening now names the community instead. Personas whose home tongue is the local dariya link the Moroccan Arabic variety in its home culture ([el árabe marroquí](../morocco/position_language_ar_ma.md)), and peninsular administrative Spanish links [el castellano peninsular](../spain/position_language_es_es.md), following the house rule that a variety is owned once and cast by reference.
