# Plot: Modern Ireland

## Taxonomy

[play_ireland](play_ireland.md)

## Owner

- Project: khai-cultures

## Cue

In the late twentieth century Ireland is still poor, Church-bound, and emptying out of its young, a small nation away on the western edge of Europe.

## Action

The Celtic Tiger boom and membership of the European Union make it rich all of a sudden; the tech multinationals make [Dublin](place_dublin.md) their European base; [the Church](position_the_church.md) collapses in under the abuse scandals; the people vote in marriage equality and repeal; and a country that exported its own becomes one that others now come to. [Cian](persona_cian.md) works away in one of the multinationals; [Mary](persona_mary.md), of the parish and the emigration generation, watches her own children come home instead of going; and out west [Bríd](persona_brid.md) raises hers with Irish in the house and English at the door. What holds across the three of them is [the pint](piece_the_pint.md), the same black glass in the city, in the smallest village, and in every Irish bar the diaspora built abroad.

## Stage

- [Dublin](place_dublin.md): the boom city and the European headquarters of the global tech.
- [the Church](position_the_church.md): the old authority falling away fast and far.
- [Cian](persona_cian.md): the young Dubliner inside the boom, at home in English and in the open world.
- [Mary](persona_mary.md): the older woman of the parish, who sees the emigration run backwards in her own lifetime.
- [Bríd](persona_brid.md): the Gaeltacht woman holding the language in the house while the country grows rich in the other one.
- [the pint](piece_the_pint.md): the glass that is the same measure at home and everywhere the diaspora went.

## Tension

Ireland becomes rich, secular, global, and confidently English-speaking, modern and open the way it never was before, but cut loose from the Church and the land that long defined it, and its first official language still, after the whole of it, mostly unspoken.
