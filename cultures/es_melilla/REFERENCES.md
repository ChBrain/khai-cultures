# Melilla: References

The provenance behind this culture: the Hofstede source data that tunes the pitch (kept here, never in the prose) and the public-domain historical sources behind the plots, personas, places, and pieces.

## Hofstede source data

The pitch [pitch_melilla.md](pitch_melilla.md) is written from Spain's profile inflected by Melilla's garrison and administrative economy, its documentary culture of proof and padrón, and the coexistence of its four communities, naming no dimension and carrying no number.

Source: Geert Hofstede, Hofstede Insights (Spain national base, adjusted for Melilla's military and public-sector employment, its permit, padrón and customs administration, and its extended family networks across the border). The figures are an estimate: Hofstede's instrument was never fielded on the autonomous cities separately.

## Language note

Melilla owns two language positions. [El español melillense](@chbrain/khai-cultures-tongues/position_language_es_es_ml.md) is the Spanish as the melillenses speak it, and it followed Tarifit out of this directory into `@chbrain/khai-cultures-tongues`. Two things were cut from its opening on the way: it placed itself on the Rif coast, and where is not one of the four chapters a position answers; and it explained its own closeness to the standard by the city's garrison and civil service, which is a claim about who lives in a place rather than about a tongue. [Tarifit](@chbrain/khai-cultures-tongues/position_language_rif.md) is the Riffian Amazigh tongue that is the home language of a large part of the city, written in Tarifit in Latin script and registered in `package.json` under `khai.languages` as `rif`, which routes it to assisted verification instead of the local detector. It no longer lives in this directory: as of the first slice of the tongues walk it is held by `@chbrain/khai-cultures-tongues` and cast from here by package specifier, because a tongue belongs to the speech community that speaks it and not to one city. **The Tarifit prose is flagged for native review**: no Riffian speaker has read it, and the house ships it under the same flag it used for Faroese rather than leave a tongue its personas speak with no position to hold it. Tarifit has no standard written norm in Melilla, is taught in no school here, and is rendered in Latin script by the convention of Riffian writing in Morocco and the diaspora; Tifinagh, official in Morocco since 2003, is not used in the city.

Peninsular administrative Spanish links [el castellano peninsular](../spain/position_language_es_es.md) and the Moroccan Arabic of cross-border trade links [el árabe marroquí](../morocco/position_language_ar_ma.md), following the house rule that a variety is owned once and cast by reference.
